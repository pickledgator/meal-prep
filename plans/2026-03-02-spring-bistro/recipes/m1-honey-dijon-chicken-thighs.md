# Honey-Dijon Chicken Thighs

_with Roasted Asparagus & Herbed Farro_

_Makes 2 servings (plus 2 extra thighs for Meal 2)_

---

## Prepped Ingredients

> From your Sunday prep:
>
> - ½ batch Herbed Farro — `components/herbed-farro.md`
> - Honey-Dijon Pan Sauce Base (from prep list)
> - Minced shallot (1, from prep)
> - Minced garlic (2 cloves, from prep)
> - Asparagus (½ bunch, trimmed, from prep)

---

## Ingredients

### Chicken

- 4 Bone-In Skin-On Chicken Thighs (~1.5 lb / 680 g total)
- Kosher Salt
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper
- 1 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil

### Pan Sauce

- 1 medium Shallot, minced (from prep)
- 2 cloves Garlic, minced (from prep)
- Honey-Dijon Pan Sauce Base (from prep): 2 Tbsp Dijon Mustard, 1 Tbsp Honey, ⅓ cup Chicken Stock, 1 Tbsp Lemon Juice
- 1 Tbsp Salted Butter
- 3-4 sprigs Fresh Thyme

### Sides

- ½ bunch Asparagus, trimmed (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- ½ batch Herbed Farro, reheated (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp chopped Flat-Leaf Parsley, _for garnish_

---

## Instructions

### Sear & Roast the Chicken

1. Preheat oven to **425°F**. Pat all 4 **chicken thighs** very dry with paper towels. Season generously on both sides with **salt** and **pepper**.

2. Heat **olive oil** in a large oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat. Place the thighs **skin-side down** and cook undisturbed for 5-6 minutes until the skin is deep golden and releases easily from the pan.

3. Flip the thighs. Transfer the skillet to the oven and roast for 18-20 minutes until the internal temperature reaches 165°F.

### Roast the Asparagus

4. While the chicken roasts, toss the **asparagus** with **olive oil**, **salt**, and **pepper** on a sheet pan. Slide into the oven alongside the chicken for the last 10-12 minutes, until tender and lightly charred at the tips.

### Make the Pan Sauce

5. Remove the chicken from the skillet. Set **2 thighs aside** to cool — these are for Meal 2 (store in the fridge once cooled). Rest the remaining 2 thighs on a plate, tented with foil.

6. Place the skillet over medium heat (careful — handle is hot). Add the minced **shallot** to the chicken drippings. Sauté for 1-2 minutes until softened. Add the **garlic** and cook 30 seconds until fragrant.

7. Pour in the **Honey-Dijon Pan Sauce Base** and drop in the **thyme sprigs**. Scrape up any browned bits from the bottom of the pan. Simmer for 2-3 minutes until slightly thickened. Remove from heat, swirl in the **butter** until melted and glossy. Discard thyme sprigs. Taste and adjust seasoning.

### Plate & Serve

8. Spoon a mound of reheated **herbed farro** onto each plate. Lean a chicken thigh against the farro, skin-side up. Arrange the **roasted asparagus** alongside. Spoon the **honey-Dijon pan sauce** generously over the chicken and farro. Finish with a scatter of **parsley**.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Don't move the chicken once it hits the pan skin-side down. The key to shatteringly crispy skin is patience — let it render and brown undisturbed. If it sticks, it's not ready to flip yet.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** Pair with a crisp Sauvignon Blanc or a dry hard cider.

**Save for Meal 2:** Let the 2 reserved chicken thighs cool, then store in an airtight container in the fridge. They'll be shredded for the grain bowl.
