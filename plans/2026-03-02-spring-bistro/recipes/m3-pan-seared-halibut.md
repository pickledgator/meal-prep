# Pan-Seared Halibut

_with Lemon-Caper Brown Butter, Crushed Potatoes & Arugula Salad_

_Makes 2 servings_

---

## Prepped Ingredients

> From your Sunday prep:
>
> - Boiled fingerling potatoes (from prep list)
> - Smoky Paprika Vinaigrette — `components/smoky-paprika-vinaigrette.md`
> - Crispy Shallots — `components/crispy-shallots.md`
> - Sliced radishes (1 portion, from prep)
> - Lemon juice (from prepped juice jar)
> - Chopped parsley (from prep)

---

## Ingredients

### Halibut

- 2 Halibut Fillets (5-6 oz / 142-170 g each)
- Kosher Salt
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper
- 1 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil

### Lemon-Caper Brown Butter

- 3 Tbsp Salted Butter
- 2 Tbsp Capers, drained and patted dry
- 2 Tbsp Lemon Juice (from prepped juice)
- 1 Tbsp finely chopped Flat-Leaf Parsley (from prep)

### Crushed Potatoes

- Boiled Fingerling Potatoes (¾ lb, from prep)
- 1½ Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil (or reserved shallot oil from prep)
- Kosher Salt
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper

### Arugula Salad

- 3 oz (85 g) Arugula (about 3 handfuls)
- 3-4 Radishes, thinly sliced (from prep)
- 1-2 Tbsp Smoky Paprika Vinaigrette (from prep)
- 1-2 Tbsp Crispy Shallots (from prep)

---

## Instructions

### Crush & Crisp the Potatoes

1. Heat **olive oil** (or the reserved shallot-infused oil) in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the boiled **fingerling potatoes** and press each one gently with the back of a fork or a spatula until it just splits open — you want craggy, uneven surfaces. Season with **salt** and **pepper**.

2. Cook undisturbed for 4-5 minutes until the bottoms are golden and crispy. Flip and crisp the other side for another 3-4 minutes. Transfer to a plate and tent with foil.

### Sear the Halibut

3. Pat the **halibut fillets** very dry with paper towels. Season both sides with **salt** and **pepper**.

4. In the same skillet (or a second one), heat **olive oil** over medium-high heat until shimmering. Place the fillets presentation-side down. Sear for 4 minutes without moving until a golden crust forms. Flip gently and cook another 3-4 minutes until the fish is just opaque through the center and flakes easily.

5. Transfer the halibut to a plate.

### Make the Brown Butter

6. Reduce heat to medium. Add the **butter** to the skillet. Let it melt and foam, swirling occasionally. Watch it carefully — it will go from golden to brown quickly (about 2 minutes). The moment it smells nutty and turns amber, add the **capers** (they'll sputter — stand back). Let them sizzle for 30 seconds until they bloom and turn slightly crispy.

7. Remove from heat immediately. Add the **lemon juice** (it will bubble vigorously) and the **parsley**. Swirl to combine. Scrape up any browned bits from the bottom of the pan — that's pure flavor.

### Toss the Salad

8. In a bowl, toss the **arugula** and **sliced radishes** with the **smoky paprika vinaigrette**. Top with **crispy shallots**.

### Plate & Serve

9. Arrange the **crushed potatoes** on one side of each plate. Place a **halibut fillet** next to them, slightly overlapping. Spoon the **lemon-caper brown butter** generously over the fish and let it pool around the potatoes. Mound the **arugula salad** alongside. Finish with a pinch of flaky salt over the fish if you have it.

---

> **Hot Tip:** The brown butter goes from perfect to burnt in seconds. Keep the heat moderate and remove the pan from the burner the instant it smells like toasted hazelnuts. The residual heat will carry it the rest of the way.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** Pair with a glass of Chablis or a dry Vermentino.
