# Shredded Chicken & Charred Asparagus Grain Bowl

_with Smoky Paprika Vinaigrette & Crispy Shallots_

_Makes 2 servings_

---

## Prepped Ingredients

> From your Sunday prep:
>
> - ½ batch Herbed Farro — `components/herbed-farro.md`
> - Smoky Paprika Vinaigrette — `components/smoky-paprika-vinaigrette.md`
> - Crispy Shallots — `components/crispy-shallots.md`
> - Asparagus (½ bunch, trimmed, from prep)

> - Reserved chicken thighs (2, from Meal 1)

---

## Ingredients

### Bowl Base

- 2 Bone-In Skin-On Chicken Thighs (reserved from Meal 1)
- ½ batch Herbed Farro, reheated (from prep)

### Charred Asparagus

- ½ bunch Asparagus, trimmed (from prep)
- 1 tsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- Pinch of Kosher Salt

### Toppings

- 2 oz (57 g) Arugula (about 2 handfuls)
- 3-4 Radishes, thinly sliced (from prep)
- 2-3 Tbsp Smoky Paprika Vinaigrette (from prep)
- 1-2 Tbsp Crispy Shallots (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp chopped Flat-Leaf Parsley, _for garnish_

---

## Instructions

### Char the Asparagus

1. Heat a cast iron skillet or grill pan over high heat until smoking. Toss the **asparagus** with **olive oil** and **salt**. Lay the spears in a single layer and cook for 3-4 minutes, turning once, until blistered and charred in spots but still snappy. Cut into 2-inch pieces.

### Warm the Chicken

2. Shred the reserved **chicken thighs**, discarding skin and bones (or chop the crispy skin into bits as a bonus topping). Warm the shredded chicken gently in the microwave (1-2 minutes, covered) or in a skillet over medium heat with a splash of water.

### Assemble the Bowls

3. Divide the reheated **herbed farro** between two bowls. Top with the warm **shredded chicken**, **charred asparagus** pieces, **arugula**, and **sliced radishes**.

### Plate & Serve

4. Drizzle each bowl with **smoky paprika vinaigrette** (about 1-1½ Tbsp per bowl). Top with a generous pinch of **crispy shallots** and a scatter of **parsley**. Serve immediately while the warm and cool components contrast.

---

> **Hot Tip:** If you saved the crispy chicken skin, crumble it over the top — it adds a salty, savory crunch that takes the bowl up a level.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** Pair with a light Côtes du Rhône or a cold pilsner.
