# Crispy Shallots

_Makes about 3-4 Tbsp_

## Ingredients

- 1 medium Shallot, thinly sliced into rings
- ¼ cup Extra-Virgin Olive Oil (or neutral oil)
- Pinch of Kosher Salt

## Instructions

1. Separate the **shallot** slices into individual rings. Pat dry with a paper towel — moisture causes splattering.

2. Place the shallot rings in a small saucepan or skillet with the **oil**. Start over medium-low heat (cold start — this ensures even cooking). Cook, stirring occasionally, for 8-10 minutes. The shallots will gradually turn golden.

3. Once the shallots are deep golden brown and the bubbling slows, use a slotted spoon to transfer them to a paper towel-lined plate. They will crisp up further as they cool. Season immediately with a pinch of **salt**.

4. Reserve the shallot-infused oil — it's excellent drizzled on the grain bowl or arugula salad.

---

**To Store:** Once fully cooled, store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days. They lose crunch after that. The infused oil can be stored separately in the fridge for up to 1 week.

**Used in:** Meals 2, 3
