# Herbed Farro

_Makes about 3 cups cooked_

## Ingredients

- 1 cup (180 g) Farro
- 2½ cups Water
- ½ tsp Kosher Salt
- Zest of 1 Lemon
- 3 Tbsp finely chopped Flat-Leaf Parsley
- 1 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

## Instructions

1. Toast the **farro** in a dry saucepan over medium heat for 2-3 minutes, stirring occasionally, until fragrant and lightly golden. This deepens the nutty flavor.

2. Add the **water** and **salt**. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer. Cover and cook for 20-25 minutes until the farro is tender but still has a pleasant chew. Drain any excess water.

3. Transfer the warm farro to a bowl. Toss with the **lemon zest**, **parsley**, and **olive oil**. Season with **pepper** and adjust salt to taste. Let cool completely.

---

**To Store:** Transfer to an airtight container and refrigerate for up to 5 days. Reheat in the microwave with a splash of water, or warm in a skillet with a drizzle of olive oil.

**Used in:** Meals 1, 2
