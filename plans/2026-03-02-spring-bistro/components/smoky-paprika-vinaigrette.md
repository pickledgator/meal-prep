# Smoky Paprika Vinaigrette

_Makes about ⅓ cup_

## Ingredients

- 3 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- 1½ Tbsp Apple Cider Vinegar
- 1 Tbsp Lemon Juice (from prepped lemons)
- 1 tsp Smoked Paprika
- 1 tsp Honey
- 1 small clove Garlic, finely grated
- ¼ tsp Kosher Salt
- Pinch of Black Pepper

## Instructions

1. In a small jar or bowl, combine the **cider vinegar**, **lemon juice**, **smoked paprika**, **honey**, **garlic**, **salt**, and **pepper**. Whisk (or shake) until the honey dissolves and the paprika is evenly distributed.

2. Slowly drizzle in the **olive oil** while whisking continuously until emulsified. Taste and adjust seasoning.

---

**To Store:** Seal the jar tightly and refrigerate for up to 1 week. Shake well before each use — the paprika will settle.

**Used in:** Meals 2, 3
