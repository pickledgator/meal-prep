# Sunday Prep List

_Difficulty: Normal_

---

## Long-Running Tasks (⏱ start these first)

☐ **Toast & Cook Farro** — see `components/herbed-farro.md`¹
Toast 1 cup farro dry, then simmer with water (~25 minutes passive)

---

## Prep Produce (⏱ 15-20 minutes)

_Work through these while the farro simmers_

- ☐ Asparagus (1 bunch; trim woody ends, split into 2 equal batches — leave whole)¹²
- ☐ Shallots (1; mince for M1 pan sauce¹ | 1; thinly slice into rings for frying²³)🫙
- ☐ Garlic (3 cloves; peel — 2 mince¹, 1 finely grate²³🫙)
- ☐ Radishes (6-8; thinly slice, split into 2 portions)²³
- ☐ Lemons (zest 1 lemon¹🫙, then juice all 3 — yield ~6 Tbsp; store juice in a jar)¹²³
- ☐ Parsley (wash, dry, finely chop ~⅓ cup total)¹²³🫙
- ☐ Fingerling Potatoes (¾ lb; wash and scrub, leave whole)³

---

_— CLEANING BREAK —_

---

## Cooked Prep Tasks (⏱ 25-30 minutes)

☐ **Boil Fingerling Potatoes**³
Place whole potatoes in salted cold water, bring to a boil, cook 15-18 minutes until fork-tender. Drain, cool, and store in the fridge.

☐ **Fry Crispy Shallots** — see `components/crispy-shallots.md`²³
Cold-start thinly sliced shallot rings in oil. Fry until deep golden (~10 minutes). Drain on paper towels. Reserve the infused oil.

---

## Prepare Components

☐ **Herbed Farro** (finish) — see `components/herbed-farro.md`¹²
Toss drained cooked farro with lemon zest, chopped parsley, olive oil, salt & pepper. Store.

☐ **Smoky Paprika Vinaigrette** — see `components/smoky-paprika-vinaigrette.md`²³
Whisk together olive oil, cider vinegar, lemon juice (1 Tbsp from prepped juice), smoked paprika, honey, grated garlic, salt & pepper. Store in a jar.

☐ **Honey-Dijon Pan Sauce Base** (for M1)¹
In a small jar, whisk together: 2 Tbsp Dijon mustard, 1 Tbsp honey, ⅓ cup chicken stock, 1 Tbsp lemon juice (from prepped juice). Seal and refrigerate. Pour into pan day-of after searing chicken.

---
