# Lemongrass Marinade

_One marinade, two proteins — aromatic, savory-sweet, and built for the grill_

_Makes about 1¼ cups (⅔ for the chicken, ⅓ reserved for the shrimp)_

## Ingredients

- 4 stalks Lemongrass, tough outer layers removed, bottom 4-5 inches finely minced (about ½ cup)
- 4 cloves of Garlic, minced
- 1 medium Shallot, minced
- ¼ cup (60 ml) Fish Sauce
- 3 Tbsp (40 g) Brown Sugar
- 2 Tbsp (30 ml) fresh Lime Juice (about 1 lime)
- 3 Tbsp (45 ml) Neutral Oil
- 1 Tbsp (15 ml) Soy Sauce
- ½ tsp Freshly Ground Black Pepper

## Instructions

1. Smash the trimmed **lemongrass** with the back of your knife before mincing — this releases the aromatic oils. Mince as finely as you can (or pulse everything in a mini food processor).

2. In a bowl, whisk together the lemongrass, **garlic**, **shallot**, **fish sauce**, **brown sugar**, **lime juice**, **oil**, **soy sauce**, and **black pepper** until the sugar dissolves.

3. **Divide before it touches any raw protein:** pour ⅓ of the marinade (about ⅓ cup) into a separate small jar and refrigerate — this is for the shrimp on Meal 3 night. Use the remaining ⅔ to marinate the chicken thighs now.

---

**To Store:** The reserved shrimp portion keeps refrigerated for up to 5 days. Marinate the chicken 12-24 hours (Sunday night for Monday dinner is perfect).

**Used in:** Meals 1, 3 (lemongrass grilled chicken; grilled shrimp for the bún bowl)
