# Scallion Oil (Mỡ Hành)

_Silky, savory, and endlessly useful — the quiet hero of Vietnamese street food_

_Makes about ¾ cup_

## Ingredients

- ½ cup (120 ml) Neutral Oil (avocado or grapeseed)
- 6 Scallions, thinly sliced (about ¾ cup)
- ¼ tsp Kosher Salt
- Pinch of Granulated Sugar

## Instructions

1. Place the sliced **scallions**, **salt**, and **sugar** in a small heatproof bowl.

2. Heat the **oil** in a small saucepan over medium heat until it shimmers, about 2-3 minutes (a scallion piece dropped in should sizzle immediately).

3. Pour the hot oil over the scallions — they will sizzle and turn vivid green. Stir once and let cool completely.

---

**To Store:** Transfer to a jar and refrigerate for up to 1 week. The oil solidifies slightly when cold; let it sit at room temperature for a few minutes or warm briefly before using.

**Used in:** Meals 1, 2, 3 (tossed with sautéed corn; spooned over meatballs and rice; tossed with vermicelli noodles)
