# Vietnamese Caramel Glaze (Nước Màu)

_Bittersweet burnt caramel meets fish sauce — deep, glossy, and unforgettable on meatballs_

_Makes about ⅔ cup_

## Ingredients

- ⅓ cup (67 g) Granulated Sugar
- 2 Tbsp (30 ml) Water, plus ⅓ cup (80 ml) more for finishing
- 3 Tbsp (45 ml) Fish Sauce
- 2 cloves of Garlic, minced
- 1 Tbsp fresh Ginger, finely grated
- ½ tsp Freshly Ground Black Pepper
- 1 Tbsp (15 ml) fresh Lime Juice (about ½ lime), _added day-of_

## Instructions

1. In a small light-colored saucepan, combine the **sugar** and **2 Tbsp water**. Cook over medium heat WITHOUT stirring (swirl the pan instead) until the sugar melts and turns a deep amber — the color of a copper penny, about 5-7 minutes. Watch closely at the end; it goes from amber to burnt fast.

2. Off the heat, carefully pour in the remaining **⅓ cup water** — it will sputter aggressively. Return to low heat and stir until any hardened caramel dissolves.

3. Stir in the **fish sauce**, **garlic**, **ginger**, and **black pepper**. Simmer 2 minutes until slightly syrupy. Cool.

4. **Day-of (Meal 2):** After glazing the meatballs, finish with the **lime juice** off the heat to brighten.

---

**To Store:** Refrigerate in a jar for up to 1 week. It thickens when cold — it will loosen as it heats in the skillet.

**Used in:** Meal 2 (caramel-glazed turkey meatballs)
