# Nuoc Cham

_The workhorse sauce of the week — bright, pungent, sweet, and spicy all at once_

_Makes about 1⅔ cups_

## Ingredients

- ⅓ cup (80 ml) Fish Sauce
- ⅓ cup (80 ml) fresh Lime Juice (about 3 limes)
- ⅓ cup (67 g) Granulated Sugar
- ⅔ cup (160 ml) Warm Water
- 2 cloves of Garlic, finely minced
- 1 Fresno or Thai Chili, thinly sliced (seeds removed for less heat)

## Instructions

1. In a bowl or jar, stir the **sugar** into the **warm water** until fully dissolved.

2. Stir in the **fish sauce** and **lime juice**.

3. Add the **garlic** and **chili**. Taste — it should hit sweet, sour, salty, and spicy in roughly equal measure. Adjust with more lime (brighter), sugar (rounder), or a splash of water (mellower).

---

**To Store:** Transfer to a jar and refrigerate for up to 1 week. The garlic and chili get more assertive as the week goes on — that's a feature.

**Used in:** Meals 1, 3 (drizzle for grilled chicken; dressing for the bún bowl and the nectarine-Sungold salad)
