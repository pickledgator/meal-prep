# Fried Shallots

_Crispy, sweet-savory shards for scattering over everything_

_Makes about ¾ cup fried shallots (plus ~½ cup shallot oil)_

## Ingredients

- 3 medium Shallots, peeled and sliced into thin, even rings (a mandoline helps)
- ¾ cup (180 ml) Neutral Oil

## Instructions

1. Combine the **shallots** and **oil** in a small saucepan — starting them together in cold oil fries them evenly. Set over medium heat.

2. Cook, stirring occasionally, as the oil comes up to a steady sizzle. Once the shallots turn pale gold (8-12 minutes), stir constantly — they go from gold to bitter quickly.

3. When they're an even light golden brown, immediately strain through a fine-mesh sieve set over a heatproof bowl. Spread the shallots on a paper towel-lined plate; they crisp as they cool.

4. Reserve the strained **shallot oil** — use it for stir-frying the green beans (Meal 2) and the corn (Meal 1) for a free flavor boost.

---

**To Store:** Once fully cool, store shallots airtight at room temperature up to 1 week (refrigeration makes them soggy). Store the oil in a jar in the fridge.

**Used in:** Meals 2, 3 (scattered over meatballs; crowning the bún bowl)
