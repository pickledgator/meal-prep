# Đồ Chua (Pickled Carrot & Daikon)

_Crunchy, tangy quick pickles that cut through everything rich_

_Makes about 3 cups_

## Ingredients

- 2 medium Carrots (about 200 g), peeled and cut into thin matchsticks
- 1 small Daikon Radish (about 225 g), peeled and cut into thin matchsticks
- ¾ cup (180 ml) Rice Vinegar
- ¾ cup (180 ml) Warm Water
- ¼ cup (50 g) Granulated Sugar
- 1½ tsp Kosher Salt

## Instructions

1. In a jar or container, stir the **sugar** and **salt** into the **warm water** until dissolved, then stir in the **rice vinegar**.

2. Pack the **carrot** and **daikon** matchsticks into the brine, pressing to submerge.

3. Refrigerate at least 1 hour before using — they hit their stride after a day.

---

**To Store:** Keep refrigerated in the brine for up to 2 weeks. Drain well before serving.

**Used in:** Meals 2, 3 (bright counterpoint to the caramel meatballs; essential layer in the bún bowl)
