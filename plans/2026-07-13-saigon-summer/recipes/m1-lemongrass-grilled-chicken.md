# Lemongrass Grilled Chicken

_with Saigon Street Corn & Herbs_

_Makes 4 servings_

## Ingredients

### Lemongrass Chicken

- 2 lb (907 g) Boneless-Skinless Chicken Thighs, marinated (from prep)
- Neutral Oil, _for the grill grates_

### Saigon Street Corn

- Kernels from 4 ears Sweet Corn (~3 cups, from prep)
- 3 Tbsp Scallion Oil (from prep) — or use the reserved shallot oil
- 1 clove Garlic, minced (from prep)
- ½ Fresno Chili, thinly sliced (from prep)
- 2 tsp Fish Sauce
- Squeeze of Lime

### Accompaniments

- 3 cups cooked Jasmine Rice, reheated (from prep)
- 2 Persian Cucumbers, sliced (from prep)
- ⅓ cup Nuoc Cham (from prep), _for drizzling_
- Fresh Mint & Cilantro leaves (a small handful each), _for serving_
- 1 Lime, cut into wedges, _for serving_

---

## Instructions

### On Prep Day:

**Marinate the Chicken**

1. Toss the **chicken thighs** with ⅔ of the **lemongrass marinade** in an airtight container. Refrigerate 12-24 hours.

### Day Of:

**Grill the Chicken**

1. Preheat a grill or grill pan to medium (not high — the sugar in the marinade burns easily). Oil the grates.

2. Remove the **chicken** from the marinade, letting excess drip off. Grill 6-7 minutes per side, until deeply caramelized at the edges and cooked through (165°F). Rest 5 minutes, then slice against the grain.

**Reheat the Rice**

3. While the chicken grills, sprinkle the **jasmine rice** with 2 Tbsp water, cover, and microwave until steaming, 2-3 minutes (or warm in a covered saucepan over low heat with a splash of water, stirring once). Keep covered until plating.

**Sauté the Corn**

4. Heat the **scallion oil** in a large skillet over high heat. Add the **garlic** and **chili**; stir 20 seconds until fragrant.

5. Add the **corn kernels** and cook, stirring only occasionally so they pick up char in spots, 3-4 minutes. Season with the **fish sauce** and a **squeeze of lime**.

### Plate & Serve

1. Spoon a bed of **jasmine rice** slightly off-center on each plate. Lean the sliced **chicken** against the rice, and pile the **street corn** alongside.

2. Fan the **cucumber slices** on the open edge of the plate for a cool, crisp contrast.

3. Drizzle the chicken generously with **nuoc cham**, letting it pool into the rice. Shower the whole plate with **mint** and **cilantro** leaves and finish with a **lime wedge**.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Resist moving the chicken for the first few minutes on the grill — the caramelizing marinade is building a lacquered crust that's the whole point of this dish.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** A cold pilsner or an off-dry Riesling stands up beautifully to the lemongrass and chili.
