_with Nectarine-Sungold Salad_

_Makes 4 servings_

## Ingredients

### Lemongrass Shrimp

- 1¼ lb (567 g) Large Shrimp (21-25), peeled & deveined
- Reserved Lemongrass Marinade (about ⅓ cup, from prep)
- Neutral Oil, _for the grill grates_

### Nectarine-Sungold Salad

- 2 firm-ripe Nectarines, sliced into thin wedges
- 1½ cups (a scant pint) Sungold or cherry tomatoes, halved
- ½ Fresno Chili, very thinly sliced (from prep)
- 3 Tbsp Nuoc Cham (from prep)
- 2 Tbsp Roasted Peanuts, roughly chopped
- Small handful Mint leaves, torn

### Bún Bowls

- 10-12 oz (300-340 g) Rice Vermicelli
- 3 Tbsp Scallion Oil (from prep)
- 1 head Butter Lettuce, leaves torn (from prep)
- 3 Persian Cucumbers, cut into batons (from prep)
- 1½ cups Đồ Chua, drained (from prep)
- ¾ cup Nuoc Cham (from prep)
- ¼ cup (35 g) Roasted Peanuts, roughly chopped
- Fried Shallots (a generous pinch per bowl), _for garnish_
- Fresh Mint & Cilantro leaves (a big handful each), _for serving_
- 1 Lime, cut into wedges, _for serving_

---

## Instructions

### Marinate the Shrimp (~20 minutes before cooking)

1. Pat the **shrimp** very dry and toss with the **reserved lemongrass marinade**. Set aside at room temperature for 15-20 minutes — no longer, or the lime starts to cure them. Thread onto skewers if grilling.

### Cook the Noodles

2. Meanwhile, bring a large pot of water to a boil. Cook the **rice vermicelli** per package (usually 3-5 minutes), rinse under cold water until fully cool, and drain very well. Toss with the **scallion oil** so the strands stay loose.

### Make the Salad

3. In a bowl, gently toss the **nectarines**, **Sungolds**, **chili**, and **nuoc cham** (3 Tbsp). Top with **peanuts** and torn **mint** just before serving.

### Grill the Shrimp

4. Heat a grill or grill pan to medium-high and oil the grates. Grill the **shrimp** 90 seconds to 2 minutes per side, until just opaque with charred edges. Don't walk away — shrimp forgive nothing.

### Plate & Serve

1. Line each wide bowl with **lettuce** on one side, then a nest of **scallion-oil noodles** in the center.

2. Arrange the **shrimp**, **cucumber batons**, and drained **đồ chua** in separate piles around the noodles — a bún bowl should look like a color wheel, not a mix.

3. Pour 3 Tbsp **nuoc cham** over each bowl (or serve alongside for dipping). Finish with **peanuts**, **fried shallots**, and big handfuls of **mint** and **cilantro**, with **lime wedges** on the rim.

4. Serve the **nectarine-Sungold salad** in a shared bowl at the table.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Toss everything together at the table before eating — the point of bún is dragging every bite through the nuoc cham pooled at the bottom.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** Grüner Veltliner or a crisp lager — anything cold and zippy.
