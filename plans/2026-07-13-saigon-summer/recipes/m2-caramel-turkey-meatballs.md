# Caramel-Glazed Turkey Meatballs

_with Blistered Green Beans & Jasmine Rice_

_Makes 4 servings_

## Ingredients

### Turkey Meatballs

- 1½ lb (680 g) Ground Turkey (93% lean — dark meat keeps them juicy)
- 2 Scallions, thinly sliced (from prep)
- 1 medium Shallot, minced (from prep)
- 2 cloves Garlic, minced (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp fresh Ginger, grated (from prep)
- 2 Tbsp Cilantro stems, finely chopped (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp Fish Sauce
- 1 Egg
- ½ cup (30 g) Panko Breadcrumbs
- ¼ tsp Freshly Ground Black Pepper
- 2 Tbsp Neutral Oil, _for cooking_
- 1 batch Caramel Glaze (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp fresh Lime Juice (about ½ lime)

### Blistered Green Beans

- 1 lb (454 g) Green Beans, blanched (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp reserved Shallot Oil (or neutral oil)
- 3 cloves Garlic, thinly sliced (from prep)
- 2 tsp Fish Sauce

### Accompaniments

- 3 cups cooked Jasmine Rice, reheated (from prep)
- 1 cup Đồ Chua, drained (from prep)
- 2-3 Tbsp Scallion Oil (from prep), _for drizzling_
- Fried Shallots (a generous pinch per bowl), _for garnish_
- Fresh Mint & Cilantro leaves (a small handful each), _for serving_

---

## Instructions

### Make the Meatballs

1. In a large bowl, gently combine the **ground turkey**, **scallions**, **shallot**, **garlic**, **ginger**, **cilantro stems**, **fish sauce**, **egg**, **panko**, and **black pepper**. Mix just until it comes together — overworking makes them dense.

2. With lightly oiled hands, roll into ~20 golf ball-sized meatballs (about 1½ Tbsp each).

3. Heat the **oil** in a large nonstick or cast-iron skillet over medium-high. Brown the meatballs on two sides, 5-6 minutes total (work in batches if crowded).

### Glaze

4. Pour the **caramel glaze** into the skillet and reduce heat to medium. Simmer, rolling the meatballs to coat, until the glaze is glossy and the meatballs are cooked through (165°F), 4-5 minutes. Off the heat, add the **lime juice** and one more roll through the glaze.

### Reheat the Rice

5. While the meatballs brown, sprinkle the **jasmine rice** with 2 Tbsp water, cover, and microwave until steaming, 2-3 minutes (or warm in a covered saucepan over low heat with a splash of water, stirring once). Keep covered until plating.

### Blister the Green Beans

6. Heat the **shallot oil** in a second skillet over high heat until nearly smoking. Add the **blanched green beans** in one layer and cook undisturbed 1-2 minutes until blistered, then toss.

7. Add the **sliced garlic**, toss 30 seconds, and finish with the **fish sauce**.

### Plate & Serve

1. Mound the **jasmine rice** in shallow bowls. Nestle 5 **meatballs** per bowl on one side and spoon any extra glaze from the pan over the top.

2. Pile the **green beans** on the other side and tuck a tangle of drained **đồ chua** between them — the pink-orange pickles against the dark glaze is the shot.

3. Drizzle the rice with **scallion oil**, then finish everything with **fried shallots**, **mint**, and **cilantro**.

---

> **Hot Tip:** A light hand is everything with turkey meatballs — mix with your fingertips, not your palms, and stop the moment the ingredients are distributed.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** The sweet-salty glaze loves a chilled dry rosé or sparkling water with lime.
