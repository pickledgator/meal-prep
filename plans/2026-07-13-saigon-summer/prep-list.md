# Sunday Prep List

_Difficulty: Normal · Estimated total: 75-90 minutes_

---

☐ **Cook Jasmine Rice** (2 cups / 400 g dry)¹²
Rinse the rice in a fine-mesh sieve until the water runs clear. Combine with 2½ cups (600 ml) water and ¾ tsp kosher salt in a medium saucepan. Bring to a boil uncovered, then cover, reduce to the lowest heat, and cook 15 minutes. Rest off the heat, still covered, 10 minutes; fluff with a fork. (yield: ~6 cups) Cool fully, then store as two 3-cup portions labeled "M1 — chicken" and "M2 — meatballs."

## Cooked Prep Tasks

☐ **Blanch Green Beans**²
Boil in well-salted water for 2 minutes, then transfer straight into an ice bath. Drain, dry, and store in an airtight container labeled "M2 — green beans." They'll blister in 3 minutes on meatball night.

☐ **Fry Shallots** — see `components/fried-shallots.md`²³
Start the rings in cold oil, fry to light gold, strain. **Reserve the strained shallot oil** in a jar — it stir-fries the green beans² and corn¹.

☐ **Scallion Oil** — see `components/scallion-oil.md`¹²³
Pour the hot oil over the sliced scallions; cool before storing.

☐ **Vietnamese Caramel Glaze** — see `components/caramel-glaze.md`²
Take the caramel to deep amber — pale caramel makes a flat glaze.

---

## Prepare Components

☐ **Nuoc Cham** — see `components/nuoc-cham.md`¹³
Makes ~1⅔ cups: ~⅓ cup for Meal 1, ~1 cup for Meal 3 (bowls + salad).

☐ **Đồ Chua** — see `components/do-chua.md`²³
Pack the carrot & daikon matchsticks into the brine. Makes ~3 cups: ~1 cup Meal 2, ~1½ cups Meal 3.

☐ **Lemongrass Marinade** — see `components/lemongrass-marinade.md`¹³
**Before it touches chicken:** pour ⅓ (about ⅓ cup) into a separate jar labeled "M3 — shrimp."

☐ **Marinate the Chicken** (2 lb thighs + remaining ⅔ marinade; airtight container, refrigerate 12-24 hours)¹
_Do this last — raw protein always ends the prep session._

---

## Day-Of Reminders (not Sunday work)

- **Meal 2:** Mix & form the meatballs day-of (~10 min) — the aromatics are already prepped and waiting in the "M2 — meatballs" containers.
- **Meal 3:** Marinate the shrimp only 15-20 minutes; cook the vermicelli fresh; slice the nectarines just before serving.
