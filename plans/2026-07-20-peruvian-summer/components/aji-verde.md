# Yogurt Ají Verde

_Makes about 1¼ cups_

## Ingredients

- 1 cup (227 g) Plain Greek Yogurt
- 1 packed cup (30 g) Cilantro leaves
- 1 large Jalapeño, stemmed and roughly chopped (seed for less heat)
- 1 Tbsp (15 g) Ají Amarillo Paste
- 2 Tbsp fresh Lime Juice
- 1 Tbsp Red Wine Vinegar
- 1 Garlic clove, roughly chopped
- 2 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- ½ tsp Kosher Salt
- Freshly ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

## Instructions

1. Add the **Greek yogurt**, **cilantro**, **jalapeño**, **ají amarillo paste**, **lime juice**, **red wine vinegar**, **garlic**, **olive oil**, and **salt** to a blender.

2. Blend until smooth and vivid green, scraping down the blender as needed. Season with **black pepper** and more **salt** to taste.

3. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before serving so the flavor can settle.

---

**To Store:** Refrigerate in an airtight container for up to 4 days. If Meal 2 is later in the week, press a piece of reusable wrap directly against the surface to preserve the color.

**Used in:** Meals 1 and 2; use ⅓ cup in Meal 1 and ¾ cup in Meal 2, leaving about 2–3 Tbsp as a buffer
