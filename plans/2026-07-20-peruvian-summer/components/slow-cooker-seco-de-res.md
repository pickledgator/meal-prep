# Slow-Cooker Seco de Res

_A bone-in beef shank adaptation of Peru's cilantro-and-ají stew_

_Makes about 5 cups beef and sauce; 4 servings_

## Ingredients

- 3½–4 lb (1.6–1.8 kg) Bone-In Beef Shank, fully thawed
- 2 tsp Kosher Salt
- 1 tsp freshly ground Black Pepper
- 2 tsp Ground Cumin
- 1 tsp Dried Oregano
- 1½ packed cups (45 g) Cilantro leaves and tender stems
- 1 cup (240 ml) Low-Sodium Beef Broth
- 1 Tbsp Neutral High-Heat Oil
- 1 large Yellow Onion (about 12 oz / 340 g), sliced
- 1 large Red Bell Pepper (about 7 oz / 200 g), diced
- 5 Garlic cloves, roughly chopped
- 2 Tbsp (30 g) Ají Panca Paste
- 1 Tbsp (15 g) Ají Amarillo Paste
- 2 Tbsp (32 g) Tomato Paste
- 12 fl oz (355 ml) Lager
- 2 Tbsp Red Wine Vinegar
- 2 Bay Leaves

## Instructions

1. Pat the **beef shank** very dry. Mix the **salt**, **black pepper**, **cumin**, and **oregano**, then rub the seasoning over every surface of the beef. In a blender, puree the **cilantro** with the **beef broth** until nearly smooth.

2. Heat the **neutral oil** in a large heavy skillet over medium-high heat. Brown the seasoned shank deeply on all accessible sides, 3–4 minutes per side. Work in batches if the shank is cross-cut. Transfer the browned beef to a 6-quart slow cooker.

3. Lower the skillet to medium. Add the **yellow onion** and **red bell pepper** and cook for 5 minutes, scraping up the browned bits. Add the **garlic**, **ají panca paste**, **ají amarillo paste**, and **tomato paste**; cook until brick-red and fragrant, about 90 seconds.

4. Pour in the **lager** and scrape the skillet thoroughly. Simmer for 2 minutes, then stir in the **red wine vinegar** and the cilantro-broth puree. Pour everything over the beef and tuck in the **bay leaves**.

5. Cover and cook on **LOW for 8–9 hours** (preferred) or **HIGH for 5–6 hours**, until the meat pulls away from the bone without resistance. Do not start from frozen; the shank must be fully thawed before it goes into the slow cooker.

6. Transfer the shank to a rimmed tray. Discard the **bay leaves** and any loose bones, cartilage, or large pieces of fat. Pull the meat into large, tender pieces. Skim the surface fat from the sauce.

7. If the sauce is thinner than gravy, pour it into the skillet and boil over medium-high heat for 10–15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it coats a spoon. Return the shredded **beef** to the sauce, taste, and adjust with **salt** and **black pepper** as needed.

---

**To Store:** Cool in a shallow container, then refrigerate for up to 4 days or freeze for up to 3 months. Keep the meat submerged in sauce so it stays moist.
