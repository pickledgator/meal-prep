# Ají Amarillo-Lime Vinaigrette

_Makes about 1⅛ cups_

## Ingredients

- ½ cup (120 ml) Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- ¼ cup (60 ml) fresh Lime Juice
- 2 Tbsp Red Wine Vinegar
- 2 Tbsp (30 g) Ají Amarillo Paste
- 1 Tbsp Honey
- 1 Garlic clove, finely grated
- ½ tsp Ground Cumin
- ¾ tsp Kosher Salt
- Freshly ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

## Instructions

1. In a jar, combine the **olive oil**, **lime juice**, **red wine vinegar**, **ají amarillo paste**, **honey**, **garlic**, **cumin**, **salt**, and several grinds of **black pepper**.

2. Seal and shake vigorously for 20 seconds, until emulsified. Taste; it should be bright and gently spicy. Adjust with **salt** and **black pepper** as needed.

---

**To Store:** Refrigerate in the sealed jar for up to 5 days. Shake well before each use.

**Used in:** Meals 1 and 2; use ½ cup per meal, leaving about 2 Tbsp for tasting and plating
