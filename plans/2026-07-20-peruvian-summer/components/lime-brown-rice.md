# Lime Brown Rice

_Makes about 6 cups cooked_

## Ingredients

- 2 cups (385 g) Long-Grain Brown Rice
- 3½ cups (840 ml) Water
- 1 tsp Kosher Salt
- Finely grated zest and 2 Tbsp juice of 1 Lime
- 1 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil

## Instructions

1. Put the **brown rice** in a fine-mesh strainer and rinse under cool running water for 30 seconds. Drain thoroughly.

2. Combine the rinsed rice, **3½ cups water**, and **salt** in a medium saucepan. Bring to a boil over high heat. Stir once, cover tightly, and reduce the heat to low.

3. Cook, covered and undisturbed, for 40–45 minutes, until the water is absorbed and the grains are tender. Remove from the heat and let stand, still covered, for 10 minutes.

4. Fluff with a fork, then fold in the **lime zest**, **lime juice**, and **olive oil**. Spread on a rimmed sheet pan to cool quickly.

5. Divide into two 3-cup portions. Store one labeled “M1 — rice” and the other labeled “M2 — rice.”

---

**To Store:** Refrigerate for up to 4 days. If Meal 2 is Thursday or later, freeze its portion as soon as the rice is cool and thaw it overnight in the refrigerator.

**Used in:** Meals 1 and 2
