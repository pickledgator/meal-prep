# Quick-Pickled Red Onions

_Makes about 1½ cups_

## Ingredients

- 1 medium Red Onion (about 8 oz / 227 g), very thinly sliced
- ½ cup (120 ml) Red Wine Vinegar
- ½ cup (120 ml) Water
- 1 Tbsp White Cane Sugar
- 1½ tsp Kosher Salt

## Instructions

1. Pack the **red onion** into a clean heatproof pint jar.

2. Combine the **red wine vinegar**, **water**, **sugar**, and **salt** in a small saucepan. Bring just to a simmer, stirring until the sugar and salt dissolve.

3. Pour the hot brine over the onion and press the slices below the surface. Cool uncovered for 30 minutes, then seal and refrigerate.

---

**To Store:** Refrigerate for up to 2 weeks. Always use a clean utensil to remove onions from the brine.

**Used in:** Meals 1 and 2; use ⅔ cup per meal, leaving about 2–3 Tbsp as a buffer
