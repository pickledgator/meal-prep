# Ají-Panca Chicken Marinade

_Makes about ½ cup; enough for 1¾ lb (794 g) chicken thighs_

## Ingredients

- 2 Tbsp (30 g) Ají Panca Paste
- 2 Tbsp Low-Sodium Soy Sauce or Tamari
- 2 Tbsp fresh Lime Juice
- 1 Tbsp Red Wine Vinegar
- 2 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- 4 Garlic cloves, finely grated
- 1 tsp Ground Cumin
- 1 tsp Dried Oregano
- ½ tsp freshly ground Black Pepper

## Instructions

1. Whisk the **ají panca paste**, **soy sauce**, **lime juice**, **red wine vinegar**, **olive oil**, **garlic**, **cumin**, **oregano**, and **black pepper** until smooth.

2. Pour over 1¾ lb (794 g) boneless-skinless chicken thighs and turn to coat. Marinate in the refrigerator for 30 minutes to 24 hours.

3. If Meal 2 is more than 24 hours away, freeze the chicken in the marinade immediately. Move it to the refrigerator the night before Meal 2; it will marinate as it thaws.

---

**To Store:** Keep unused marinade refrigerated for up to 3 days. Once it has touched raw chicken, discard any excess rather than serving it as a sauce.

**Used in:** Meal 2
