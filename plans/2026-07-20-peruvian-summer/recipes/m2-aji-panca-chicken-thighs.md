# Ají-Panca Chicken Thighs

_with Charred Summer Vegetables & Ají Verde_

_Makes 4 servings_

## Ingredients

### Ají-Panca Chicken

- 1¾ lb (794 g) Boneless-Skinless Chicken Thighs, about 8 small thighs
- 1 batch Ají-Panca Chicken Marinade (about ½ cup), from prep

### Charred Summer Vegetables

- 2 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil, divided
- 3 medium Zucchini (1¼ lb / 567 g), cut into ¾-inch half-moons (from prep)
- 1 large Red Bell Pepper (about 7 oz / 200 g), cut into ½-inch strips (from prep)
- 2 cups (300 g) fresh Sweet Corn kernels (from prep)
- ½ tsp Ground Cumin
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_
- Freshly ground Black Pepper, _to taste_
- 1 lb (454 g) ripe Tomatoes, cut into bite-size wedges
- ½ batch Ají Amarillo-Lime Vinaigrette (½ cup), shaken
- ½ cup loosely packed Cilantro leaves, roughly chopped

### Accompaniments

- ½ batch Lime Brown Rice (about 3 cups), from prep
- 2 Tbsp Water, _for reheating the rice_
- ¾ cup Yogurt Ají Verde
- ⅔ cup Quick-Pickled Red Onions, drained
- 1 Lime, cut into wedges

---

## Instructions

### On Prep Day:

**Marinate the Chicken**

1. Prepare the **Ají-Panca Chicken Marinade** according to `components/aji-panca-chicken-marinade.md` and coat the **chicken thighs**. Refrigerate for no more than 24 hours; if Meal 2 is later than Monday, freeze the chicken in its marinade immediately and thaw it overnight in the refrigerator before cooking.

### Day Of:

**Char the Vegetables**

1. If frozen, make sure the marinated **chicken** thawed overnight in the refrigerator. Remove it from the refrigerator while you heat a large heavy skillet over medium-high. Scrape off excess marinade so it does not scorch; discard the used marinade.

2. Add 1 Tbsp **olive oil** to the hot skillet. Add the **zucchini** and **red bell pepper**, season with **salt** and **black pepper**, and cook without moving for 3 minutes. Toss and cook until browned but still firm, 3 minutes more. Transfer to a large serving bowl.

3. Add the remaining 1 Tbsp **olive oil**, the **sweet corn**, and **cumin** to the skillet. Season with **salt** and **black pepper** and cook, stirring only occasionally, until blistered in spots, 4–5 minutes. Add the corn to the bowl with the zucchini. Fold in the raw **tomatoes**, **½ cup vinaigrette**, and **cilantro**. Taste and adjust the seasoning.

**Cook the Chicken and Reheat the Rice**

4. Return the skillet to medium-high heat. Lay in the **marinated chicken thighs** in a single layer. Cook for 5–6 minutes without moving, flip, then cook for another 5–7 minutes, until browned and an instant-read thermometer reaches 165°F at the thickest point. Rest for 3 minutes.

5. While the chicken cooks, put the **lime brown rice** and **2 Tbsp water** in a microwave-safe bowl. Cover loosely and microwave on high for 2 minutes. Stir, then microwave for 1–2 minutes more, until piping hot.

### Plate & Serve

1. Spread 3 Tbsp **Yogurt Ají Verde** into a swoop on each of four plates. Add a mound of hot **lime brown rice**, then lean two chicken thighs against it. Spoon the charred summer vegetables alongside, letting some vinaigrette run toward the rice. Crown each plate with **drained pickled red onions** and finish with a **lime wedge**.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Browning depends on a dry, uncrowded pan. If your skillet is smaller than 12 inches, cook the chicken in two batches rather than steaming it.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** Pair with a cold lager, a tart passion-fruit seltzer, or chilled hibiscus tea.
