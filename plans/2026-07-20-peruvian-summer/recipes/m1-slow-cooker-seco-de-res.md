_with Summer Corn-Tomato Salad & Lime Brown Rice_

_Makes 4 servings_

## Ingredients

### Seco & Rice

- 1 batch Slow-Cooker Seco de Res (about 5 cups), from prep
- ½ batch Lime Brown Rice (about 3 cups), from prep
- 2 Tbsp Water, _for reheating the rice_

### Summer Corn-Tomato Salad

- 2 cups (300 g) fresh Sweet Corn kernels, from prep
- 1 lb (454 g) ripe Tomatoes, cut into bite-size wedges
- ½ batch Ají Amarillo-Lime Vinaigrette (½ cup), shaken
- ⅔ cup Quick-Pickled Red Onions, drained
- ½ cup loosely packed Cilantro leaves, roughly chopped
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_
- Freshly ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

### To Finish

- ⅓ cup Yogurt Ají Verde
- 1 Lime, cut into wedges

---

## Instructions

### On Prep Day:

**Slow-Cook the Beef**

1. Prepare the **Slow-Cooker Seco de Res** according to `components/slow-cooker-seco-de-res.md`. Cool it in its sauce and refrigerate.

### Day Of:

**Reheat the Seco and Rice**

1. Put the **seco de res** in a wide saucepan, cover, and warm over medium-low heat for 12–15 minutes, stirring twice, until the meat and sauce are steaming throughout. If the sauce tightens too much, add water 1 Tbsp at a time.

2. While the beef warms, put the **lime brown rice** and **2 Tbsp water** in a microwave-safe bowl. Cover loosely and microwave on high for 2 minutes. Stir, then microwave for 1–2 minutes more, until piping hot. Keep covered.

**Build the Summer Salad**

3. In a large bowl, combine the raw **sweet corn**, **tomatoes**, **½ cup vinaigrette**, **drained pickled red onions**, and **cilantro**. Toss gently, taste, and season with **salt** and **black pepper**. Let stand for 5 minutes while the beef finishes reheating.

### Plate & Serve

1. Spoon the hot **lime brown rice** into one side of four shallow bowls. Nestle the **seco de res** beside and partly over the rice, letting the green cilantro sauce pool underneath. Mound the bright corn-tomato salad on the open side. Dot each bowl with **Yogurt Ají Verde** and finish with a **lime wedge**.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Seco tastes even better after a night in the refrigerator. Reheat it gently so the shank stays in succulent pieces instead of breaking into dry strands.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** Pour the same crisp lager used in the braise, or serve sparkling water with extra lime.
