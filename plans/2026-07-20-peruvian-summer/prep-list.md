# Sunday Prep List

_Difficulty: Normal — about 75–90 minutes active, plus 8–9 hours unattended_

---

## Before Sunday

☐ **Thaw Beef Shank** — move the 3½–4 lb (1.6–1.8 kg) bone-in shank from freezer to refrigerator by Friday morning; allow up to 48 hours to thaw fully¹

---

## Prep for Long-Running Tasks (⏱ about 25 minutes)

- ☐ **Yellow Onion — 1 large / about 12 oz / 340 g**
  - all → peel; slice → Beef Shank Seco de Res 🫙
- ☐ **Garlic — 11 cloves**
  - 5 → roughly chop → Beef Shank Seco de Res 🫙
  - 1 → finely grate → Ají Amarillo-Lime Vinaigrette 🫙
  - 1 → roughly chop → Yogurt Ají Verde 🫙
  - 4 → finely grate → Ají-Panca Chicken Marinade 🫙
- ☐ **Red Bell Peppers — 2 large / about 14 oz / 400 g**
  - 1 → stem; seed; dice → Beef Shank Seco de Res 🫙
  - 1 → stem; seed; cut into ½-inch strips → “M2 — charred vegetables”²
- ☐ **Cilantro — 2 large bunches / about 4 oz / 114 g**
  - 1½ packed cups leaves and tender stems (45 g) → wash; dry → Beef Shank Seco de Res 🫙
  - 1 packed cup leaves (30 g) → wash; dry → Yogurt Ají Verde 🫙
  - ½ cup loosely packed leaves (about 15 g) → wash; dry; leave whole → “M1 — salad & garnish”¹
  - ½ cup loosely packed leaves (about 15 g) → wash; dry; leave whole → “M2 — vegetables & garnish”²
  - remainder → wash; dry; divide → “M1/M2 — garnish”¹²
- ☐ **Limes — 7**
  - 2 → juice to yield ¼ cup (60 ml) → Ají Amarillo-Lime Vinaigrette 🫙
  - 1 → juice to yield 2 Tbsp → Yogurt Ají Verde 🫙
  - 1 → finely zest; juice to yield 2 Tbsp → Lime Brown Rice 🫙
  - 1 → juice to yield 2 Tbsp → Ají-Panca Chicken Marinade 🫙
  - 1 → leave whole → “M1 — serving wedges”¹
  - 1 → leave whole → “M2 — serving wedges”²

## Prep Remaining Produce (⏱ about 15 minutes)

- ☐ **Red Onion — 1 medium / about 8 oz / 227 g**
  - all → peel; very thinly slice → Quick-Pickled Red Onions 🫙
- ☐ **Sweet Corn — 4 ears / about 4 cups / 600 g kernels**
  - 2 ears → shuck; cut about 2 cups (300 g) kernels → “M1 — corn-tomato salad”¹
  - 2 ears → shuck; cut about 2 cups (300 g) kernels → “M2 — charred vegetables”²
- ☐ **Ripe Tomatoes — 2 lb / 907 g**
  - 1 lb (454 g) → wash; dry; leave whole → “M1 — corn-tomato salad; cut day-of”¹
  - 1 lb (454 g) → wash; dry; leave whole → “M2 — charred vegetables; cut day-of”²
- ☐ **Zucchini — 3 medium / 1¼ lb / 567 g**
  - all → halve lengthwise; cut into ¾-inch half-moons → “M2 — charred vegetables”²; add a dry paper towel
- ☐ **Jalapeño — 1 large / about 1 oz / 28 g**
  - all → stem; roughly chop; seed for less heat → Yogurt Ají Verde 🫙
