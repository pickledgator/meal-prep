## Ingredients

### The Salad

- 5 heads Little Gem Lettuce, separated into whole leaves (from prep)
- 4 packed cups (8 oz / 227 g) shaved Brussels Sprouts (from prep)
- 20 oz (567 g) picked Rotisserie Chicken (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- 1 cup Caesar Dressing — `components/caesar-dressing.md`
- 4½ cups Garlic Sourdough Croutons (the full batch) — `components/garlic-sourdough-croutons.md`
- ½ cup (1½ oz / 40 g) shaved Parmigiano-Reggiano
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

### To Serve

- 5 Lemon Wedges (from prep)

---

## Instructions

### Day Of:

**Warm the Chicken**

1. Heat the **olive oil** in a large skillet over medium-high. Add the **picked chicken** in a single layer and leave it alone for 2–3 minutes, until the edges sizzle and catch some brown, then toss once and cook 1 minute more. Season with **salt** and **pepper** and slide it onto a plate to sit while you build the salad — you want it warm and a little crisp, not piping hot.

**Dress the Salad**

2. Put the **shaved brussels sprouts** in your largest bowl and add ⅓ cup of the **Caesar dressing**. Toss thoroughly and let them sit for 5 minutes — shaved sprouts are sturdy enough to soften and sweeten in the dressing, which is exactly why they go in first.

3. Add the **little gem leaves** and the remaining dressing (about ⅔ cup). Toss gently with your hands, lifting from the bottom, until every leaf is coated and glossy.

4. Shave the **Parmigiano-Reggiano** into wide ribbons with a vegetable peeler. Add the **croutons** and about half the parmesan to the bowl and toss once more, briefly.

### Plate & Serve

5. Pile the salad high onto plates, keeping the little gem leaves standing rather than flattened. Tuck the warm chicken into the folds of the leaves in a few places instead of burying it under the greens. Shower the remaining parmesan ribbons over the top, add a heavy grind of **black pepper**, and set a **lemon wedge** on each plate for squeezing at the table.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Shave the brussels sprouts on a mandoline if you have one, or halve them and slice as thin as your knife allows. If they come out chunky, add a pinch of salt when you dress them and give them 10 minutes instead of 5.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** A cold, high-acid white — Vermentino, Pinot Grigio, or a dry Lugana.
