# Pan-Seared Sirloin with Parmesan Polenta

_with Roasted Broccoli & Brussels and Lemon-Rosemary Butter_

_Makes 5 servings_

## Ingredients

### Roasted Broccoli & Brussels

- 1 lb (454 g) Brussels Sprouts, halved (from prep)
- 1 lb (454 g) Broccoli Florets (from prep)
- 3 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_
- 3–4 Tbsp Basil Pesto — `components/basil-pesto.md`
- 5 Lemon Wedges (from prep)

### The Steak

- 2 lb (907 g) Top Sirloin, salted (from prep) — 2 steaks, about 1¼-inch thick
- 1 Tbsp Avocado Oil
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_
- 5 coins Lemon-Rosemary Butter — `components/lemon-rosemary-butter.md`

### Parmesan Polenta

- 6 cups Parmesan Polenta (from prep) — `components/parmesan-polenta.md`
- ¾–1 cup reserved Chicken Broth (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp Salted Butter
- Kosher Salt & Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

### Heirloom Tomato Side

- 2 Heirloom Tomatoes (from prep)
- Extra-Virgin Olive Oil, _for drizzling_
- Flaky Salt, _to taste_
- ¼ cup torn Basil leaves

---

## Instructions

### Day Of:

**Roast the Vegetables**

1. Slide a rimmed sheet pan into the oven and preheat to 450°F. Meanwhile, toss the **halved brussels sprouts** and **broccoli florets** with the **olive oil**, a big pinch of **salt**, and **pepper**. Spread them onto the hot pan in a single layer, turning the brussels cut-side down, and roast 16–20 minutes without stirring, until the cut faces are deeply browned and the broccoli tips are charred and crisp.

**Sear the Steak**

2. Pull the **steaks** from the fridge, pat the surface dry, and grind **black pepper** over both sides. Heat the **avocado oil** in a cast-iron skillet over medium-high until it just begins to smoke. Lay in the steaks and don't move them for 4 minutes. Flip and cook 3–4 minutes more, until an instant-read thermometer reads 128–130°F at the center for medium-rare.

3. Move the steaks to a cutting board and set all 5 coins of the **lemon-rosemary butter** across the tops. Rest for 8–10 minutes. Don't skip the rest — this is where the juices redistribute, and the butter melting into them becomes a pan sauce by accident.

**Reheat the Polenta**

4. While the steaks rest, scrape the cold **parmesan polenta** into a saucepan with ¾ cup of the **reserved chicken broth**. Set over medium-low heat and whisk for 8–10 minutes, breaking up the set blocks, until loose and creamy — add the remaining broth a splash at a time if it's stiff. Beat in the **salted butter** and season with **salt** and **pepper**.

**The Tomatoes**

5. Slice the **heirloom tomatoes** ½-inch thick, shingle them on a small plate, and drizzle with **olive oil**. Finish with **flaky salt** and half the **torn basil**.

6. Slice the rested steak against the grain into ½-inch planks, then spoon the pooled butter and board juices back over the slices.

### Plate & Serve

7. Spoon a wide pool of polenta into the base of each shallow bowl or plate and swirl the back of the spoon through it. Fan the steak slices across one side so the buttery juices run into the polenta, and pile the roasted broccoli and brussels opposite them. Spoon the **basil pesto** over the vegetables only — it should read as bright green against the browned florets. Scatter the last of the **torn basil**, set a **lemon wedge** on each plate for squeezing over the vegetables, and pass the tomato plate at the table.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Pull the steak at 128–130°F, not at your target. Carryover heat takes a 1¼-inch sirloin up another 5 degrees while it rests, which lands it at a perfect medium-rare.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** Something with structure — Chianti Classico, Montepulciano, or a Cabernet Franc.
