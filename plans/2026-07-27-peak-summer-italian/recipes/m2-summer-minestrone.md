# Summer Minestrone with Chicken

_with Basil Pesto & Grilled Garlic Bread_

_Makes 5 servings_

---

## Prepped Ingredients

> From your Sunday prep:
>
> - Summer Minestrone Base — `components/minestrone-base.md`
> - Basil Pesto — `components/basil-pesto.md`
> - "M2 — soup chicken" (12 oz / 340 g shredded rotisserie chicken)
> - "M2 — soup vegetables" (broccoli florets, diced zucchini)
> - "M2 — soup topping" (¼ cup grated parmesan)
> - "M2 — soup finish" (3 Tbsp lemon juice)

> - "M2/M3 — torn basil" (basil on the counter)

## Ingredients

### The Soup

- 9 cups Summer Minestrone Base (the full batch) — `components/minestrone-base.md`
- ½ cup Water, _as needed_
- 8 oz (227 g) small Broccoli Florets (from prep)
- 2½ cups (12 oz / 340 g) diced Zucchini (from prep)
- 12 oz (340 g) shredded Rotisserie Chicken (from prep)
- 3 Tbsp fresh Lemon Juice (from prep)
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

### Grilled Garlic Bread

- 5 thick slices Sourdough (from prep)
- 2 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- 1 clove Garlic, halved (from prep)

### To Finish

- 5–7 Tbsp Basil Pesto (1–1½ Tbsp per bowl) — `components/basil-pesto.md`
- ¼ cup (20 g) finely grated Parmigiano-Reggiano (from prep)
- ¼ cup torn Basil leaves
- Extra-Virgin Olive Oil, _for drizzling_

---

## Instructions

### Day Of:

**Bring the Base Up**

1. Scrape the **minestrone base** into a large Dutch oven and set over medium heat. Bring to a simmer, 8–10 minutes, stirring now and then and loosening with the **water** if it has set thick in the fridge. Fish out the **parmesan rind** and **bay leaf** and discard them.

2. Add the **broccoli florets**. Simmer 3 minutes.

3. Add the **diced zucchini**. Simmer 3 minutes more — the broccoli should be bright green and still have snap, the zucchini just tender. Stop here; another five minutes turns both of them gray.

4. Stir in the **shredded chicken** and warm through, about 2 minutes. Off the heat, stir in the **lemon juice**, then taste and season with **salt** and plenty of **black pepper**. The lemon is what makes it taste like July rather than November — add it until the broth tastes lifted.

**Grill the Bread**

5. While the soup simmers, brush both sides of the **sourdough slices** with the **olive oil**. Toast in a grill pan, cast-iron skillet, or on the grill over medium-high heat until charred in stripes, about 2 minutes per side. While each slice is still hot, scrub the cut face of the **halved garlic clove** across the surface — the toasted crust acts as a grater.

### Plate & Serve

6. Ladle the soup into wide, shallow bowls — it should be chunky enough that a spoon stands up in it, about 2½ cups per person. Spoon 1–1½ Tbsp of **basil pesto** into the center of each and drag it through once with the tip of a spoon so it ribbons green through the broth rather than disappearing into it. Scatter the **grated parmesan** and **torn basil** over the top, finish with a thread of **olive oil** and a grind of **black pepper**, and lean a slice of garlic bread against the rim.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Never stir pesto into the pot. Basil turns bitter and drab the moment it hits sustained heat — added at the bowl, it stays sharp and perfumed.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** A light, chillable red — Barbera or a young Chianti served at cellar temperature.
