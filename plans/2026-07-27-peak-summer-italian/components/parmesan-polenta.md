# Parmesan Polenta

_Built from grits and a tube of prepared polenta_

_Makes about 6 cups (5 servings)_

The grits do the work a long-cooked coarse polenta would — they turn silky and hold liquid. The tube, grated in at the end, adds cornmeal body and thickens the pot without another 30 minutes of whisking. Creamy without any cream, so it stays lactose-light.

## Ingredients

- ¾ cup (120 g) Grits (stone-ground or quick — not instant)
- 3½ cups (840 ml) Water
- 1 tsp Kosher Salt, plus more _to taste_
- 1 (18 oz / 510 g) tube Prepared Polenta, coarsely grated (from prep)
- 1 cup (240 ml) Rotisserie Chicken Broth — `components/rotisserie-chicken-broth.md`
- 3 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- ¾ cup (60 g) finely grated Parmigiano-Reggiano (from prep)
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

### To Reheat (Meal 3 night)

- ~1 cup reserved Rotisserie Chicken Broth or Water
- 1 Tbsp Salted Butter

## Instructions

### On Prep Day:

1. Bring the **water** and **salt** to a rolling boil in a heavy saucepan. Rain the **grits** in with one hand while whisking with the other. Once the whisk leaves a trail, drop the heat to its lowest setting and cook, whisking hard for 15 seconds every 5 minutes: **25–35 minutes for stone-ground, 5–8 for quick**. It's ready when a taste has no grit left in it. The pot will look loose and soupy — that's correct, the polenta thickens it next.

2. Add the **grated prepared polenta** a large handful at a time, whisking after each addition, then pour in the **broth**. Cook 8–10 minutes, mashing any stubborn lumps against the side of the pan with a spoon or a potato masher, until it's completely smooth and thick enough to hold the line a whisk drags through it.

3. Off the heat, beat in the **olive oil**, then the **Parmigiano-Reggiano**, then plenty of **black pepper**. Now taste for **salt** — hold back, since the tube comes salted. Cool uncovered for 20 minutes before storing; it will set firm in the fridge, which is what you want.

### Day Of:

4. Scrape the cold polenta into a saucepan with ¾ cup of the **reserved broth**. Set over medium-low and whisk, breaking up the set blocks, for 8–10 minutes until loose, glossy, and creamy — add the rest a splash at a time if it's still stiff. Beat in the **butter** and re-season with **salt** and **pepper**.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Grate the tube on the large holes of a box grater instead of slicing or crumbling it. Shreds melt into the pot in about two minutes; chunks turn into rubbery lumps you spend the whole time chasing with a masher.

---

*Using hominy grits? They're nixtamalized, so the corn flavor leans slightly more tortilla than polenta. It's good here — just finish with an extra tablespoon of parmesan to keep the savory side forward.

---

**To Store:** Airtight container in the refrigerator up to 5 days.

**Used in:** Meal 3
