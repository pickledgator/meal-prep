# Summer Minestrone Base

_Makes about 9 cups_

The soffritto, beans, and broth get built on Sunday. The summer vegetables stay out until Meal 2 night so they arrive at the table bright green instead of olive-drab.

## Ingredients

- 3 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- 1 Yellow Onion, ¼-inch dice (from prep)
- 2 Carrots, ¼-inch dice (from prep)
- 2 Celery stalks, ¼-inch dice (from prep)
- 1½ tsp Kosher Salt, plus more _to taste_
- 4 cloves Garlic, thinly sliced (from prep)
- 1 tsp Dried Oregano
- ½ tsp Red Pepper Flakes
- 2 Tbsp Tomato Paste
- 1 pint (10 oz / 283 g) Cherry Tomatoes, halved (from prep)
- 2 (15.5 oz / 439 g) cans Cannellini Beans, drained and rinsed
- 6 cups (1.4 L) Rotisserie Chicken Broth — `components/rotisserie-chicken-broth.md`
- 1 Parmesan Rind (from prep)
- 1 Bay Leaf

## Instructions

1. Heat the **olive oil** in a large Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the **onion**, **carrots**, **celery**, and 1 tsp of the **salt**. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the onion is translucent and the carrots have softened at the edges, 8–10 minutes. Don't rush this over high heat — you want sweet, not browned.

2. Add the **garlic**, **oregano**, and **red pepper flakes**. Cook until fragrant, about 1 minute.

3. Add the **tomato paste** and stir constantly for 2 minutes, until it darkens from bright red to brick and starts to stick to the pot. This is where the savory backbone comes from.

4. Add the **cherry tomatoes** and cook, pressing a few against the pot with a spoon, until they collapse and release their juice, about 5 minutes.

5. Add the **cannellini beans**, **broth**, **Parmesan rind**, **bay leaf**, and remaining ½ tsp **salt**. Bring to a simmer and cook, uncovered, for 20 minutes.

6. Taste and adjust **salt**. Press about ½ cup of the beans against the side of the pot with a spoon and stir them back in — they'll thicken the broth to a silky body without any cream. Cool, leaving the rind and bay leaf in.

---

> **Hot Tip:** The parmesan rind is doing real work here — it dissolves slowly into glutamates that make the broth taste like it simmered all day. Leave it in through storage and fish it out on soup night.

---

**To Store:** Airtight container in the refrigerator up to 4 days, or freeze up to 3 months. It thickens considerably when chilled; loosen with water on the night.

**Used in:** Meal 2
