# Garlic Sourdough Croutons

_Makes about 4½ cups_

## Ingredients

- 8 oz (227 g) Sourdough, cut into 1-inch cubes (from prep)
- ¼ cup (60 ml) Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- 2 cloves Garlic, finely grated (from prep)
- ½ tsp Kosher Salt
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_
- 2 Tbsp (10 g) finely grated Parmigiano-Reggiano (from prep)

## Instructions

1. In a large bowl, whisk together the **olive oil**, **garlic**, **salt**, and **pepper**.

2. Add the **sourdough cubes** and toss with your hands for a solid 30 seconds, squeezing gently so the bread drinks up the oil evenly rather than leaving one soaked cube next to five dry ones.

3. Spread in a single layer on a rimmed sheet pan. Bake at 400°F for 15–18 minutes, tossing once at the halfway mark, until deeply golden and crisp at the edges but still slightly chewy at the center of the largest cubes.

4. Tip the hot croutons back into the bowl, add the **Parmigiano-Reggiano**, and toss so it melts into the surface. Cool completely before storing.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Add the cheese _after_ baking, not before — parmesan scorches and turns bitter at 400°F, but it clings perfectly to hot bread straight out of the oven.

---

**To Store:** Airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days. Don't refrigerate — they go soft. If they lose their snap, 4 minutes in a 350°F oven brings them back.
