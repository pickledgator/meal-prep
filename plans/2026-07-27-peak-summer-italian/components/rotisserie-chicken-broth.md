# Rotisserie Chicken Broth

_Makes about 8 cups_

The carcasses have already been roasted, so this builds real depth in an hour instead of four.

## Ingredients

- 2 Rotisserie Chicken carcasses, broken into large pieces, plus any skin and wing tips (from prep)
- 1 Yellow Onion, quartered (from prep)
- 1 Carrot, cut into 2-inch pieces (from prep)
- 2 Celery stalks, cut into 2-inch pieces (from prep)
- 4 cloves Garlic, smashed (from prep)
- 3 sprigs Rosemary (from prep)
- 1 Bay Leaf
- 1 tsp Black Peppercorns
- 10 cups (2.4 L) cold Water
- 1 tsp Kosher Salt, plus more _to taste_

## Instructions

1. Pile the **carcass pieces** into a large stockpot with the **onion**, **carrot**, **celery**, **garlic**, **rosemary**, **bay leaf**, and **peppercorns**. Pour in the **water** — the bones should be just covered.

2. Bring to a bare simmer over medium-high heat, then drop the heat to low so only a few lazy bubbles break the surface. Skim off any gray foam that rises in the first few minutes. Simmer uncovered for 60 minutes.

3. Strain through a fine-mesh sieve into a large bowl or storage container, pressing gently on the solids to release the liquid; discard the solids. Stir in the **salt** and taste — it should taste like good chicken soup, not like water.

4. Blot the surface with a paper towel to lift off surface fat (or chill and lift the fat cap off in one piece). Measure out **6 cups** for the Minestrone Base and **1 cup** for the Parmesan Polenta you cook next, then jar the last **1 cup** for loosening that polenta on Meal 3 night. Any extra beyond 8 cups is a bonus — save it for thinning the soup or reheating leftovers.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Never let it boil hard. A rolling boil emulsifies the fat and turns the broth cloudy and greasy — a bare simmer keeps it clean and golden.

---

**To Store:** Refrigerate in an airtight container up to 4 days, or freeze up to 3 months. It will set to a light jelly when cold — that's collagen, and it's a good sign.

**Used in:** Meal 2 (the soup itself), Meal 3 (loosening the polenta)
