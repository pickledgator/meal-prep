# Caesar Dressing

_Makes about 1¼ cups_

No raw egg, no blender — mayonnaise does the emulsifying so this comes together in a bowl in five minutes.

## Ingredients

- 3 Anchovy Fillets, drained (or 1½ tsp Worcestershire Sauce)
- 2 cloves Garlic, finely grated (from prep)
- ¼ tsp Kosher Salt, plus more _to taste_
- ⅓ cup (75 g) Mayonnaise
- 2 tsp Dijon Mustard
- 2 Tbsp fresh Lemon Juice (from prep)
- ½ cup (40 g) finely grated Parmigiano-Reggiano (from prep)
- 3 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- 2–3 Tbsp cold Water
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

## Instructions

1. On a cutting board, pile the **anchovy fillets** with the **garlic** and the **salt**. Drag the flat of your knife across the pile repeatedly until it becomes a smooth gray-green paste — the salt is doing the grinding for you. Scrape into a medium bowl.

2. Whisk in the **mayonnaise**, **Dijon**, and **lemon juice** until completely smooth.

3. Whisk in the **Parmigiano-Reggiano**, then stream in the **olive oil** while whisking.

4. Whisk in the **cold water** 1 tablespoon at a time until the dressing pours in a thick ribbon and coats a spoon without gluing to it. Finish with a generous amount of **black pepper** and more **salt** to taste — it should be assertively garlicky and sharp with lemon, since lettuce will mute it.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Taste it on a leaf of lettuce, not off a spoon. Straight from the spoon it will always seem too strong; on a leaf it's usually still under-seasoned.

---

**To Store:** Airtight jar in the refrigerator for up to 5 days. It thickens as it chills — loosen with a teaspoon of water before using.
