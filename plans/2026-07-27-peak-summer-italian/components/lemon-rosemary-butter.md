# Lemon-Rosemary Butter

_Makes about ⅓ cup (5 coins)_

## Ingredients

- 4 Tbsp (57 g) Salted Butter, softened at room temperature
- 1 tsp finely grated Lemon Zest (from prep)
- 1 tsp fresh Lemon Juice (from prep)
- 1 clove Garlic, finely grated (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp finely minced Rosemary (from prep)
- ¼ tsp Flaky Salt
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

## Instructions

1. In a small bowl, mash the **softened butter** with a fork until smooth and spreadable.

2. Add the **lemon zest**, **lemon juice**, **garlic**, **rosemary**, **flaky salt**, and a generous grind of **black pepper**. Mash and stir until evenly streaked through.

3. Scrape the butter onto a piece of parchment or plastic wrap. Fold the paper over and roll into a log about 1 inch thick, twisting the ends closed like a candy wrapper. Refrigerate until firm, at least 1 hour.

4. Slice into 5 equal coins just before serving.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Mince the rosemary much finer than feels necessary. Long needles read as woody in a cold butter; a fine mince perfumes the whole coin instead.

---

**To Store:** Wrapped log in the refrigerator up to 1 week, or freeze up to 2 months (slice coins straight off the frozen log).

**Used in:** Meal 3
