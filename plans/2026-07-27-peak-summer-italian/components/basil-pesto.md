# Basil Pesto

_Makes about ¾ cup (12 Tbsp)_

## Ingredients

- 3 Tbsp (25 g) Pine Nuts
- 2 cloves Garlic (from prep)
- 2 packed cups (2 oz / 56 g) Basil leaves, washed and thoroughly dried (from prep)
- ½ tsp Kosher Salt, plus more _to taste_
- ⅓ cup (80 ml) Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- ½ cup (40 g) finely grated Parmigiano-Reggiano (from prep)

## Instructions

1. Toast the **pine nuts** in a dry skillet over medium heat, shaking often, until they smell nutty and are blond-gold in patches, 3–4 minutes. Tip them onto a plate immediately — they go from golden to burnt in under a minute. Cool.

2. In a food processor, pulse the **garlic** and cooled pine nuts until finely chopped, about 8 short pulses.

3. Add the **basil** and **salt**. Pulse 10–12 times, scraping the sides once, until the basil is chopped but not pureed.

4. With the processor running, stream in the **olive oil** until you have a loose, spoonable paste.

5. Scrape into a bowl and stir in the **Parmigiano-Reggiano** by hand. Taste and adjust **salt**.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Stir the cheese in by hand rather than blitzing it. Processing parmesan into the oil makes pesto gummy and dulls the green.

---

**To Store:** Pack into a small jar, smooth the top, and pour a thin film of olive oil over the surface to seal out air — this keeps it bright green rather than brown. Refrigerate up to 5 days, or freeze in an ice cube tray.

**Used in:** Meals 2, 3
