# Sunday Prep List

_Difficulty: Normal — about 70 minutes of active work inside a 2-hour window. The broth simmers 60 minutes; the polenta wants 20 with quick grits or 45 with stone-ground, both while you're doing something else._

---

## Set Up

☐ **Preheat Oven to 400°F**

☐ **Set out a large stockpot, a heavy saucepan, and two sheet pans**

---

## Prep for Long-Running Tasks (⏱ 20 minutes)

- ☐ **Rotisserie Chickens — 2 (2½–3 lb / 1.1–1.4 kg each; about 2 lb / 907 g picked meat)**
  - 20 oz / 567 g meat → pull into large bite-size pieces, skin discarded → "M1 — Caesar chicken"¹
  - 12 oz / 340 g meat → shred → "M2 — soup chicken"²
  - both carcasses, plus skin and wing tips → break into large pieces → Chicken Broth 🫙
- ☐ **Yellow Onions — 2 medium**
  - 1 → peel; quarter → Chicken Broth 🫙
  - 1 → peel; ¼-inch dice → Minestrone Base 🫙
- ☐ **Carrots — 3 medium / 8 oz / 227 g**
  - 1 → scrub; cut into 2-inch pieces → Chicken Broth 🫙
  - 2 → peel; ¼-inch dice → Minestrone Base 🫙
- ☐ **Celery — 4 stalks**
  - 2 → cut into 2-inch pieces → Chicken Broth 🫙
  - 2 → ¼-inch dice → Minestrone Base 🫙
- ☐ **Garlic — 2 heads / 16 cloves**
  - 4 → peel; smash → Chicken Broth 🫙
  - 4 → peel; thinly slice → Minestrone Base 🫙
  - 2 → peel; finely grate → Caesar Dressing 🫙
  - 2 → peel; finely grate → Garlic Sourdough Croutons 🫙
  - 2 → peel → Basil Pesto 🫙
  - 1 → peel; finely grate → Lemon-Rosemary Butter 🫙
  - 1 → leave whole and unpeeled → "M2 — garlic bread"²
- ☐ **Fresh Rosemary — 1 small bunch / 5 sprigs**
  - 3 → leave whole → Chicken Broth 🫙
  - 2 → strip needles; mince very finely (1 Tbsp) → Lemon-Rosemary Butter 🫙
- ☐ **Sourdough Loaf — 1 round / 1 lb / 454 g**
  - ½ loaf / 8 oz / 227 g → cut into 1-inch cubes → Garlic Sourdough Croutons 🫙
  - ½ loaf / 8 oz / 227 g → cut into 5 thick (¾-inch) slices → "M2 — garlic bread"²
- ☐ **Parmigiano-Reggiano — 10 oz / 283 g wedge**
  - rind → trim off in one piece → Minestrone Base 🫙
  - ½ cup / 40 g → finely grate → Caesar Dressing 🫙
  - ½ cup / 40 g → finely grate → Basil Pesto 🫙
  - ¾ cup / 60 g → finely grate → Parmesan Polenta 🫙
  - 2 Tbsp / 10 g → finely grate → Garlic Sourdough Croutons 🫙
  - ¼ cup / 20 g → finely grate → "M2 — soup topping"²
  - remaining wedge (~1½ oz / 40 g) → leave in a wedge; shave day-of → "M1 — shaved parmesan"¹

## Prep Remaining Ingredients (⏱ 20 minutes)

- ☐ **Brussels Sprouts — 1½ lb / 680 g**
  - 8 oz / 227 g → trim; halve; shave paper-thin (about 4 packed cups) → "M1 — shaved brussels"¹
  - 1 lb / 454 g → trim; halve through the root → "M3 — roasting vegetables"³
- ☐ **Broccoli — 2 large crowns / 2 lb / 907 g**
  - 8 oz / 227 g → cut into small ½-inch florets → "M2 — soup vegetables"²
  - 1 lb / 454 g → cut into large 1½-inch florets with a flat cut side → "M3 — roasting vegetables"³
- ☐ **Zucchini — 1 large / 12 oz / 340 g**
  - all → trim; ½-inch dice (about 2½ cups) → "M2 — soup vegetables"²
- ☐ **Cherry Tomatoes — 1 pint / 10 oz / 283 g**
  - all → halve → Minestrone Base 🫙
- ☐ **Little Gem Lettuce — 5 heads / 1¼ lb / 567 g**
  - all → trim the root; separate into whole leaves; wash; spin completely dry → "M1 — lettuce"¹
- ☐ **Lemons — 5**
  - 1 → juice (2 Tbsp) → Caesar Dressing 🫙
  - 1 → finely zest (1 tsp); juice (1 tsp) → Lemon-Rosemary Butter 🫙
  - 1 → juice (3 Tbsp) → "M2 — soup finish"²
  - 1 → cut into 5 wedges → "M3 — vegetables"³
  - 1 → cut into 5 wedges → "M1 — serving"¹
- ☐ **Fresh Basil — 2 bunches / 3 oz / 85 g**
  - 2 packed cups leaves / 2 oz / 56 g → pick; wash; spin completely dry → Basil Pesto 🫙
  - remainder → stand the stems in a glass of water on the counter → "M2/M3 — torn basil"²³
- ☐ **Heirloom Tomatoes — 2 medium / 1 lb / 454 g**
  - both → leave whole on the counter; slice day-of → "M3 — tomato side"³
- ☐ **Prepared Polenta — 1 tube / 18 oz / 510 g**
  - all → coarsely grate on the large holes of a box grater → Parmesan Polenta 🫙
- ☐ **Top Sirloin Steaks — 2 lb / 907 g / 2 steaks**
  - both → pat dry; season all over with 2 tsp kosher salt; set on a rack over a plate, uncovered in the fridge → "M3 — steaks"³
