# Slow Cooker Chipotle-Lime Chicken

_Makes ~2 cups shredded chicken_

## Ingredients

- 3 Bone-In, Skin-On Chicken Thighs (~1.5 lbs / 680 g)
- 2-3 Chipotle Peppers in Adobo, roughly chopped, plus 1 Tbsp adobo sauce
- 3 cloves Garlic, smashed
- Juice of 1 Lime (~2 Tbsp)
- ½ cup (120 ml) Chicken Stock
- 1 tsp Ground Cumin
- ½ tsp Dried Oregano
- ½ tsp Smoked Paprika
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

## Instructions

1. Pat **chicken thighs** dry with paper towels. Season both sides with **salt**, **pepper**, **cumin**, **oregano**, and **smoked paprika**.

2. Place the **garlic** and chopped **chipotle peppers** with **adobo sauce** in the bottom of the slow cooker. Arrange the seasoned chicken thighs on top, skin-side up, in a single layer.

3. Pour the **chicken stock** and **lime juice** around (not over) the chicken.

4. Cover and cook on LOW for 5-6 hours or HIGH for 3-4 hours, until the chicken is fall-apart tender.

5. Remove the thighs, discard the skin and bones. Using two forks, shred the chicken and return it to the braising liquid. Stir to coat — the chicken will soak up the smoky, spicy juices as it sits.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Start with 2 chipotles for medium heat. Taste the braising liquid after cooking — if you want more kick, stir in another chopped chipotle. The remaining chipotles from the can freeze beautifully: spread individual peppers on parchment, freeze, then transfer to a bag.

**To Store:** Keep shredded chicken in braising liquid in an airtight container. Refrigerate up to 4 days.
