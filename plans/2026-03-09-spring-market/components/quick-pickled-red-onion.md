# Quick-Pickled Red Onion

_Makes about ½ cup_

## Ingredients

- ½ large Red Onion, peeled and thinly sliced into rings
- ¼ cup (60 ml) Rice Vinegar or Red Wine Vinegar
- 1 tsp Sugar
- 1 tsp Honey
- ½ tsp Kosher Salt

## Instructions

1. Place the sliced **red onion** in a small jar or heatproof bowl.

2. In a small saucepan or microwave-safe cup, warm the **vinegar** with the **sugar**, **honey**, and **salt**, stirring until dissolved. About 30 seconds in the microwave or 1 minute on the stove — no need to boil.

3. Pour the warm brine over the onion rings. Press down so they're mostly submerged. Let sit at room temperature for at least 30 minutes, then cover and refrigerate.

---

**To Store:** Keep in brine in a sealed jar. Refrigerate up to 2 weeks. They get better over time.

**Used in:** Meals 1, 2
