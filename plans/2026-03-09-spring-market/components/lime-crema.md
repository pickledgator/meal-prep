# Lime Crema

_Makes about ⅓ cup_

## Ingredients

- ¼ cup (60 g) Plain Greek Yogurt
- Juice of ½ Lime (~1 Tbsp)
- 2 Tbsp finely chopped Cilantro
- Pinch of Kosher Salt

## Instructions

1. In a small bowl, stir together the **Greek yogurt**, **lime juice**, **cilantro**, and **salt** until smooth.

2. Taste and adjust — it should be tangy and bright with a little herby punch from the cilantro.

---

**To Store:** Cover and refrigerate up to 5 days.

**Used in:** Meal 1 (~2 Tbsp per serving, drizzled over tacos)
