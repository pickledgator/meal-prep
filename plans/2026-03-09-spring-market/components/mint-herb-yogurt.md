# Mint-Herb Yogurt

_Makes about ½ cup_

## Ingredients

- ½ cup (120 g) Plain Greek Yogurt
- 3 Tbsp finely chopped Fresh Mint
- 1 Tbsp fresh Lemon Juice
- 1 small clove Garlic, finely grated
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_

## Instructions

1. In a small bowl, stir together the **Greek yogurt**, **mint**, **lemon juice**, and **garlic**.

2. Season with **salt** to taste. The yogurt should be cool, bright, and distinctly minty — a counterpoint to the warm-spiced kofta.

---

**To Store:** Cover and refrigerate up to 5 days. The mint flavor will deepen as it sits.

**Used in:** Meal 2 (~3-4 Tbsp per serving, dolloped alongside kofta)
