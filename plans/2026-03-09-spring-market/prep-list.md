# Sunday Prep List

_Difficulty: Normal_

---

☐ **Start Slow Cooker Chicken** — see `components/slow-cooker-chipotle-lime-chicken.md`¹
Season and load chicken thighs into slow cooker with chipotle, lime, garlic, cumin, and stock. Set on LOW for 5-6 hours. (⏱ 10 minutes active, 5-6 hours passive)

☐ **Cook Rice** (¾ cup / 150 g dry; rinse, then simmer in 1¼ cups water, ~18 minutes)²
Bring to a boil, reduce to low, cover. Rest 5 minutes off heat, then fluff. Cool and store. (yield: ~2¼ cups cooked)

---

## Prep Produce (⏱ 15 minutes)

- ☐ Red Onion (½ onion; peel & thinly slice into rings for pickling¹ | ¼ onion; small dice for salad² | 2 Tbsp; grate for kofta²)🫙
- ☐ Garlic (3 cloves; smash for crockpot¹ | 2 cloves; mince for kofta² | 1 small clove; grate for yogurt²)🫙
- ☐ Green Cabbage (¼ small head / ~6 oz; thinly shred for slaw)¹
- ☐ Cilantro (1 bunch; wash, dry, pick leaves — reserve 2 Tbsp chopped for crema, remainder for taco garnish)¹🫙
- ☐ Flat-Leaf Parsley (1 bunch; wash, dry, pick — reserve 3 Tbsp chopped for kofta², remainder for rice² and garnish³)🫙
- ☐ Fresh Mint (pick leaves — reserve 3 Tbsp chopped for yogurt², 2 Tbsp for salad², 6-8 leaves for pea puree³)🫙

---

_— CLEANING BREAK —_

---

## Prepare Components (⏱ 20 minutes)

☐ **Quick-Pickled Red Onion** — see `components/quick-pickled-red-onion.md`¹²
Combine sliced red onion with warm vinegar brine. (yield: ~½ cup)

☐ **Lime Crema** — see `components/lime-crema.md`¹
Stir together yogurt, lime juice, cilantro, and salt. (yield: ~⅓ cup)

☐ **Mint-Herb Yogurt** — see `components/mint-herb-yogurt.md`²
Combine yogurt with mint, lemon juice, and garlic. (yield: ~½ cup)

☐ **Mix & Shape Lamb Kofta**²
In a bowl, combine **ground lamb** (½ lb) with minced garlic (2 cloves), grated red onion (2 Tbsp), chopped parsley (2 Tbsp), cumin (1 tsp), coriander (½ tsp), salt (½ tsp), and pepper. Mix gently, then shape into 4-6 small oval patties (~¾" thick). Place on a plate, cover, and refrigerate.

---

## When Chicken Is Done

☐ **Shred Crockpot Chicken**¹
Remove thighs from slow cooker, discard skin and bones. Shred with two forks and return to the braising liquid. Store together — the chicken will absorb more flavor as it sits.

---

¹ = Chipotle-Lime Chicken Tacos (M1)

🫙 = used in a prepped component (keep out during prep)
⏱ = estimated time
