# Slow Cooker Chipotle-Lime Chicken Tacos

_with Cabbage Slaw & Lime Crema_

_Makes 2 servings_

## Ingredients

### Tacos

- ~1½ cups Shredded Chipotle-Lime Chicken, with braising liquid (from prep)
- 8-12 small Corn Tortillas

### Cabbage-Lime Slaw

- ~1½ cups (a few generous handfuls) shredded Green Cabbage (from prep)
- Juice of ½ Lime (~1 Tbsp)
- 1 tsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- Pinch of Kosher Salt

### Toppings

- 3-4 Tbsp Quick-Pickled Red Onion (from prep)
- 1 Avocado, sliced
- 2 oz (57 g) Cotija Cheese, crumbled
- ~3 Tbsp Lime Crema (from prep)
- Cilantro leaves, _for garnish_
- Lime wedges, _for serving_

---

## Instructions

### Day Of:

**Warm the Chicken**

1. Transfer the **shredded chicken** with a few spoonfuls of braising liquid to a small saucepan. Warm over medium-low heat for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until heated through. Alternatively, microwave covered for 2-3 minutes.

**Make the Slaw**

2. Toss the shredded **cabbage** with **lime juice**, **olive oil**, and a pinch of **salt**. Let it sit while you warm the tortillas — the lime will gently soften the cabbage.

**Warm the Tortillas**

3. Heat **corn tortillas** directly over a gas flame for 15-20 seconds per side until charred in spots and pliable. No gas stove? Warm in a dry skillet over high heat, 30 seconds per side. Stack in a clean towel to stay warm.

### Plate & Serve

4. Double-stack the tortillas (two per taco for structure). Spoon a generous mound of **chipotle chicken** into each. Top with **cabbage slaw**, **pickled red onion**, and slices of **avocado**. Crumble **cotija** over the top, drizzle with **lime crema**, and scatter with **cilantro** leaves. Serve with **lime wedges** for squeezing.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Double-stacking corn tortillas isn't just traditional — it prevents taco blowouts. The inner tortilla absorbs the juices while the outer one holds everything together.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** A cold Mexican lager or a bright Paloma cocktail (grapefruit, lime, tequila) is the move here.
