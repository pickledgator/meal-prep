# Lamb Kofta with Mint Yogurt

_with Herbed Rice & Cucumber-Tomato Salad_

_Makes 2 servings_

## Ingredients

### Lamb Kofta

- 4-6 Lamb Kofta patties (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp Neutral Oil, _for cooking_

### Herbed Rice

- ~1½ cups cooked Long-Grain White Rice (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp chopped Flat-Leaf Parsley
- 1 tsp Lemon Zest
- 1 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- Pinch of Kosher Salt

### Cucumber-Tomato Salad

- 1 Persian Cucumber, diced
- 2 Roma Tomatoes, diced
- 2 Tbsp diced Red Onion (from remaining ¼ onion)
- 2 Tbsp chopped Fresh Mint
- 1 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- 1 Tbsp fresh Lemon Juice
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_

### To Finish

- ~½ cup Mint-Herb Yogurt (from prep)
- 2-3 Tbsp Quick-Pickled Red Onion (from prep)
- Flaky Salt, _for finishing_

---

## Instructions

### Day Of:

**Sear the Kofta**

1. Heat **neutral oil** in a skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Place the **kofta patties** in the pan and cook undisturbed for 3-4 minutes until a dark, caramelized crust forms. Flip and cook another 3-4 minutes until cooked through (internal temp 160°F). Transfer to a plate.

**Make the Herbed Rice**

2. Reheat the **rice** in the microwave with a splash of water (2 minutes, covered) or in a small pot over low heat. Stir in the **parsley**, **lemon zest**, **olive oil**, and a pinch of **salt**.

**Toss the Salad**

3. Combine the **cucumber**, **tomatoes**, **red onion**, and **mint** in a bowl. Dress with **olive oil**, **lemon juice**, and **salt**. Toss gently.

### Plate & Serve

4. Spoon the herbed rice onto plates. Arrange the **kofta** alongside — 2-3 per plate. Dollop the **mint-herb yogurt** generously next to the kofta. Pile the **cucumber-tomato salad** on the other side. Top the kofta with a few **pickled red onion** rings and a sprinkle of **flaky salt**.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Don't overwork the lamb when mixing — handle it like you would a burger. Overmixing makes dense, tough kofta. Mix just until the spices are incorporated, then shape gently.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** A dry Lebanese rosé or a glass of Côtes du Rhône pairs beautifully with the warm spices.
