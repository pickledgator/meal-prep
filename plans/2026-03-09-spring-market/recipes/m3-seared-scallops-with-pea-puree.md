# Seared Scallops with Spring Pea Puree

_with Sautéed Snap Peas & Sourdough_

_Makes 2 servings_

---

## Prepped Ingredients

> From your Sunday prep:
>
> - Fresh mint leaves (from prep list)
> - Parsley (from prep list)

---

## Ingredients

### Spring Pea Puree

- 1 cup (150 g) Frozen English Peas
- 1 small Shallot, diced
- 1 small clove Garlic, minced
- 6-8 Fresh Mint leaves
- 1 Tbsp fresh Lemon Juice
- 2 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- 2 Tbsp Water
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_

### Seared Scallops

- 6-8 large Dry-Packed Sea Scallops (~¾ lb / 340 g)
- 1 Tbsp Neutral Oil (avocado or grapeseed), _for cooking_
- 1 Tbsp Salted Butter
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

### Sautéed Sugar Snap Peas

- 6 oz (170 g) Sugar Snap Peas, strings removed
- 1 tsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- Pinch of Kosher Salt

### To Finish

- 1 Tbsp chopped Flat-Leaf Parsley, _for garnish_
- Flaky Salt, _for finishing_
- Squeeze of Lemon
- Crusty Sourdough, sliced and toasted, _for serving_

---

## Instructions

### Day Of:

**Make the Pea Puree**

1. Heat 1 Tbsp **olive oil** in a small saucepan over medium heat. Add the **shallot** and cook 2 minutes until softened. Add the **garlic** and cook 30 seconds. Add the **frozen peas** and **water**, cover, and cook 3 minutes until peas are bright green and tender.

2. Transfer to a blender (or use an immersion blender). Add the **mint leaves**, **lemon juice**, remaining 1 Tbsp **olive oil**, and a good pinch of **salt**. Blend until smooth. Taste and adjust seasoning. Keep warm.

**Sear the Scallops**

3. Remove **scallops** from the fridge 10 minutes early. Pat each scallop thoroughly dry with paper towels — this is the most important step. Season generously with **salt** and **pepper**.

4. Heat **neutral oil** in a large skillet over high heat until it just begins to smoke. Place scallops in the pan clockwise, leaving space between each. Cook undisturbed for 2-3 minutes until a deep golden-brown crust forms. Flip each scallop (same clockwise order — first in, first flipped). Add the **butter** to the pan and cook 1-2 minutes more, spooning the foaming butter over the scallops. Remove immediately.

**Sauté the Snap Peas**

5. Wipe the pan, return to high heat with **olive oil**. Add the **snap peas** and cook 2-3 minutes, tossing occasionally, until bright green with a few charred spots. Season with **salt**.

### Plate & Serve

6. Spoon a generous pool of **pea puree** into the center of each plate — use the back of the spoon to create a shallow well. Arrange 3-4 **scallops** on top of the puree, seared-side up to show off the crust. Scatter the **snap peas** around the plate. Finish with **parsley**, a squeeze of **lemon**, and a pinch of **flaky salt**. Serve with warm **sourdough** on the side for swiping through the puree.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Dry scallops are everything. If your scallops release a pool of milky liquid when you start cooking, they're "wet" (treated with sodium tripolyphosphate). Dry-packed scallops sear properly — look for them at the fish counter, not in the frozen aisle. Pat them obsessively dry.
