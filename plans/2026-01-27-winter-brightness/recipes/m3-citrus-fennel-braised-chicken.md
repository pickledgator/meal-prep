# Citrus-Fennel Braised Chicken

_with Gremolata & Roasted Carrots_

_Makes 2 servings_

## Ingredients

### For the Braised Chicken

- 4 Bone-In, Skin-On Chicken Thighs (about 1½ lbs / 680 g total)
- 1 Tbsp Olive Oil
- 1 medium Fennel Bulb, halved and sliced ¼-inch thick (fronds reserved)
- 1 Blood Orange, sliced into ¼-inch rounds
- ½ cup (80 g) Castelvetrano Olives, lightly crushed
- 2 cloves Garlic, smashed
- 1 cup (240 ml) Chicken Stock
- 1 tsp fresh Thyme leaves (or ½ tsp dried)
- Kosher Salt
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper

### For the Roasted Carrots

- 4 medium Carrots (about 12 oz / 340 g), peeled and cut into 3-inch pieces on a diagonal
- 1 Tbsp Olive Oil
- ½ tsp Cumin
- Kosher Salt

### For Serving

- 1½ cups Sesame Rice, reheated (from prep)
- 2 Tbsp Gremolata (from prep)
- 1-2 Tbsp Citrus-Ginger Vinaigrette (from prep)
- Reserved Fennel Fronds, _for garnish_

---

## Instructions

### On Prep Day:

**Prep the Vegetables**

1. Halve the **fennel** bulb through the root, remove the core, and slice into ¼-inch wedges. Reserve the fronds in a damp paper towel. Store fennel in an airtight container.

2. Peel the **carrots** and cut on a diagonal into 3-inch pieces. Store in an airtight container.

3. Slice the **blood orange** into ¼-inch rounds. Store in an airtight container, separated by parchment if stacking.

### Day Of:

**Sear the Chicken**

1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Pat the **chicken thighs** very dry with paper towels and season generously with **salt** and **pepper** on both sides.

2. Heat **olive oil** in a large oven-safe skillet or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add the chicken thighs skin-side down. Sear undisturbed for 6-8 minutes until the skin is deeply golden and crispy. Flip and sear the other side for 2 minutes. Transfer to a plate.

**Start the Roasted Carrots**

3. While chicken sears, toss the **carrots** with **olive oil**, **cumin**, and a pinch of **salt** on a sheet pan. Spread in a single layer. Place in the oven and roast for 30-35 minutes, flipping halfway, until caramelized and tender.

**Build the Braise**

4. Reduce heat to medium. Add the **sliced fennel** and **smashed garlic** to the skillet with the chicken drippings. Season with a pinch of salt. Cook for 3-4 minutes, stirring occasionally, until fennel begins to soften.

5. Nestle the **blood orange slices** and **olives** among the fennel. Pour in the **chicken stock** and add the **thyme**. Bring to a simmer.

6. Return the chicken thighs to the skillet, skin-side up, nestling them into the fennel and citrus. The liquid should come about halfway up the chicken (skin should stay above the liquid to stay crispy).

**Braise**

7. Transfer to the oven, uncovered. Braise for 25-30 minutes until the chicken reaches 175°F internal temperature and the fennel is meltingly tender.

### Plate & Serve

1. Spoon **sesame rice** onto each plate, creating a bed. Arrange 2 **chicken thighs** over the rice, spooning the braised fennel, oranges, and olives around and over the chicken.

2. Place **roasted carrots** alongside. Drizzle the carrots with **citrus-ginger vinaigrette**.

3. Scatter **gremolata** over the chicken and finish with **fennel fronds**. Spoon a bit of the braising liquid over everything.

---

> **Hot Tip:** The secret to crispy-skinned braised chicken is keeping the skin above the braising liquid. Don't submerge the thighs—let the oven's dry heat crisp the skin while the liquid keeps the meat moist below.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** A medium-bodied Côtes du Rhône or an orange wine would complement the Mediterranean flavors beautifully.
