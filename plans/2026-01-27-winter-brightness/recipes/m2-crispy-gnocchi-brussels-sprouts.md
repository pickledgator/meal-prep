# Crispy Gnocchi with Brussels Sprouts

_in Brown Butter Sage_

_Makes 2 servings_

---

## Prepped Ingredients

> From your Sunday prep:
>
> - Brussels sprouts (halved and ready)
> - Gremolata — `components/gremolata.md`

---

## Ingredients

### For the Gnocchi & Brussels

- 1 lb (454 g) Potato Gnocchi (shelf-stable or refrigerated)
- 12 oz (340 g) Brussels Sprouts, halved (from prep)
- 4 Tbsp (57 g) Unsalted Butter
- 8-10 fresh Sage Leaves
- 2 cloves Garlic, thinly sliced
- Zest of ½ Lemon
- Kosher Salt
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper
- Pinch of Red Pepper Flakes (optional)

### For Serving

- ½ oz (15 g) Parmesan, freshly shaved
- 2 Tbsp Gremolata (from prep)
- Flaky Salt, _for finishing_

---

## Instructions

### On Prep Day:

**Prep the Brussels Sprouts**

1. Trim the stem ends of the **Brussels sprouts** and halve them through the root. Remove any yellowed outer leaves. Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator.

### Day Of:

**Cook the Brussels Sprouts**

1. Heat 1 Tbsp of the **butter** in a large (12-inch) skillet over medium-high heat. Once melted and foaming, add the **Brussels sprouts** cut-side down in a single layer. Season with **salt** and **pepper**.

2. Cook undisturbed for 4-5 minutes until deeply golden brown on the cut side. Stir and continue cooking for 2-3 more minutes until crisp-tender. Transfer to a plate.

**Crisp the Gnocchi**

3. Add 1 Tbsp **butter** to the same skillet over medium-high heat. Add the **gnocchi** in a single layer (work in batches if needed). Cook undisturbed for 3-4 minutes until golden and crispy on the bottom. Flip and crisp the other side, about 2-3 more minutes. Transfer to the plate with Brussels sprouts.

**Make the Brown Butter**

4. Reduce heat to medium. Add the remaining 2 Tbsp **butter** to the skillet. Once melted, add the **sage leaves** and **sliced garlic**. Cook, swirling occasionally, until the butter foams, then turns golden brown and smells nutty, about 2-3 minutes. The sage will crisp and the garlic will turn golden.

5. Remove from heat immediately (brown butter can burn quickly). Add the **lemon zest** and a pinch of **red pepper flakes** if using.

**Bring It Together**

6. Return the gnocchi and Brussels sprouts to the skillet. Toss gently to coat everything in the brown butter. Taste and adjust seasoning with **salt** and **pepper**.

### Plate & Serve

1. Divide the gnocchi and Brussels sprouts between two warmed plates or shallow bowls, making sure to include the crispy sage leaves and golden garlic.

2. Shave **Parmesan** generously over the top. Scatter **gremolata** over each plate for brightness. Finish with a pinch of **flaky salt**.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Don't skip the undisturbed cooking time for both the Brussels sprouts and gnocchi. That's how you get the deeply caramelized, crispy exterior that makes this dish special. Resist the urge to stir!

---

**Serving Suggestion:** This rich, nutty dish pairs wonderfully with a crisp Vermentino or a light Beaujolais.
