# Miso-Glazed Cod

_with Sesame Rice & Roasted Broccolini_

_Makes 2 servings_

---

> From your Sunday prep:
>
> - Sesame Rice — `components/sesame-rice.md`
> - Citrus-Ginger Vinaigrette — `components/citrus-ginger-vinaigrette.md`

> - Broccolini (trimmed and ready to roast)

---

## Ingredients

### For the Miso Glaze

- 3 Tbsp White Miso Paste
- 1 Tbsp Honey
- 1 Tbsp Rice Vinegar
- 1 tsp Toasted Sesame Oil
- 1 tsp freshly grated Ginger
- 1 clove Garlic, finely grated

### For the Cod

- 2 (6 oz / 170 g) Cod Fillets, skin removed
- Neutral Oil, _for the pan_
- Kosher Salt
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper

### For the Roasted Broccolini

- 1 bunch Broccolini (6 oz / 170 g), trimmed
- 1 Tbsp Olive Oil
- 2 cloves Garlic, smashed
- Pinch of Red Pepper Flakes
- Kosher Salt

### For Serving

- 1½ cups Sesame Rice, reheated (from prep)
- 2-3 Tbsp Citrus-Ginger Vinaigrette (from prep)
- 1 tsp Sesame Seeds, _for garnish_

---

## Instructions

### On Prep Day:

**Make the Miso Glaze**

1. In a small bowl, whisk together the **miso paste**, **honey**, **rice vinegar**, **sesame oil**, **ginger**, and **garlic** until smooth. Transfer to an airtight container and refrigerate.

**Prep the Broccolini**

2. Trim the tough ends from the **broccolini** (about 1 inch). If any stalks are thick, halve them lengthwise. Store in a produce bag in the refrigerator.

### Day Of:

**Roast the Broccolini**

1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Toss the **broccolini** with **olive oil**, **smashed garlic**, **red pepper flakes**, and a pinch of **salt** on a sheet pan. Spread in a single layer. Roast for 12-15 minutes until charred at the edges and tender.

**Cook the Cod**

2. While broccolini roasts, pat the **cod fillets** dry and season lightly with **salt** and **pepper**. Heat a drizzle of **neutral oil** in an oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat.

3. Sear the cod for 2-3 minutes until golden on the bottom. Flip the fillets, then brush the tops generously with the **miso glaze**.

4. Transfer the skillet to the oven (alongside the broccolini) and roast for 6-8 minutes until the cod flakes easily and the glaze is bubbling and caramelized. For extra caramelization, broil for 1-2 minutes at the end, watching carefully.

### Plate & Serve

1. Divide the **sesame rice** between two shallow bowls or plates, creating a bed in the center. Place the **miso-glazed cod** on top of the rice. Arrange the **roasted broccolini** alongside.

2. Drizzle each plate with **citrus-ginger vinaigrette**. Sprinkle with **sesame seeds** and serve immediately.

---

> **Hot Tip:** The key to perfect miso-glazed fish is high heat at the end. The sugars in the miso and honey need that blast of heat to caramelize without burning. Watch it closely under the broiler—it can go from perfect to burnt quickly.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** Pair with a chilled glass of dry Riesling or Grüner Veltliner—the slight sweetness complements the miso glaze beautifully.
