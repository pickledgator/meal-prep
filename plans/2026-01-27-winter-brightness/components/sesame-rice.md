# Sesame Rice

_Makes about 3 cups cooked_

## Ingredients

- 1 cup Jasmine Rice
- 1½ cups Water
- ½ tsp Kosher Salt
- 1 Tbsp Rice Vinegar
- 1 tsp Toasted Sesame Oil
- 1 Tbsp Sesame Seeds, toasted

## Instructions

1. Rinse the **rice** in a fine-mesh strainer under cold water until the water runs clear, about 30 seconds.

2. Combine the rinsed rice, **water**, and **salt** in a medium saucepan. Bring to a boil over high heat, then reduce to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes.

3. Remove from heat and let steam, covered, for 5 minutes.

4. Fluff the rice with a fork. Drizzle with **rice vinegar** and **sesame oil**, then fold in the **toasted sesame seeds**. Toss gently to combine.

---

**To Store:** Let cool completely, then transfer to an airtight container. Refrigerate for up to 4 days. Reheat with a splash of water in the microwave or in a covered pan over low heat.

**Used in:** Meals 1, 3
