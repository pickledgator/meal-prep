# Citrus-Ginger Vinaigrette

_Makes about ½ cup_

## Ingredients

- 2 Tbsp fresh Blood Orange Juice (from about ½ orange)
- 1 Tbsp fresh Lemon Juice
- 1 Tbsp Rice Vinegar
- 1 tsp freshly grated Ginger
- 1 small clove Garlic, finely grated
- 1 tsp Honey
- ¼ cup Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

## Instructions

1. In a small bowl or jar, combine the **blood orange juice**, **lemon juice**, **rice vinegar**, **ginger**, **garlic**, and **honey**. Whisk to combine.

2. While whisking continuously, slowly drizzle in the **olive oil** until emulsified. Alternatively, add all ingredients to a jar with a tight-fitting lid and shake vigorously.

3. Season with **salt** and **pepper** to taste. The dressing should be bright and balanced—add more honey if too tart, more citrus if too mellow.

---

**To Store:** Transfer to an airtight container and refrigerate for up to 5 days. Shake or whisk before using as it will separate.

**Used in:** Meals 1, 3
