# Gremolata

_Makes about ¼ cup_

## Ingredients

- ¼ cup finely chopped Flat-Leaf Parsley (about ½ bunch, packed)
- Zest of 1 Lemon
- 2 cloves Garlic, minced
- 1 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- Pinch of Kosher Salt

## Instructions

1. Finely chop the **parsley** leaves, discarding the thick stems. You want a fine, even chop.

2. Combine the chopped parsley with the **lemon zest** and **minced garlic** in a small bowl.

3. Drizzle with **olive oil** and add a pinch of **salt**. Toss to combine.

4. For the brightest flavor, prepare within a few hours of serving. If making ahead, press plastic wrap directly onto the surface to prevent browning.

---

**To Store:** Cover tightly and refrigerate for up to 2 days. The color will dull slightly but flavor remains good.

**Used in:** Meals 2, 3
