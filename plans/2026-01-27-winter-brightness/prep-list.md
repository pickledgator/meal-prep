# Sunday Prep List

_Difficulty: Normal_

---

## Start Grains First (⏱ 5 minutes active, 20 minutes passive)

☐ **Cook Sesame Rice** — see `components/sesame-rice.md`¹³
Rinse 1 cup jasmine rice, combine with 1½ cups water and ½ tsp salt, bring to boil, reduce to low, cover and cook 15 min, then rest 5 min covered

---

## Prep Produce (⏱ 15-20 minutes)

While rice cooks, prep all vegetables:

- ☐ Garlic (10 cloves; peel and separate)¹²³🫙
  - 1 clove: finely grate¹ (for miso glaze)
  - 1 clove: finely grate¹ (for vinaigrette)
  - 2 cloves: mince² (for gremolata)
  - 2 cloves: smash¹ (for broccolini)
  - 2 cloves: thinly slice² (for gnocchi day-of)
  - 2 cloves: smash³ (for braise)
- ☐ Fresh Ginger (2-inch piece; peel)¹³🫙
  - 1 tsp: finely grate¹ (for miso glaze)
  - 1 tsp: finely grate¹ (for vinaigrette)
- ☐ Lemons (3)¹²³🫙
  - Lemon 1: zest, then juice² (zest for gremolata, juice reserve)
  - Lemon 2: zest half, juice whole¹ (zest for gnocchi day-of, juice for vinaigrette)
  - Lemon 3: juice only¹ (backup for balance)
- ☐ Blood Oranges (2)³🫙
  - Orange 1: juice half³ (for vinaigrette), slice other half into ¼" rounds³
  - Orange 2: slice into ¼" rounds³
- ☐ Fennel (1 bulb; halve, core, slice ¼" thick)³
  - Reserve fronds in damp paper towel for garnish
- ☐ Broccolini (1 bunch; trim tough ends, halve thick stalks)¹
- ☐ Brussels Sprouts (12 oz; trim stems, halve through root)²
- ☐ Carrots (4 medium; peel, cut on diagonal into 3" pieces)³
- ☐ Flat-Leaf Parsley (1 bunch; pick ½ bunch leaves, finely chop)²³🫙
  - ¼ cup chopped for gremolata

## Prepare Components (⏱ 15 minutes)

☐ **Finish Sesame Rice** — see `components/sesame-rice.md`¹³
Fluff rice, fold in 1 Tbsp rice vinegar, 1 tsp sesame oil, 1 Tbsp toasted sesame seeds
_(yield: ~3 cups)_

☐ **Make Miso Glaze** (M1)¹
Whisk 3 Tbsp white miso, 1 Tbsp honey, 1 Tbsp rice vinegar, 1 tsp sesame oil, 1 tsp grated ginger, 1 grated garlic clove
_(yield: ~¼ cup)_

☐ **Make Citrus-Ginger Vinaigrette** — see `components/citrus-ginger-vinaigrette.md`¹³
Combine 2 Tbsp blood orange juice, 1 Tbsp lemon juice, 1 Tbsp rice vinegar, 1 tsp ginger, 1 grated garlic clove, 1 tsp honey; whisk in ¼ cup olive oil
_(yield: ~½ cup)_

☐ **Make Gremolata** — see `components/gremolata.md`²³
Combine ¼ cup chopped parsley, zest of 1 lemon, 2 minced garlic cloves, 1 Tbsp olive oil, pinch of salt
_(yield: ~¼ cup)_
