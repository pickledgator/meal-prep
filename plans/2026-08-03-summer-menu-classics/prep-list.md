## Prep Aromatics & Produce (⏱ 25–30 minutes)

- ☐ **Garlic — 17 cloves / ~2 heads**
  - 3 → finely grate → Garlic-Ginger Soy Sauce 🫙
  - 1 → finely grate → Peanut-Lime Dressing 🫙
  - 4 → finely grate → Italian Herb Marinade 🫙
  - 4 → mince → "M3 — fried rice"³
  - 3 → mince → "M4 — garlic herb noodles"⁴
  - 2 → thinly slice → "M5 — pasta"⁵
- ☐ **Ginger — 1 (2-inch) piece**
  - 1½ Tbsp → peel; finely grate → Garlic-Ginger Soy Sauce 🫙
  - 1 tsp → peel; finely grate → Peanut-Lime Dressing 🫙
- ☐ **Limes — 3**
  - 2 → juice (~¼ cup / 60 ml) → Peanut-Lime Dressing 🫙
  - ½ → juice → Sriracha Mayo 🫙
  - ½ → cut into wedges → "M2 — serving"²
- ☐ **Lemons — 2**
  - 1 → finely zest; juice (~3 Tbsp) → Italian Herb Marinade 🫙
  - 1 → keep whole → "M4 + M5 — finishing squeeze"⁴⁵
- ☐ **Persian Cucumbers — 7 / ~1½ lb / 680 g**
  - 3 → slice into ⅛-inch rounds → Quick-Pickled Cucumbers 🫙
  - 4 → keep whole; refrigerate → "M2 — smashed cucumbers"²
- ☐ **Jalapeños — 2**
  - 1 → thinly slice → "M1 — bowls"¹
  - 1 → thinly slice → "M2 — noodle salad"²
- ☐ **Scallions — 1 bunch / 7**
  - 2 → thinly slice → "M1 — bowls"¹
  - 2 → thinly slice → "M2 — noodle salad"²
  - 3 → slice; separate whites and greens → "M3 — fried rice"³
- ☐ **Red Bell Peppers — 4**
  - 1 → ½-inch dice → "M3 — fried rice"³
  - 2 → cut into 1¼-inch squares → "M4 — kebabs"⁴
  - 1 → cut into thin strips → "M5 — pasta"⁵
- ☐ **Zucchini — 5 medium / ~2½ lb / 1.1 kg**
  - 1 → ½-inch dice → "M3 — fried rice"³
  - 2 → cut into ¾-inch-thick rounds; halve any wider than 2 inches → "M4 — kebabs"⁴
  - 2 → halve lengthwise; cut into ¼-inch half-moons → "M5 — pasta"⁵
- ☐ **Red Onion — 1 large**
  - all → cut into 1¼-inch chunks; separate into petals 2–3 layers thick → "M4 — kebabs"⁴
- ☐ **Flat-Leaf Parsley — 1 bunch**
  - all → wash; dry very well; wrap in a barely damp paper towel → "M4 + M5 — chop day-of"⁴⁵
