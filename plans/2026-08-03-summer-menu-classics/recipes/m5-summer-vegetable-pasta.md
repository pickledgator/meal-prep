# Summer Vegetable Pasta

_with Chicken Italian Sausage_

_Makes 4 servings (2 for dinner + 2 leftover)_

---

## Prepped Ingredients

> From your Sunday prep:
>
> - "M5 — pasta" (¼ cup) — `components/italian-herb-marinade.md`
> - "M5 — pasta": zucchini half-moons, bell pepper strips, sliced garlic (2 cloves)
> - Washed parsley (chop 2 Tbsp today)
> - Remaining lemon half (from M4)

---

## Ingredients

- 1 lb (454 g) Chicken Italian Sausage, casings removed
- 8 oz (227 g) Linguine
- 2 Zucchini, ¼-inch half-moons (from prep)
- 1 Red Bell Pepper, thin strips (from prep)
- 2 cloves Garlic, thinly sliced (from prep)
- ¼–½ tsp Red Pepper Flakes
- ¼ cup Italian Herb Marinade (from prep)
- ½ cup (45 g) grated Parmesan, _plus more for serving_
- 2 Tbsp chopped Flat-Leaf Parsley (from prep)
- ½ Lemon
- 1 Tbsp Olive Oil
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_
- Fresh Basil, _optional, for serving_

---

## Instructions

### Cook

1. Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil. Take the **"M5 — pasta" marinade** out of the fridge to warm up, and chop 2 tablespoons of the **parsley**.

2. Meanwhile, heat the **olive oil** in your largest skillet over medium-high. Add the **sausage** and cook, breaking it into bite-size crumbles, until browned, 5–6 minutes. Wash hands after handling the raw sausage.

3. Add the **zucchini** and **bell pepper** to the sausage and its fat. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the edges brown but the vegetables stay crisp-tender, 4–5 minutes. Add the **garlic** and **red pepper flakes**; cook 1 minute more.

4. While the vegetables cook, drop the **linguine** into the boiling water and cook until al dente, 9–10 minutes. Scoop out ¾ cup of pasta water, then drain.

5. Add the pasta to the skillet with the marinade, ½ cup of the pasta water, and the **parmesan**. Toss vigorously 1–2 minutes until a glossy sauce coats the noodles, adding more pasta water as needed. Off the heat, stir in the parsley and a squeeze of the **lemon** half. Taste for **salt**.

### Plate & Serve

6. Pile into shallow bowls. Finish with extra parmesan, a pinch of red pepper flakes, and torn **basil** if you have it.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Pull the zucchini a shade early — it keeps cooking in the sauce, and Friday-night leftovers reheat better when it starts crisp.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** The last of the week's wine — a medium-bodied Sangiovese won't complain.
