# Spicy Salmon Bowls

_with Marinated Cucumbers_

_Makes 4 servings (2 for dinner + 2 leftover)_

## Ingredients

### Spicy Salmon

- 1½ lb (680 g) Salmon Fillets, skin removed
- 1 Tbsp Neutral Oil
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_
- ½ cup Garlic-Ginger Soy Sauce (from prep)

### Accompaniments

- 3½ cups Steamed Jasmine Rice (from prep)
- Quick-Pickled Cucumbers (from prep)
- Sriracha Mayo (from prep)
- 1 Jalapeño, thinly sliced (from prep)
- 2 Scallions, thinly sliced (from prep)
- 2 tsp Toasted Sesame Seeds

---

## Instructions

### Broil the Salmon

1. Set an oven rack about 6 inches below the broiler and heat the broiler on high. Line a rimmed baking sheet with foil.

2. Pat the **salmon** dry and cut it into 1¼-inch cubes. Toss with the **oil** and a few good pinches of **salt**, then arrange in a single layer on the sheet. Wash hands and board after handling the raw fish.

3. Broil 5–6 minutes. Pour half of the **garlic-ginger soy sauce** (¼ cup) over the salmon and gently toss to coat, then broil another 2–3 minutes, until the glaze bubbles and the edges char in spots. Watch closely — a honey glaze goes from caramelized to burnt in under a minute.

### While the Salmon Broils

4. Reheat the **rice**: sprinkle with 1 tablespoon of water, cover, and microwave 2½–3 minutes until steaming. Fluff with a fork and season with a small pinch of salt.

### Plate & Serve

5. Spoon a bed of rice into each bowl. Pile the glazed salmon on one side and, using a slotted spoon, a generous heap of **pickled cucumbers** on the other. Drizzle the remaining ¼ cup of sauce over the salmon, zigzag the **sriracha mayo** across the whole bowl, and finish with **jalapeño** slices, **scallions**, and a shower of **sesame seeds**.

---

> **Hot Tip:** For leftovers, pack the cucumbers in their own small container so the rice doesn't absorb the pickle brine overnight.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** A crisp lager or an off-dry Riesling stands up nicely to the heat.
