# Shrimp Fried Rice

_with Garlic Butter_

_Makes 4 servings (2 for dinner + 2 leftover)_

---

## Prepped Ingredients

> From your Sunday prep:
>
> - "M3 — fried rice" (4 cups cold rice) — `components/steamed-jasmine-rice.md`
> - "M3 — fried rice": minced garlic (4 cloves), diced red bell pepper, diced zucchini, sliced scallions (whites and greens separated)

---

## Ingredients

- 4 cups cold Steamed Jasmine Rice (from prep)
- 1 lb (454 g) Shrimp, peeled & deveined
- 3 Eggs, beaten
- 1 cup (155 g) Frozen Shelled Edamame, _no need to thaw_
- 1 Red Bell Pepper, ½-inch dice (from prep)
- 1 Zucchini, ½-inch dice (from prep)
- 4 cloves Garlic, minced (from prep)
- 3 Scallions, whites and greens separated (from prep)
- 3 Tbsp (42 g) Salted Butter, divided
- 2 Tbsp Neutral Oil, divided
- 2½ Tbsp Low-Sodium Soy Sauce
- 1 tsp Toasted Sesame Oil
- Kosher Salt & Black Pepper, _to taste_

---

## Instructions

### Get Set

1. Break the **cold rice** apart into loose grains with wet hands — clumps steam instead of fry. Chop the **shrimp** into ½-inch pieces and season with a pinch of **salt**. Wash hands, knife, and board after the raw shrimp.

### Cook

2. Heat your largest skillet or wok over high heat with 1 tablespoon of the **oil**. Pour in the **eggs** and soft-scramble, 30–45 seconds; scrape onto a plate.

3. Add the remaining 1 tablespoon oil. Cook the shrimp, tossing, until just opaque, 1½–2 minutes; move to the egg plate.

4. Add 1 tablespoon of the **butter**. Sauté the **bell pepper** and **zucchini** until browned at the edges but still crisp, about 3 minutes. Add the **edamame** and **scallion whites**; cook 1 minute more.

5. Push the vegetables to the edges. Melt the remaining 2 tablespoons butter in the center and add the **garlic** — 30 seconds, until fragrant (this is the garlic butter that makes the dish). Add the rice and toss everything together, then press it flat and let it sit untouched for 45 seconds. Toss, press, and repeat for 3–4 minutes, until the rice is toasted in spots.

6. Drizzle the **soy sauce** around the rim of the pan (it caramelizes on the hot metal) along with the **sesame oil**. Return the eggs and shrimp, toss 1 minute, and stir in most of the **scallion greens**. Season with salt and **pepper**.

### Plate & Serve

7. Heap into deep bowls and scatter the reserved scallion greens over the top.

---

> **Hot Tip:** The press-and-wait move is the whole game — every pause builds crispy, wok-charred bits you can't get by constant stirring.
