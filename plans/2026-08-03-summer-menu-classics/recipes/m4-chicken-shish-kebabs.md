# Marinated Chicken Shish Kebabs

_with Garlic Herb Noodles_

_Makes 4 servings (2 for dinner + 2 leftover)_

---

## Prepped Ingredients

> From your Sunday prep:
>
> - "M4 — kebab marinade" (¾ cup) — `components/italian-herb-marinade.md`
> - "M4 — kebabs": bell pepper squares, zucchini rounds, red onion petals
> - "M4 — garlic herb noodles": minced garlic (3 cloves)
> - Washed parsley (chop ¼ cup today)
> - Whole lemon (use half tonight, save half for M5)

---

## Ingredients

### Kebabs

- 1½ lb (680 g) Boneless-Skinless Chicken Thighs
- ¾ cup Italian Herb Marinade (from prep)
- 2 Red Bell Peppers, 1¼-inch squares (from prep)
- 2 Zucchini, ¾-inch rounds (from prep)
- 1 Red Onion, petals (from prep)
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_

### Garlic Herb Noodles

- 8 oz (227 g) Linguine
- 3 Tbsp (42 g) Salted Butter
- 3 cloves Garlic, minced (from prep)
- ½ cup (45 g) grated Parmesan, _plus more for serving_
- ¼ cup chopped Flat-Leaf Parsley (from prep)
- ½ tsp Italian Seasoning
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _lots_
- ½ Lemon

---

## Instructions

### Morning Of (or at least 1 hour ahead)

1. Cut the **chicken thighs** into 1¼-inch chunks and combine with the **"M4 — kebab marinade"** in its container or a zip-top bag. Refrigerate 1–8 hours. Wash hands and board after handling the raw chicken.

### Day Of

2. If using bamboo **skewers**, soak them 20 minutes. Heat the broiler on high with a rack ~6 inches below (or a grill to medium-high), and set a large pot of well-salted water over high heat for the pasta.

3. Thread the chicken onto 8 skewers, alternating with **bell pepper**, **zucchini**, and **red onion**. Discard the used marinade. Season the vegetables lightly with **salt**. Leave a whisper of space between pieces so their sides actually cook.

4. Broil or grill 12–14 minutes, giving the skewers a quarter-turn every 3–4 minutes, until charred at the edges and the chicken reads 165°F.

### While the Kebabs Cook

5. Chop ¼ cup of the **parsley**. Cook the **linguine** in the boiling water until al dente, 9–10 minutes. Scoop out ⅓ cup of pasta water, then drain.

6. In the empty pasta pot over medium heat, melt the **butter** and cook the **garlic** until fragrant, about 1 minute. Return the noodles with ¼ cup of the reserved pasta water and the **parmesan**; toss until glossy. Off the heat, add the parsley, **Italian seasoning**, a very generous grind of **black pepper**, and a squeeze of the **lemon** half. Loosen with the last splash of pasta water if needed.

### Plate & Serve

7. Twirl a nest of noodles onto each plate and lay two skewers across the top (or slide the pieces off, if you're not standing on ceremony). Shower with extra parmesan.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Thighs forgive the broiler in a way breasts don't — but if you swapped in breast, pull the skewers at 160°F and let carryover heat finish the job.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** A chilled Pinot Grigio or a light Chianti.
