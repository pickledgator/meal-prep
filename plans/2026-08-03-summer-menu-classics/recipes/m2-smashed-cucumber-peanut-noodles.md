# Smashed Cucumber Peanut Noodle Salad

_with Marinated Tofu_

_Makes 4 servings (2 for dinner + 2 leftover)_

## Ingredients

- 8 oz (227 g) Rice Noodles, pad thai width
- 1 cup (155 g) Frozen Shelled Edamame
- 4 Persian Cucumbers (from prep)
- ½ tsp Kosher Salt
- Peanut-Lime Dressing (from prep)
- Marinated Baked Tofu (from prep)
- 1 Jalapeño, thinly sliced (from prep)
- 2 Scallions, thinly sliced (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp Toasted Sesame Seeds
- Lime Wedges (from prep), _for serving_

---

## Instructions

### Smash the Cucumbers

1. Set a large pot of water over high heat. While it comes to a boil, lay the **cucumbers** on a cutting board and whack them with the flat side of a chef's knife (or a rolling pin) until they crack open. Tear or cut into bite-size pieces, toss with the **salt** in a colander, and let them drain while you cook the noodles.

### Cook the Noodles

2. Cook the **rice noodles** in the boiling water, stirring occasionally, until tender with a slight chew, 6–8 minutes, adding the **edamame** for the final 2 minutes. Drain, rinse under cold water until fully cool, then drain very well.

3. If the **dressing** thickened in the fridge, whisk in warm water 1 tablespoon at a time until it pours like heavy cream again.

### Assemble

4. In a large bowl, toss the noodles and edamame with about ¾ of the dressing. Shake the excess liquid off the cucumbers, add them, and toss again.

### Plate & Serve

5. Twirl the noodles into shallow bowls. Top each with a pile of **tofu** (room temperature is right — or re-crisp it 3–4 minutes in a dry skillet if you prefer it hot), then drizzle the remaining dressing over the tofu. Finish with **jalapeño**, **scallions**, and **sesame seeds**, and hook a **lime wedge** on the rim.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Salting the smashed cucumbers pulls out water that would otherwise dilute the peanut dressing — don't skip the 10-minute drain.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** This one's great slightly cold, which makes the leftovers arguably better than night one.
