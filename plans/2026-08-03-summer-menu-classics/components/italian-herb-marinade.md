# Italian Herb Marinade

_Makes about 1 cup — double duty: kebab marinade and pasta finisher_

## Ingredients

- ⅔ cup (160 ml) Olive Oil
- Zest and Juice of 1 Lemon (about 3 Tbsp juice; from prep)
- 2 Tbsp Red Wine Vinegar
- 4 cloves Garlic, finely grated (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp Italian Seasoning
- 1 tsp (7 g) Honey
- 1¼ tsp Kosher Salt
- ½ tsp freshly ground Black Pepper
- ¼ tsp Red Pepper Flakes

## Instructions

1. In a bowl or jar, whisk together the **olive oil**, **lemon zest and juice**, **red wine vinegar**, **garlic**, **Italian seasoning**, **honey**, **salt**, **pepper**, and **red pepper flakes** until emulsified.

2. Divide: ¾ cup into a container or zip-top bag → "M4 — kebab marinade" (the chicken joins it Thursday morning); ¼ cup into a small jar → "M5 — pasta."

---

**To Store:** Refrigerate up to 1 week. The olive oil will solidify when cold — 10 minutes at room temperature and a good stir brings it back.

**Used in:** Meals 4, 5
