# Marinated Baked Tofu

_Makes 4 portions_

## Ingredients

- 2 (14 oz / 397 g) blocks Extra-Firm Tofu
- ½ cup (120 ml) Garlic-Ginger Soy Sauce (`components/garlic-ginger-soy-sauce.md`)
- 1½ Tbsp Cornstarch
- 1 Tbsp Neutral Oil

## Instructions

1. **Press:** Drain the **tofu**, wrap each block in a clean kitchen towel, and set something heavy on top (a cast-iron skillet works). Press 30 minutes while you continue prepping.

2. **Cube:** Cut the pressed tofu into 1-inch cubes.

3. **Marinate:** Toss the cubes with the **garlic-ginger soy sauce** in a bowl. Marinate 20–30 minutes, gently tossing once or twice.

4. Preheat the oven to 425°F. Line a rimmed baking sheet with parchment.

5. **Bake:** Lift the tofu out of the marinade (leave the excess behind; discard it), toss with the **cornstarch** and **oil**, and spread in a single layer on the sheet. Bake 25–30 minutes, flipping halfway, until deeply golden at the edges.

6. Cool completely before storing.

---

**To Store:** Airtight in the refrigerator up to 5 days. Serve at room temperature in the noodle salad, or re-crisp 3–4 minutes in a dry skillet.
