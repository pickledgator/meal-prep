# Garlic-Ginger Soy Sauce

_Makes about 1 cup — the week's workhorse: salmon glaze and tofu marinade_

## Ingredients

- ½ cup (120 ml) Low-Sodium Soy Sauce
- 3 Tbsp (63 g) Honey
- 2 Tbsp Rice Vinegar
- 1 Tbsp Toasted Sesame Oil
- 3 cloves Garlic, finely grated (from prep)
- 1½ Tbsp finely grated Ginger (from prep)
- 1 tsp Sriracha, _optional, for gentle background heat_

## Instructions

1. In a bowl or jar, whisk together the **soy sauce**, **honey**, **rice vinegar**, **sesame oil**, **garlic**, **ginger**, and **sriracha** (if using) until the honey fully dissolves.

2. Divide: ½ cup goes straight into the tofu marinade (`components/marinated-baked-tofu.md`, step 3); pack the remaining ½ cup into a small container → "M1 — salmon glaze."

---

**To Store:** Refrigerate up to 1 week. Stir before using — the garlic and ginger settle.

**Used in:** Meals 1, 2
