# Quick-Pickled Cucumbers

_Makes about 1½ cups_

## Ingredients

- 3 Persian Cucumbers, sliced into ⅛-inch rounds (from prep)
- ⅓ cup (80 ml) Rice Vinegar
- 1 Tbsp White Cane Sugar
- 1 tsp Kosher Salt
- ½ tsp Toasted Sesame Oil

## Instructions

1. In a jar or bowl, whisk the **rice vinegar**, **sugar**, and **salt** until dissolved. Stir in the **sesame oil**.

2. Add the **cucumber rounds** and toss so everything is coated and mostly submerged. Cover and refrigerate at least 30 minutes.

---

**To Store:** Airtight in the refrigerator up to 5 days — they'll be perfectly seasoned by Monday's salmon bowls.
