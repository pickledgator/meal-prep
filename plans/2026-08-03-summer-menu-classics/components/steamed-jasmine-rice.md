# Steamed Jasmine Rice

_Makes about 7½ cups cooked_

## Ingredients

- 2½ cups (500 g) Jasmine Rice
- 3 cups (710 ml) Water
- ¾ tsp Kosher Salt

## Instructions

1. Place the **rice** in a fine-mesh sieve and rinse under cold running water, agitating with your hand, until the water runs mostly clear, about 1 minute. Drain well.

2. Combine the rinsed rice, **water**, and **salt** in a medium pot. Bring to a boil over high heat, uncovered.

3. As soon as it boils, stir once, cover with a tight-fitting lid, and reduce the heat to the lowest setting. Cook for 15 minutes — no peeking.

4. Off the heat, let the rice rest, covered, for 10 minutes, then fluff with a fork.

5. Pack 3½ cups into an airtight container → "M1 — rice."

6. Spread the remaining 4 cups on a rimmed baking sheet to cool completely — drying it out is exactly what fried rice wants — then transfer to a second airtight container → "M3 — fried rice."

---

**To Store:** Refrigerate both containers for up to 5 days.

**Used in:** Meals 1, 3
