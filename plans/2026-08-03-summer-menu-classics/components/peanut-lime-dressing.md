# Peanut-Lime Dressing

_Makes about 1 cup_

## Ingredients

- ½ cup (128 g) Creamy Peanut Butter
- ¼ cup (60 ml) fresh Lime Juice (from 2 limes, from prep)
- 2 Tbsp Low-Sodium Soy Sauce
- 1 Tbsp Rice Vinegar
- 1 Tbsp (21 g) Honey
- 2 tsp Toasted Sesame Oil
- 1 clove Garlic, finely grated (from prep)
- 1 tsp finely grated Ginger (from prep)
- 3–4 Tbsp Warm Water

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, whisk together the **peanut butter**, **lime juice**, **soy sauce**, **rice vinegar**, **honey**, **sesame oil**, **garlic**, and **ginger** until completely smooth.

2. Whisk in the **warm water** 1 tablespoon at a time until the dressing is the consistency of heavy cream. It will thicken in the refrigerator — you'll loosen it again with warm water on Tuesday.

---

**To Store:** Airtight in the refrigerator up to 1 week.

**Used in:** Meal 2
