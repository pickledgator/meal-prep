# Oven-"Smoked" Brisket

_Makes ~1¾ lb cooked brisket + ~2 cups braising liquid — serves 4 with a little left over_

Smoked paprika and chipotle stand in for the smoker; a low, covered oven braise stands in for the twelve-hour pit. Brisket is genuinely better made a day (or three) ahead — the slices reheat in their own braising liquid without drying out.

## Ingredients

- 1 Beef Brisket (2½–3½ lb / 1.1–1.6 kg), thawed ❄️
- 3 Tbsp Smokehouse Rub — `components/smokehouse-rub.md`
- 1 Tbsp Olive Oil
- 1 medium Yellow Onion, cut into thick slices
- 4 cloves Garlic, smashed
- 1½ cups (360 ml) Chicken Stock _(or water + 1½ tsp bouillon)_
- ¼ cup (60 ml) Apple Cider Vinegar
- 2 Tbsp Worcestershire Sauce
- 1 Chipotle in Adobo, roughly chopped, + 1 Tbsp adobo sauce
- 2 Bay Leaves

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 300°F. Pat the **brisket** very dry and coat all sides with the **smokehouse rub**, pressing to adhere.

2. Heat the **olive oil** in a Dutch oven over medium-high. Sear the brisket, fat side down first, 3–4 minutes per side, until deeply browned. Transfer to a plate. (If your brisket is too long for the pot, halve it crosswise and sear in batches.)

3. Drop the heat to medium. Add the **onion** and cook 2–3 minutes in the drippings, scraping up the browned bits. Add the **garlic** and cook 30 seconds. Pour in the **stock**, **vinegar**, and **Worcestershire**; stir in the **chipotle + adobo** and **bay leaves**.

4. Return the brisket fat side up — the liquid should come about halfway up its sides (top up with water if needed). Cover tightly and braise in the oven **3–3½ hours** (figure 45–60 minutes per pound), until a fork slides in and twists with almost no resistance.

5. Rest the brisket in its liquid, uncovered, ~30 minutes. Discard the bay leaves and skim the fat cap off the liquid. **Ladle 1 cup (240 ml) of the braising liquid into the Smoky Ranch Beans** 🫙 (`components/smoky-ranch-beans.md`) — the rest stays with the meat.

---

**To Store:** Keep the brisket whole (unsliced), submerged in the remaining braising liquid, covered, in the refrigerator up to 4 days. Cold brisket slices far more cleanly than warm — slice it straight from the fridge on the night you serve it.

**Used in:** Meal 1 (braising liquid also builds the Smoky Ranch Beans)
