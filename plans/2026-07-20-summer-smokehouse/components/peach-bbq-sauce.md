# Peach BBQ Sauce

_Makes about 1½ cups (360 ml)_

Peak-July peaches where the ketchup would usually go — fruity, smoky, and just hot enough from the chipotle.

## Ingredients

- 1 Tbsp Olive Oil
- ½ medium Yellow Onion, finely chopped
- 3 cloves Garlic, minced
- 2 ripe Peaches (~12 oz / 340 g), pitted & roughly chopped (no need to peel)
- 2 Tbsp Tomato Paste
- ¼ cup (60 ml) Apple Cider Vinegar
- 2 Tbsp Brown Sugar
- 1 Tbsp Honey
- 1 Chipotle in Adobo, chopped, + 2 tsp adobo sauce
- ½ tsp Smoked Paprika
- 1 tsp Dijon Mustard
- ¼ tsp Kosher Salt, plus more _to taste_
- 2 Tbsp Water

## Instructions

1. Heat the **olive oil** in a small saucepan over medium. Cook the **onion** with a pinch of salt until soft and translucent, 4–5 minutes. Add the **garlic** and cook 1 minute more.

2. Add the **peaches**, **tomato paste**, **vinegar**, **brown sugar**, **honey**, **chipotle + adobo**, **smoked paprika**, **dijon**, **salt**, and **water**. Simmer over medium-low, stirring occasionally and mashing the peaches as they soften, 15–20 minutes, until the peaches have collapsed and the sauce is jammy.

3. Blend smooth with an immersion blender (or a regular blender, or leave it rustic and chunky — both are good). If it's thinner than a squeezable BBQ sauce, simmer 3–4 minutes more. Taste — it should hit sweet, tangy, smoky, and a little hot in that order. Adjust with salt or a splash more vinegar.

---

**To Store:** Airtight jar in the refrigerator up to 1 week.

**Usage plan:** ½ cup to glaze the brisket + ½ cup at the table (Meal 1), ¼ cup as a dipper (Meal 2), ~¼ cup buffer.

**Used in:** Meals 1 & 2
