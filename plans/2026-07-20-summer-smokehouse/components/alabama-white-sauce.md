# Alabama White Sauce

_Adapted from the North Alabama style credited to Big Bob Gibson_

_Makes about 1¼ cups_

Mayo-based, vinegar-sharp, and aggressively black-peppered — no cooking required. It doubles as this week's slaw dressing.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup (220 g) Mayonnaise
- 3 Tbsp (45 ml) Apple Cider Vinegar
- 1 Tbsp Prepared Horseradish
- 1½ Tbsp Lemon Juice (from ½ lemon)
- 2 tsp Dijon Mustard
- 1 tsp Honey
- 1 clove Garlic, finely grated
- 2 tsp coarsely ground Black Pepper
- ¼ tsp Cayenne
- ¼ tsp Kosher Salt

## Instructions

1. In a medium bowl, whisk together the **mayonnaise**, **vinegar**, **horseradish**, **lemon juice**, **dijon**, **honey**, **garlic**, **black pepper**, **cayenne**, and **salt** until smooth and pourable.

2. Taste — it should be tangy first, peppery second, with a horseradish hum underneath. Add another ½ Tbsp vinegar if it isn't making you blink.

3. Refrigerate at least 30 minutes before using; the flavors round out as it sits.

---

**To Store:** Airtight jar in the refrigerator up to 1 week.

**Usage plan:** ⅓ cup dresses the corn slaw (Meal 1), ¾ cup for drizzling and dunking the chicken (Meal 2), small buffer.

**Used in:** Meals 1 & 2
