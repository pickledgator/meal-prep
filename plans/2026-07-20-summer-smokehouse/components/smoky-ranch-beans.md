# Smoky Ranch Beans

_Makes about 3½ cups_

Pinto beans simmered in the brisket's own braising liquid — one braise, two flavors. Make these Sunday right after the brisket comes out of the oven.

## Ingredients

- 1 Tbsp Olive Oil
- ½ medium Yellow Onion, finely chopped
- 3 cloves Garlic, minced
- 1 Chipotle in Adobo, minced, + 2 tsp adobo sauce
- 1 tsp Ground Cumin
- 1 tsp Dried Oregano
- 2 tsp Tomato Paste
- 2 (15 oz / 425 g) cans Pinto Beans, drained & rinsed
- 1 cup (240 ml) Brisket braising liquid 🫙 _(or chicken stock)_
- 1 tsp Apple Cider Vinegar
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_

## Instructions

1. Heat the **olive oil** in a saucepan over medium. Cook the **onion** until soft, 4–5 minutes. Add the **garlic**, **chipotle + adobo**, **cumin**, **oregano**, and **tomato paste**; stir 1 minute until fragrant and brick-red.

2. Add the **beans** and **braising liquid**. Simmer gently, uncovered, 15 minutes, until the liquid is saucy rather than soupy.

3. Mash a few spoonfuls of beans against the side of the pot to thicken. Finish with the **vinegar** and season with **salt** (go easy — the braising liquid is already seasoned).

---

**To Store:** Airtight container in the refrigerator up to 4 days, labeled "M1 — beans." Reheat with a splash of water.

**Used in:** Meal 1
