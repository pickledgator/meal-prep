# Quick-Pickled Red Onions

_Makes about 1½ cups_

Sharp-sweet pink ribbons that cut through the rich brisket and dress the peach-tomato salad. The brine pulls double duty as part of the salad dressing.

## Ingredients

- 1 large Red Onion, halved & thinly sliced
- ½ cup (120 ml) Apple Cider Vinegar
- ½ cup (120 ml) Water
- 1 Tbsp White Cane Sugar
- 1½ tsp Kosher Salt

## Instructions

1. Pack the sliced **red onion** into a pint jar or heatproof container.

2. In a small saucepan (or a microwave-safe measuring cup, ~90 seconds on high), heat the **vinegar**, **water**, **sugar**, and **salt** until steaming and the sugar dissolves.

3. Pour the hot brine over the onions, pressing them down to submerge. Let cool to room temperature, then lid and refrigerate. Ready in 1 hour; better by dinner time.

---

**To Store:** In their brine, refrigerated, up to 2 weeks.

**Usage plan:** ½ cup over the brisket (Meal 1), ½ cup + 1 Tbsp brine in the peach-tomato salad (Meal 2); extras are great on everything.

**Used in:** Meals 1 & 2
