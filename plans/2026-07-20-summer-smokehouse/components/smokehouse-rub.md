# Smokehouse Rub

_Makes about 5½ Tbsp_

## Ingredients

- 2 Tbsp Smoked Paprika
- 1 Tbsp Brown Sugar
- 2 tsp Kosher Salt
- 2 tsp coarsely ground Black Pepper
- 1½ tsp Garlic Powder
- 1 tsp Ground Cumin
- 1 tsp Chili Powder

## Instructions

1. In a small bowl, whisk together the **smoked paprika**, **brown sugar**, **salt**, **pepper**, **garlic powder**, **cumin**, and **chili powder** until no streaks of sugar remain.

2. Use 3 Tbsp immediately for the brisket (see `components/oven-smoked-brisket.md`). Transfer the remaining ~2½ Tbsp to a small jar.

---

**To Store:** Airtight jar at room temperature, labeled "M2 — chicken & corn." Keeps for months.

**Used in:** Oven-"Smoked" Brisket (Meal 1) and Grilled Chicken + Charred Corn (Meal 2)
