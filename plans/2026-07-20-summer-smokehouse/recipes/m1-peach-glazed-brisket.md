# Peach-Glazed Oven Brisket

_with Smoky Ranch Beans & Corn Slaw_

_Makes 4 servings_

## Ingredients

### Brisket

- Braised Brisket (from prep), cold
- ¾ cup (180 ml) reserved braising liquid (from prep)
- ½ cup (120 ml) Peach BBQ Sauce, plus ½ cup _for serving_

### Corn Slaw

- 6 cups (420 g) shredded Green Cabbage (from prep)
- Kernels from 1 ear Sweet Corn (~¾ cup / 110 g, from prep — raw and sweet is the point)
- ½ cup chopped Cilantro (or a couple of big handfuls)
- ⅓ cup (80 ml) Alabama White Sauce (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp Apple Cider Vinegar
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_

### Accompaniments

- Smoky Ranch Beans (full batch / ~3½ cups), reheated
- ½ cup Quick-Pickled Red Onions
- 2 Tbsp chopped Cilantro, _for the beans_
- 4 thick slices Sourdough + Olive Oil _(optional)_
- Flaky Salt _(optional)_

---

## Instructions

### Reheat & Glaze the Brisket

1. Preheat the oven to 325°F. Slice the **cold brisket** against the grain into ¼-inch slices. Shingle the slices in a baking dish, pour the **braising liquid** over, cover tightly with foil, and warm 15–18 minutes.

2. While the brisket warms, make the slaw and reheat the beans (steps 3–4).

3. **Corn Slaw:** In a large bowl, toss the **cabbage**, **corn kernels**, and **cilantro** with the **Alabama white sauce**, **vinegar**, and a big pinch of **salt**. Let it sit 10 minutes to soften slightly, then toss again and taste for salt.

4. **Beans:** Warm the **smoky ranch beans** in a saucepan over medium-low with a splash of water, covered, 6–8 minutes, stirring once or twice, until steaming. _(Optional: brush the **sourdough** with **olive oil** and toast in a dry skillet or toaster while the beans warm.)_

5. Switch the oven to broil. Uncover the brisket, brush the slices with ½ cup **peach BBQ sauce**, and broil ~6 inches from the element for 3–4 minutes, until the glaze bubbles and the edges char in spots. Watch it closely — sugar moves fast under a broiler.

### Plate & Serve

6. Spoon a generous bed of **beans** onto one side of each plate and scatter with **cilantro**. Lean 3–4 slices of glazed **brisket** against the beans, spooning any glaze from the dish over the top. Crown the brisket with a tangle of **pickled red onions** and a pinch of **flaky salt**. Pile the **slaw** on the open side of the plate — keep it separate so it stays crunchy — and tuck in the toast if using. Pass the remaining **peach BBQ sauce** at the table.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Cold brisket slices cleanly; warm brisket shreds. Always slice straight from the fridge, and always against the grain — find the direction the muscle fibers run and cut perpendicular to them. If the grain shifts mid-brisket (it does), shift your knife angle with it.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** Iced tea, a cold lager, or a juicy Zinfandel.
