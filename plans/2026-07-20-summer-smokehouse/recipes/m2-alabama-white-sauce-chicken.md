# Grilled Chicken with Alabama White Sauce

_with Charred Corn & Peach-Tomato Salad_

_Makes 4 servings_

---

## Prepped Ingredients

> From your Sunday prep:
>
> - Alabama White Sauce (~¾ cup) — `components/alabama-white-sauce.md`
> - Smokehouse Rub (2½ Tbsp) — `components/smokehouse-rub.md`
> - Quick-Pickled Red Onions (½ cup + 1 Tbsp brine) — `components/quick-pickled-red-onions.md`
> - Peach BBQ Sauce (¼ cup, optional dipper) — `components/peach-bbq-sauce.md`
> - Corn in husks (4 ears), 2 whole peaches, reserved ½ lemon, washed cilantro

---

## Ingredients

### Chicken

- 1¾ lb (795 g) Boneless-Skinless Chicken Thighs (about 8)
- 1 Tbsp Neutral Oil
- 2 Tbsp Smokehouse Rub (from prep)

### Charred Corn

- 4 ears Sweet Corn, shucked
- 1 Tbsp Neutral Oil
- ½ tsp Smokehouse Rub (from prep)
- ½ Lemon (from prep)
- 2 Tbsp chopped Cilantro

### Peach-Tomato Salad

- 2 ripe Peaches, pitted & cut into wedges
- 2 Heirloom Tomatoes (~1¼ lb / 570 g), cored & cut into wedges
- 2 Persian Cucumbers (~160 g), sliced on the bias
- ½ cup Quick-Pickled Red Onions, plus 1 Tbsp of their brine
- 2 Tbsp Olive Oil
- ¼ cup Cilantro leaves
- Flaky Salt & coarsely ground Black Pepper

### For Serving

- ¾ cup Alabama White Sauce
- ¼ cup Peach BBQ Sauce _(optional, for dipping)_

---

## Instructions

### Grill

1. Heat a grill to medium-high (or a cast-iron grill pan over medium-high). Pat the **chicken thighs** dry, toss with the **neutral oil**, and coat evenly with the **smokehouse rub**.

2. Rub the **corn** with **oil** and lay it on the grill. Roll a quarter-turn every 2–3 minutes until charred in spots all over, 10–12 minutes total.

3. Meanwhile, grill the **chicken** alongside: 5–6 minutes per side, until deeply browned with crisp edges and 165°F at the thickest point. Rest 5 minutes.

### Salad (while everything grills)

4. On a platter, arrange the **tomato** and **peach** wedges and **cucumbers**. Scatter the **pickled red onions** over. Spoon the **pickle brine** and **olive oil** across everything, then finish with **cilantro leaves**, **flaky salt**, and plenty of **black pepper**. Don't toss it — arranged wedges hold their shape and look better.

### Finish the Corn

5. Squeeze the **½ lemon** over the hot corn, dust with the remaining **½ tsp rub**, and scatter with **cilantro**.

### Plate & Serve

6. Pile the rested **chicken** on a board or platter and drizzle generously with **Alabama white sauce** in wide zigzags — the classic move is a full dunk, so don't be shy. Stack the corn alongside. Serve the salad platter, the remaining **white sauce** in a ramekin, and the **peach BBQ sauce** as a second dipper for the table.

---

> **Hot Tip:** White sauce is a *finishing* sauce — never brush it on during grilling (the mayo splits and scorches). Sauce the chicken in the last 30 seconds on the grill or right as it comes off, so the vinegar and black pepper stay bright against the char.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** Cold pilsner, dry rosé, or peach-mint iced tea.
