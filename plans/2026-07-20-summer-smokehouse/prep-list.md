# Sunday Prep List

_Difficulty: Normal — ~75–90 minutes active. The brisket braises passively for 3–3½ hours, so start it first and plan to be around the house._

> **Before Sunday** ❄️ — Move the **brisket** from freezer to fridge **Friday night** (a 3 lb piece needs 24–48 hours to thaw). Forgot? Cold-water thaw day-of: sealed bag, fully submerged, change the water every 30 minutes — about 2–3 hours.

---

☐ **Preheat Oven to 300°F** 🫙

☐ **Mix Smokehouse Rub** — see `components/smokehouse-rub.md`
3 Tbsp used today on the brisket 🫙; store ~2½ Tbsp in a jar labeled "M2 — chicken & corn"²

☐ **Start Oven-"Smoked" Brisket** (steps 1–4) — see `components/oven-smoked-brisket.md`¹
Rub, sear, and build the braise (slice 1 yellow onion + smash 4 garlic cloves now — see produce breakdown below), then into the oven for **3–3½ hours passive**. Set a timer for 3 hours.

---

## Prep Produce (⏱ ~25 minutes)

- ☐ **Garlic — 11 cloves total; peel all**
  - 4 → smash; used today in Brisket braise 🫙 _(grab these first, before the sear)_
  - 3 → mince; used today in Peach BBQ Sauce 🫙
  - 3 → mince; used today in Smoky Ranch Beans 🫙
  - 1 → grate; used today in Alabama White Sauce 🫙
- ☐ **Yellow Onions — 2 medium total; peel**
  - 1 → thick slices; used today in Brisket braise 🫙 _(with the garlic above)_
  - ½ → fine chop; used today in Peach BBQ Sauce 🫙
  - ½ → fine chop; used today in Smoky Ranch Beans 🫙
- ☐ Red Onion (1 large; halve & thinly slice) — used today in Quick-Pickled Red Onions 🫙
- ☐ **Peaches — 4 total; wash**
  - 2 ripest → pit & rough chop (no peeling); used today in Peach BBQ Sauce 🫙
  - 2 → leave whole at room temperature; slice day-of for "M2 — peach-tomato salad"²
- ☐ Green Cabbage (1 small head; quarter, core & shred ~6 cups / 420 g) — store labeled "M1 — corn slaw"¹
- ☐ **Sweet Corn — 5 ears total**
  - 1 → shuck & cut kernels off the cob (~¾ cup / 110 g); store labeled "M1 — corn slaw"¹
  - 4 → leave in husks in the fridge; for "M2 — charred corn" (shuck day-of)²
- ☐ Cilantro (1 bunch; wash & spin dry) — store whole, wrapped in a barely-damp towel; chop day-of¹²
- ☐ Lemon (1; juice ½ = 1½ Tbsp) — juice used today in Alabama White Sauce 🫙; wrap & store the other ½ for "M2 — corn"²

## Cooked Prep Tasks

☐ **Quick-Pickled Red Onions** — see `components/quick-pickled-red-onions.md`¹²
Heat brine, pour over onions, cool, refrigerate (yield: ~1½ cups)

☐ **Peach BBQ Sauce** — see `components/peach-bbq-sauce.md`¹²
Sauté aromatics, simmer 15–20 minutes, blend (yield: ~1½ cups)

---

## Prepare Components

☐ **Alabama White Sauce** — see `components/alabama-white-sauce.md`¹²
Whisk everything together — no cooking (yield: ~1¼ cups)

---

## When the Brisket Comes Out (~3–3½ hour mark)

☐ **Finish the Brisket** (step 5) — see `components/oven-smoked-brisket.md`¹
Rest 30 minutes in the liquid, discard bay leaves, skim fat; ladle **1 cup braising liquid** into the beans below 🫙; store the brisket whole, submerged, labeled "M1 — brisket"

☐ **Smoky Ranch Beans** — see `components/smoky-ranch-beans.md`¹
Sauté aromatics, simmer with the braising liquid ~15 minutes (yield: ~3½ cups); store labeled "M1 — beans"
