# Santa Maria Rub

_The classic Central-Coast dry rub for the tri-tip — salt, pepper, garlic, and a little smoke._

_Makes about ⅓ cup — enough for 2 tri-tips (~4 lb)_

## Ingredients

- 2 Tbsp Kosher Salt
- 1 Tbsp Coarsely Ground Black Pepper
- 1 Tbsp Garlic Powder
- 2 tsp Smoked Paprika _(or sweet paprika)_
- 1 tsp Onion Powder
- 1 tsp Dried Oregano
- ½ tsp Cayenne _(optional)_

## Instructions

1. Combine all ingredients in a small jar or bowl and stir (or shake) to blend evenly.

2. The night before, pat the tri-tips dry and coat all over with the rub. Set them on a rack over a sheet pan and refrigerate **uncovered overnight** — this dry-brine seasons deep and dries the surface for a better crust.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Uncovered overnight is the secret to a restaurant-quality crust. If you're short on time, even 1 hour of rub + a thorough pat-dry helps.

**To Store:** The dry blend keeps airtight in the pantry for months.

**Used in:** Main (Santa Maria tri-tip)³.
