# Charred Corn Succotash

_The summery base for both mains — a bed of sweet corn, blistered tomatoes, and beans under the tri-tip and salmon._

_Makes about 7 cups (serves 8–10 as a side)_

## Ingredients

- 6 ears Sweet Corn, husked (~4½ cups kernels)
- 1 lb (454 g) Cherry or Grape Tomatoes, halved
- 1 can (15 oz / 425 g) Cannellini Beans, drained & rinsed _(or 1½ cups fresh shell beans, cooked)_
- 1 Shallot, finely diced
- 2 cloves Garlic, minced
- 2 Tbsp Salted Butter
- 1 Tbsp Olive Oil
- ¼ cup Fresh Basil, torn
- Juice of ½ Lemon
- Kosher Salt & Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_
- Pinch of Red Pepper Flakes _(optional)_

## Instructions

### On Prep Day:
**Char the Corn**

1. Char the husked **corn** directly over a grill flame (or in a dry cast-iron skillet over high heat), turning, until blistered and blackened in spots, 6–8 minutes. Cool, then stand each ear in a bowl and cut off the kernels. Store the kernels airtight.

### Day Of:
**Build the Succotash**

2. Melt the **butter** with the **olive oil** in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the **shallot** and **garlic** and cook until soft and fragrant, 2–3 minutes.

3. Add the **charred corn** and **cannellini beans**; warm through, 3–4 minutes. Fold in the **cherry tomatoes** and cook just until they begin to slump, 2 minutes — you want them warm but still holding shape.

4. Off the heat, finish with the **lemon juice**, torn **basil**, **salt**, **pepper**, and **red pepper flakes** if using.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Char the corn a day ahead — that smoky flavor is the backbone of the dish, and it saves you a step at the stove while guests are arriving. Reheat the assembled succotash gently just before plating.

**To Store:** Charred kernels keep airtight in the fridge up to 3 days; fully assembled succotash keeps up to 2 days (reheat gently, refresh with a squeeze of lemon and fresh basil).

**Used in:** Main (tri-tip & salmon)³.
