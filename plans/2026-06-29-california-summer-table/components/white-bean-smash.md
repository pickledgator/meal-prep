# White Bean Smash

_The creamy base for the heirloom tomato bruschetta._

_Makes about 2½ cups_

## Ingredients

- 2 cans (15 oz / 425 g each) Cannellini Beans, drained & rinsed
- 2 cloves Garlic, finely grated
- Juice of 1 Lemon
- ¼ cup (60 ml) Extra-Virgin Olive Oil, plus more for finishing
- 2–3 Tbsp Water, as needed
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_
- Pinch of Red Pepper Flakes _(optional)_

## Instructions

1. _(Optional, for a silkier result)_ Warm the **beans**, **garlic**, and **olive oil** in a small saucepan over low heat for 3–4 minutes, until fragrant.

2. Transfer to a bowl and mash with a fork or potato masher until mostly smooth but still a little chunky. Stir in the **lemon juice** and loosen with **water** a tablespoon at a time until spreadable.

3. Season generously with **salt**, **pepper**, and **red pepper flakes** if using. Finish with a drizzle of **olive oil**.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Keep it slightly chunky — a rustic smash holds up better under juicy tomatoes than a runny purée.

**To Store:** Airtight in the refrigerator up to 3 days. Bring to room temperature and re-stir (loosen with a splash of oil or water) before serving.

**Used in:** Heavy Apps (heirloom tomato bruschetta)¹.
