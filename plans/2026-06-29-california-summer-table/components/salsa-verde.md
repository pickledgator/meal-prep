# Salsa Verde

_The bright herb sauce drizzled over both mains — the tri-tip and the salmon._

_Makes about 1½ cups_

## Ingredients

- 1¼ cups (packed) Fresh Flat-Leaf Parsley, finely chopped (~1 bunch)
- ⅔ cup (packed) Fresh Basil, finely chopped
- ⅓ cup (50 g) Capers, drained & roughly chopped
- 3 cloves Garlic, finely grated or minced
- Zest of 1 Lemon + Juice of 1 Lemon
- 1–2 Tbsp Red Wine Vinegar
- 1 cup (240 ml) Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- ½ tsp Red Pepper Flakes
- 2–3 Anchovy Fillets, mashed to a paste (optional, _for savory depth_)
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_

## Instructions

1. In a medium bowl, combine the **parsley**, **basil**, **capers**, **garlic**, **lemon zest**, and **red pepper flakes**.

2. Stir in the **olive oil**, **lemon juice**, and **red wine vinegar** until the sauce is loose and spoonable. Mash in the **anchovy** if using.

3. Season with **salt** to taste. Let sit at least 30 minutes before serving so the flavors meld.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Chop by hand rather than blending — a blender turns it dark and pasty. You want a loose, chunky, olive-oil-forward sauce with visible flecks of green.

**To Store:** Airtight in the refrigerator up to 3 days (brightest in the first 1–2). Bring to room temperature and re-stir before serving; top with a thin film of olive oil to keep it green.

**Used in:** Main (tri-tip & salmon)³ — use ~2 Tbsp per plate (~¾ cup for the 6 tri-tip plates, ~½ cup across the salmon).
