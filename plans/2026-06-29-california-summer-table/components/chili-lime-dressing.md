# Chili-Lime Dressing

_The bright, sweet-hot dressing for the melon, cucumber & feta salad._

_Makes about ⅔ cup_

## Ingredients

- Juice of 3 Limes + Zest of 1
- 3 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- 1 Tbsp Honey
- Pinch of Red Pepper Flakes
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_

## Instructions

1. In a small bowl or jar, whisk (or shake) together the **lime juice** and **zest**, **olive oil**, **honey**, and **red pepper flakes**.

2. Season with **salt** to taste. It should be bright, a little sweet, and gently spicy — punchy enough to stand up to sweet melon and salty feta.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Make it a day ahead so the flavors round out, but dress the salad only at the last second — lime juice draws water out of the melon and cucumber fast.

**To Store:** Airtight in the refrigerator up to 3 days. Re-whisk (it separates) before using.

**Used in:** Melon, Cucumber & Feta Salad².
