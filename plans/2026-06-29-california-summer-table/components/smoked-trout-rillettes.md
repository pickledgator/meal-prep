# Smoked Trout Rillettes

_The rich, make-ahead seafood spread for the apps board — served with seeded crackers, radishes & cucumber._

_Makes about 2 cups (serves 8 as a spread)_

## Ingredients

- 8–10 oz (250–285 g) Hot-Smoked Trout, skin & bones removed, flaked
- 4 Tbsp (½ stick / 57 g) Salted Butter, softened
- ¼ cup (60 g) Full-Fat Greek Yogurt _(or crème fraîche)_
- 1 small Shallot, minced
- Zest of 1 Lemon + Juice of ½–1 Lemon
- 1 Tbsp Capers, chopped _(optional)_
- 2 Tbsp Fresh Dill, chopped, plus more _for garnish_
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_
- Pinch of Cayenne _(optional)_
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_

## Instructions

1. In a medium bowl, mash the softened **butter** and **Greek yogurt** together with a fork until smooth and creamy.

2. Fold in the flaked **smoked trout**, **shallot**, **lemon zest** and **juice**, **capers**, and **dill**. Mash to a coarse, spreadable texture — keep some flaky texture rather than a smooth purée.

3. Season with **black pepper**, **cayenne**, and **salt** to taste (smoked trout is salty, so add salt last). Cover and refrigerate at least 1 hour for the flavors to set.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Pregnancy note — refrigerated smoked fish is the one thing on the apps table an expecting guest should skip. To serve it safely to everyone, spoon the rillettes into a small dish and bake at 375°F until hot and steaming (165°F), 12–15 minutes, for a warm smoked-trout dip.

**To Store:** Airtight in the refrigerator up to 3 days. Bring to cool room temperature ~20 minutes before serving so it spreads easily.

**Used in:** Heavy Apps (smoked trout rillettes)¹.
