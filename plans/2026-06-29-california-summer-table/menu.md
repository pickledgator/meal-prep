# The Menu

_Dinner Party · Friday, July 3, 2026 · 8 guests_

_California Summer Table — a peak-season, make-ahead celebration menu. 6 tri-tip, 2 salmon (pescatarian). No pork · dairy-light. Mostly pregnancy-safe by design — the one exception is the smoked trout rillettes, which the expecting guest should skip (or serve warmed into a baked dip)._
