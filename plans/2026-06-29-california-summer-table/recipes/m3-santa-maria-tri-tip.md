# Santa Maria Tri-Tip

_with Charred Corn Succotash, Grilled Zucchini & Salsa Verde_

_Serves 6 (the meat-eaters) — see `m3-salmon-salsa-verde.md` for the 2 pescatarian plates_

## Ingredients

### The Tri-Tip

- 2 Tri-Tip Roasts (~4 lb / 1.8 kg total), rubbed & dry-brined (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp Olive Oil, _for searing_

### Grilled Zucchini _(shared with the salmon plates)_

- 4 medium Zucchini (~1½ lb / 680 g), sliced lengthwise ¼" thick
- 2 Tbsp Olive Oil
- Kosher Salt & Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_
- Squeeze of Lemon, _to finish_

### To Plate

- 1 batch Charred Corn Succotash, warm (from prep)
- ~¾ cup Salsa Verde (from prep) — ~2 Tbsp per plate
- Flaky Salt & Fresh Basil, _for finishing_

---

## Instructions

### Day Of — Suggested Timeline

_Guests at ~6:30 → mains plated ~7:45. Work backward:_

**~90 min before serving — Roast (reverse sear, part 1)**
1. Pull the tri-tips from the fridge 1 hour ahead to temper. Preheat oven to **250°F** with a rack set over a sheet pan.

2. Roast the tri-tips until an instant-read thermometer reads **115–120°F** at the thickest part, about 45–60 minutes. Remove, tent loosely with foil, and rest. _(They can hold, tented, up to ~45 minutes.)_

**Afternoon — Grill the Zucchini**
3. Toss the **zucchini** with **olive oil**, **salt**, and **pepper**. Grill (or use a grill pan) over medium-high until marked and just tender, 2–3 minutes per side. Finish with a squeeze of **lemon**. Hold at room temperature; warm briefly before plating.

**At service — Sear (reverse sear, part 2)**
4. Get a grill or cast-iron skillet screaming hot. Rub the rested tri-tips with **olive oil** and sear hard on all sides until a deep crust forms and the internal temp reaches **~130°F for medium-rare**, 2–3 minutes per side.

5. **For the pregnant guest:** cut off one end portion and sear it longer (or return to the pan) until cooked through with no pink — **155–160°F**. The tapered end of a tri-tip cooks more done naturally, which makes this easy.

6. Rest the seared meat **10 minutes**, then slice **thinly against the grain** (the grain changes direction in a tri-tip — pivot your knife halfway across).

### Plate & Serve

1. Spoon a generous bed of warm **charred corn succotash** onto each plate or a large platter.

2. Fan the sliced **tri-tip** over the succotash, letting it lean for height. Lay 2–3 pieces of **grilled zucchini** alongside.

3. Spoon **~2 Tbsp salsa verde** over the meat and let it pool into the succotash. Finish with **flaky salt** and a few torn **basil** leaves.

---

> **Hot Tip:** An instant-read thermometer is non-negotiable for reverse sear — pull at 115–120°F, and the hard sear will carry it to a perfect medium-rare. Slicing against the grain is what makes tri-tip tender.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** A juicy Central-Coast Zinfandel, Syrah, or a Santa Barbara Pinot Noir.
