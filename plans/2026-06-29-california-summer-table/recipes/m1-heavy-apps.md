# Heavy Apps

_Marinated Olive Board · Heirloom Tomato Bruschetta · Smoked Trout Rillettes_

_Serves 8 — grazing as guests arrive_

---

## Prepped Ingredients

> From your make-ahead prep (all done the day before):
>
> - Smoked Trout Rillettes — `components/smoked-trout-rillettes.md`
> - White Bean Smash — `components/white-bean-smash.md`
> - Marinated Olives (warm-marinated, chilled)

## Ingredients

### The Board

- 12 oz (340 g) Castelvetrano or mixed Olives, marinated (from prep)
- 8 oz (227 g) Marcona Almonds
- 8 oz (227 g) Aged Manchego, cut into wedges/shards

### Heirloom Tomato Bruschetta

- 1 large loaf Sourdough (~1 lb), sliced ½" thick (~20–24 slices)
- 2½ cups White Bean Smash (from prep)
- 1½–2 lb (680–900 g) Heirloom Tomatoes, diced
- ½ cup Fresh Basil, torn
- 2 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- 1 Tbsp Red Wine Vinegar
- Kosher Salt & Flaky Salt, _to taste_

### Smoked Trout Rillettes

- ~2 cups Smoked Trout Rillettes (from prep)
- 1 box Seeded Crackers _(or grilled baguette)_
- 1 bunch Radishes, halved or sliced
- 2 Persian Cucumbers, sliced
- 1 Lemon, cut into wedges
- Fresh Dill, _for garnish_

---

## Instructions

### On Prep Day:

**Make the Rillettes**
1. Make the **smoked trout rillettes** ahead — see `components/smoked-trout-rillettes.md`. Chill until serving.

**Marinate the Olives**
2. Gently warm the **olives** in a small saucepan with ¼ cup olive oil, 2 smashed **garlic cloves**, a strip of **lemon zest**, ½ tsp **fennel seed**, and a pinch of **red pepper flakes** over low heat for 5 minutes (do not fry). Cool, then refrigerate to marinate overnight.

### Day Of:

**Marinate the Tomatoes**
3. Up to 1 hour before serving, toss the diced **heirloom tomatoes** with the torn **basil**, **olive oil**, **red wine vinegar**, and a pinch of **kosher salt**. Let sit at room temperature (don't refrigerate — cold dulls tomatoes).

**Grill the Bread (as guests arrive)**
4. Brush the **sourdough** slices with olive oil and grill or broil until crisp and charred at the edges, 1–2 minutes per side.

### Plate & Serve

1. **The Board:** Pile the **marinated olives** (slotted, with a little of their oil), **Marcona almonds**, and **Manchego** shards on a large board or platter. Set out first — it needs zero last-minute work.

2. **Bruschetta:** Just before serving, spread each warm grilled slice with a generous smear of **white bean smash**, spoon over the **marinated tomatoes** (let some juice soak in), and finish with **flaky salt** and a thread of **olive oil**. Arrange on a platter.

3. **Rillettes:** Mound the **smoked trout rillettes** in a shallow bowl, drag a spoon across the top, and drizzle with olive oil. Surround with **seeded crackers**, **radishes**, sliced **cucumber**, and **lemon wedges**. Shower with **dill**.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Assemble the bruschetta at the last minute — smear-and-top only when guests are walking in, or the bread goes soggy. And label the rillettes: it's the one item the expecting guest should skip (refrigerated smoked fish), or bake it into a warm dip (see the component card) to make it safe for the whole table.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** A chilled dry rosé or a crisp Vermentino. Pour sparkling water with lemon or a chilled grape juice spritzer for the expecting guest and anyone not drinking.
