# Wild Salmon with Salsa Verde

_The pescatarian main — the same plate as the tri-tip, with salmon in place of the beef_

_Serves 2 (plus 2 extra fillets prepped as a buffer)_

---

## Prepped Ingredients

> From your make-ahead prep:
>
> - Charred Corn Succotash — `components/charred-corn-succotash.md`
> - Salsa Verde — `components/salsa-verde.md`
> - Grilled Zucchini (from `m3-santa-maria-tri-tip.md` — grill enough for all 8 plates)

---

## Ingredients

- 4 Wild Salmon Fillets (6 oz / 170 g each), skin-on — _2 to serve + 2 as a buffer_
- 1 Tbsp Olive Oil
- Kosher Salt & Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_
- ½ Lemon
- ~½ cup Salsa Verde (from prep) — ~2 Tbsp per plate
- 1 batch Charred Corn Succotash, warm (shared)
- Grilled Zucchini (shared)
- Flaky Salt & Fresh Basil, _for finishing_

---

## Instructions

### Day Of

1. Pat the **salmon** dry and season with **salt** and **pepper**. Preheat oven to **400°F**.

2. Heat an oven-safe skillet over medium-high with the **olive oil**. Place the salmon skin-side down and sear undisturbed, 3 minutes, until the skin crisps.

3. Transfer the skillet to the oven and roast 6–8 minutes, until the salmon reaches **145°F** and flakes easily — **fully cooked through**, which is the pregnancy-safe doneness. Finish with a squeeze of **lemon**.

> **Note:** Roast all 4 fillets so you have a comfortable buffer — extra portions are delicious flaked over the succotash, or chilled for lunch the next day.

### Plate & Serve

1. Spoon a bed of warm **charred corn succotash** onto the plate — identical to the tri-tip plates so the pescatarian course looks right at home at the table.

2. Set a **salmon fillet** on top. Lay 2–3 pieces of **grilled zucchini** alongside.

3. Spoon **~2 Tbsp salsa verde** over the salmon. Finish with **flaky salt** and torn **basil**.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Wild salmon is leaner than farmed and overcooks fast — start checking at 6 minutes. Cooking to 145°F keeps it safe for the expecting guest while still moist.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** The same rosé from earlier carries beautifully across to the salmon.
