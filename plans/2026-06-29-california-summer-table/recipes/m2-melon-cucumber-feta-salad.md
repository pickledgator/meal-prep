# Melon, Cucumber & Feta Salad

_with Mint, Chili & Lime_

_Serves 8_

---

## Prepped Ingredients

> From your make-ahead prep:
>
> - Chili-Lime Dressing — `components/chili-lime-dressing.md`
> - Toasted pistachios
> - Cubed melon & sliced cucumbers (best cut the morning/afternoon of)

---

## Ingredients

### The Salad

- 1 medium Cantaloupe (~7 cups cubed), _optionally mixed with watermelon or honeydew_
- 4 Persian Cucumbers, sliced on the diagonal
- 6 oz (170 g) Feta (pasteurized), crumbled or cubed
- ⅓ cup Fresh Mint, torn
- 2 Fresno Chilies, thinly sliced (seeds removed for less heat)
- ½ cup (~2½ oz) Shelled Pistachios, toasted & roughly chopped
- ⅔ cup Chili-Lime Dressing (from prep) — see `components/chili-lime-dressing.md`

---

## Instructions

### On Prep Day:

1. Toast the **pistachios** in a dry skillet until fragrant; cool and chop.

2. Make the **chili-lime dressing** — see `components/chili-lime-dressing.md`. Refrigerate.

### Day Of:

3. Cube the **melon** and slice the **cucumbers**; keep chilled and well-drained (pat the melon dry if very juicy).

### Plate & Serve

1. On a large platter, scatter the **melon** and **cucumbers**. Tuck in the **feta** and half the sliced **Fresno**.

2. Spoon the **chili-lime dressing** over just before serving (dress too early and the melon weeps).

3. Shower with torn **mint**, the toasted **pistachios**, and the remaining **Fresno**. Finish with a pinch of flaky salt.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Salt the melon lightly — a little salinity makes it taste sweeter and ties it to the feta. Dress at the very last second to keep everything crisp.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** A great palate-resetting course before the rich main — keep the same rosé or pour a dry Riesling.
