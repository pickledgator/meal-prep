# Prep List

_Dinner Party · Friday, July 3, 2026 · 8 guests · Difficulty: Challenging_

**Goal:** By the time guests arrive, everything is cooked or chilled — the only jobs left are grilling the bread, searing the meat, roasting the salmon, and plating. Two sessions: a ~90-minute Day-Before push, then a mostly-hands-off Day-Of.

---

## Day Before (Thursday) — ⏱ ~90 min

_Long-keeping, make-ahead-stable items — get these fully done._

☐ **Make the Santa Maria Rub** — see `components/santa-maria-rub.md`³

☐ **Rub & Dry-Brine the Tri-Tips** — coat both roasts in rub, set on a rack over a sheet pan, refrigerate **uncovered overnight**³ 🫙

☐ **Make the Salsa Verde** (yield ~1½ cups) — see `components/salsa-verde.md`³

☐ **Make the White Bean Smash** (yield ~2½ cups) — see `components/white-bean-smash.md`¹

☐ **Make the Smoked Trout Rillettes** (yield ~2 cups) — see `components/smoked-trout-rillettes.md`¹

☐ **Char the Corn** (6 ears; grill/blister, cool, cut kernels, store) — see `components/charred-corn-succotash.md`³ 🫙

☐ **Marinate the Olives** (warm with oil, garlic, lemon zest, fennel seed, chili; cool & refrigerate)¹

☐ **Toast the Pistachios** (½ cup; cool)²

☐ **Make the Chili-Lime Dressing** — see `components/chili-lime-dressing.md`²

## Day Of (Friday)

### Afternoon — Prep Produce (⏱ ~40 min)

- ☐ Cherry Tomatoes (1 lb; halve)³ 🫙
- ☐ Shallot (1; finely dice³) | Garlic (2 cloves; mince³) 🫙
- ☐ Zucchini (4; slice lengthwise ¼")³
- ☐ Radishes (halve or slice¹) | Persian Cucumbers (2 for the rillettes¹ | 4 sliced on diagonal for the salad²)
- ☐ Cantaloupe (cube, ~7 cups²) | Fresno (2; thinly slice²) | Feta (6 oz; crumble²) | Mint (pick & tear²)
- ☐ Lemons & Limes (wedges for the rillettes & serving; juice as needed)¹²³

### Afternoon — Cooked Prep

☐ **Grill the Zucchini** (mark, season, finish with lemon; hold at room temp — enough for all 8 plates)³

☐ **Assemble the Succotash** (sauté shallot + garlic in butter, add charred corn + beans + tomatoes, finish lemon + basil) — see `components/charred-corn-succotash.md`³ _(reheat gently at service)_

☐ **Slice the Sourdough** (½"; ready to grill later)¹

### ~90 min before guests — Start the Meat

☐ **Roast the Tri-Tips** at 250°F to internal **115–120°F** (~45–60 min); pull, tent, and rest³ — see `m3-santa-maria-tri-tip.md`

### As Guests Arrive → Plating (à la minute)

☐ **Grill the sourdough & assemble the Bruschetta** (smash + marinated tomatoes)¹
☐ **Plate the Olive / Almond / Manchego board**¹
☐ **Set out the Smoked Trout Rillettes** with crackers, radishes, cucumber & lemon (label it — the expecting guest skips this, or warm it into a baked dip)¹
→ _serve **Heavy Apps**_

☐ **Dress & plate the Melon Salad**²
→ _serve **First Course**_

☐ **Sear the Tri-Tips** hot to ~130°F MR (sear one end portion to 155–160°F for the expecting guest); rest 10 min, slice against the grain³
☐ **Roast the Salmon** at 400°F to **145°F** (~10–12 min; all 4 fillets)³
☐ **Reheat succotash & zucchini; plate the Mains** (succotash bed → protein → zucchini → salsa verde drizzle → flaky salt + basil)³
→ _serve **Main Course**_
