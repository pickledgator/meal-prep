## Prep Produce (⏱ 25 minutes)

- ☐ Garlic (11 cloves; peel and mince¹²³ | 2 cloves; peel and leave whole¹)🫙
- ☐ Fresh Ginger (2-inch piece; peel and grate)²🫙
- ☐ Orange (2; juice¹ | 2; segment for slaw³)
- ☐ Lime (4; juice¹²³ | 2; cut into wedges²)
- ☐ Scallions (4; slice thinly¹² | 2; slice for garnish³)
- ☐ Fresh Cilantro (1 bunch; divide — ½ cup chopped for components¹²³ | remainder for garnish)🫙
- ☐ Fresh Thyme (6-8 sprigs; pick leaves)¹²🫙
- ☐ Scotch Bonnet Pepper (1; seed and mince finely*)³🫙
- ☐ Broccolini (1 bunch; trim woody ends)¹
- ☐ Lacinato Kale (1 bunch; remove stems, roughly chop)²
- ☐ Green Cabbage (½ small head; shred thinly for slaw)³
- ☐ Cauliflower (1 head; cut into 2 steaks, ~1½" thick)³

*Wear gloves when handling hot peppers and avoid touching your face.
