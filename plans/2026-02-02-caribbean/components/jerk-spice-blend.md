# Jerk Spice Blend

_Makes about 3 Tbsp_

## Ingredients

- 1 Tbsp Brown Sugar
- 1 tsp Smoked Paprika
- 1 tsp Ground Allspice
- 1 tsp Dried Thyme
- 1 tsp Garlic Powder
- 1 tsp Ground Cumin
- ½ tsp Cayenne Pepper
- ½ tsp Ground Cinnamon
- ½ tsp Kosher Salt
- ½ tsp Freshly Ground Black Pepper
- ¼ tsp Ground Nutmeg
- 1 small Scotch Bonnet or Habanero Pepper, seeded and minced very finely (optional, for heat)*

*Wear gloves when handling hot peppers

## Instructions

1. In a small bowl, combine the **brown sugar**, **smoked paprika**, **allspice**, **thyme**, **garlic powder**, **cumin**, **cayenne**, **cinnamon**, **salt**, **black pepper**, and **nutmeg**. Mix well.

2. If using, fold in the **minced Scotch bonnet pepper** to distribute evenly throughout the blend.

3. Store the spice blend in an airtight container until ready to use.

---

**To Store:** Keep in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 1 week, or in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks if fresh pepper is included.

**Used in:** Meal 3 (Jerk-Spiced Cauliflower Steaks)
