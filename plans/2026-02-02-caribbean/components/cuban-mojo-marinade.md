# Cuban Mojo Marinade

_Makes about ¾ cup_

## Ingredients

- Juice of 2 Oranges (~½ cup)
- Juice of 2 Limes (~¼ cup)
- 4 cloves of Garlic, minced
- 2 tsp Dried Oregano
- 1 tsp Ground Cumin
- 1 tsp Kosher Salt
- ½ tsp Freshly Ground Black Pepper
- ¼ cup Olive Oil
- 1 Tbsp Honey
- 1 Tbsp Soy Sauce

## Instructions

1. In a medium bowl, whisk together the **orange juice**, **lime juice**, **minced garlic**, **oregano**, **cumin**, **salt**, and **pepper**.

2. While whisking, slowly drizzle in the **olive oil** to emulsify the marinade.

3. Stir in the **honey** and **soy sauce** until well combined.

4. Place the **chicken thighs** in a large zip-top bag or shallow dish. Pour the mojo marinade over the chicken, turning to coat evenly. Seal or cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours, or up to 24 hours for maximum flavor.

---

**To Store:** Keep chicken in marinade in the refrigerator. Use within 24 hours for best quality.

**Used in:** Meal 1 (Mojo Chicken Thighs)
