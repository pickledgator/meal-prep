# Coconut Rice

_Makes about 4 cups cooked_

## Ingredients

- 1½ cups Long-Grain White Rice
- 1 (13.5 oz / 400 ml) can Full-Fat Coconut Milk
- 1 cup Water
- 2 cloves of Garlic, whole
- 2 sprigs Fresh Thyme
- 1 tsp Kosher Salt
- ½ tsp Freshly Ground Black Pepper

## Instructions

1. Rinse the **rice** under cold water until the water runs clear. Drain well.

2. In a medium saucepan, combine the **rinsed rice**, **coconut milk**, **water**, **whole garlic cloves**, **thyme sprigs**, **salt**, and **pepper**.

3. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat, then reduce heat to low. Cover and simmer for 15-18 minutes, until the rice is tender and has absorbed all the liquid.

4. Remove from heat and let stand, covered, for 5 minutes. Remove the garlic cloves and thyme sprigs. Fluff with a fork.

5. Let the rice cool completely, then transfer to an airtight container and refrigerate until ready to use.

---

**To Store:** Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 5 days. Reheat portions as needed in the microwave or on the stovetop with a splash of water.

**Used in:** Meals 1 and 3 (Mojo Chicken, Jerk Cauliflower)
