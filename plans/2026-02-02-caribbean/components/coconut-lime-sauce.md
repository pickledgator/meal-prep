# Coconut-Lime Sauce

_Makes about 1 cup_

## Ingredients

- 1 (13.5 oz / 400 ml) can Full-Fat Coconut Milk
- Juice of 2 Limes (~¼ cup)
- 1 Tbsp grated Fresh Ginger
- 2 cloves of Garlic, minced
- 1 Tbsp Soy Sauce
- 1 tsp Brown Sugar
- ½ tsp Kosher Salt
- ¼ tsp Crushed Red Pepper Flakes
- 2 Tbsp chopped Fresh Cilantro
- 1 tsp Fresh Thyme Leaves

## Instructions

1. In a medium bowl, whisk together the **coconut milk**, **lime juice**, **grated ginger**, **minced garlic**, **soy sauce**, **brown sugar**, **salt**, and **red pepper flakes** until well combined.

2. Stir in the **chopped cilantro** and **thyme leaves**.

3. Taste and adjust seasoning if needed. The sauce should be creamy with bright citrus notes and a hint of heat.

---

**To Store:** Transfer to an airtight container and store in the refrigerator for up to 5 days. Whisk before using as coconut milk may separate.

**Used in:** Meal 2 (Coconut-Lime Snapper)
