# Citrus-Herb Oil

_Makes about ⅓ cup_

## Ingredients

- ¼ cup Olive Oil
- 2 Tbsp Fresh Lime Juice
- 3 Tbsp chopped Fresh Cilantro
- 1 Tbsp chopped Fresh Thyme Leaves
- 1 clove of Garlic, minced
- ¼ tsp Kosher Salt
- Pinch of Freshly Ground Black Pepper

## Instructions

1. In a small bowl or jar, combine the **olive oil**, **lime juice**, **chopped cilantro**, **thyme**, **minced garlic**, **salt**, and **pepper**.

2. Whisk or shake vigorously until well combined and slightly emulsified.

3. Taste and adjust seasoning if needed. The oil should be bright, herbaceous, and citrusy.

---

**To Store:** Transfer to an airtight jar or container and store in the refrigerator for up to 1 week. Shake or whisk before using as the oil may separate.

**Used in:** Meals 1, 2, and 3 (as a finishing drizzle for all three dishes)
