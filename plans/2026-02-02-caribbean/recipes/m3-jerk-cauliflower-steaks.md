# Jerk-Spiced Cauliflower Steaks

_with Rice & Peas and Orange-Cabbage Slaw_

_Makes 2 servings_

---

## Ingredients

### For the Cauliflower

- 2 Cauliflower Steaks, ~1½" thick (from prep)
- 2 Tbsp Olive Oil
- 2-3 Tbsp Jerk Spice Blend (from prep)
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_

### For the Rice & Peas

- 2 cups Coconut Rice, reheated (from prep)
- 1 (15 oz / 425 g) can Red Kidney Beans, drained and rinsed
- 2 cloves of Garlic, minced
- 2 sprigs Fresh Thyme
- Kosher Salt and Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

### For the Orange-Cabbage Slaw

- 2 cups shredded Green Cabbage (from prep)
- Orange segments from 2 Oranges (from prep)
- 2 Tbsp Fresh Lime Juice
- 1 Tbsp Olive Oil
- 1 tsp Honey
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_
- 2 Tbsp Fresh Cilantro, chopped

### For Finishing

- 2-3 Tbsp Citrus-Herb Oil (from prep)
- 2 Tbsp sliced Scallions, _for garnish_
- Lime wedges, _for serving_

---

## Instructions

### Roast the Cauliflower

1. Preheat the oven to 425°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.

2. Brush both sides of the **cauliflower steaks** with **olive oil**. Generously coat both sides with the **jerk spice blend**, pressing gently to adhere. Season with a pinch of **salt**.

3. Place the cauliflower steaks on the prepared baking sheet. Roast for 20-25 minutes, flipping halfway through, until tender and caramelized at the edges.

> **Hot Tip:** For extra char, broil the cauliflower for the last 2-3 minutes, watching carefully to prevent burning.

### Prepare the Rice & Peas

1. In a medium saucepan over medium heat, combine the **coconut rice**, **kidney beans**, **minced garlic**, and **thyme sprigs**. Add a splash of water if needed to prevent sticking.

2. Cook, stirring occasionally, for 5-7 minutes until heated through. Remove the thyme sprigs and season with **salt** and **pepper** to taste.

### Make the Orange-Cabbage Slaw

1. In a medium bowl, whisk together the **lime juice**, **olive oil**, **honey**, and a pinch of **salt**.

2. Add the **shredded cabbage** and **orange segments**. Toss well to coat. Let sit for 5 minutes to soften slightly.

3. Just before serving, fold in the **chopped cilantro**.

### Plate & Serve

1. Spoon a generous portion of **rice and peas** onto each plate, creating a base.

2. Place a **roasted cauliflower steak** on top of or alongside the rice.

3. Arrange a portion of the **orange-cabbage slaw** next to the cauliflower. Drizzle the cauliflower and rice with **citrus-herb oil**.

4. Garnish with **sliced scallions** and serve with **lime wedges** on the side.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** Pair with a light beer or tropical iced tea with fresh mint.
