_with Coconut Rice and Charred Broccolini_

_Makes 2 servings_

## Ingredients

### For the Chicken

- 4 Bone-In, Skin-On Chicken Thighs, marinated (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp Olive Oil, _for cooking_

### Accompaniments

- 2 cups Coconut Rice, reheated (from prep)
- 1 bunch Broccolini, trimmed (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp Olive Oil, _for broccolini_
- Kosher Salt and Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_
- 2-3 Tbsp Citrus-Herb Oil (from prep), _for finishing_
- 2 Tbsp sliced Scallions (from prep), _for garnish_
- 2 Tbsp Fresh Cilantro, _for garnish_
- Lime wedges, _for serving_

---

## Instructions

### Cook the Chicken

1. Remove the **marinated chicken thighs** from the refrigerator and let them come to room temperature for 10 minutes. Pat dry with paper towels.

2. Heat a large oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat. Add 1 Tbsp **olive oil**. Once shimmering, place the **chicken thighs** skin-side down in the pan. Cook without moving for 5-7 minutes, until the skin is deeply golden and crispy.

3. Flip the chicken and cook for another 3-4 minutes. Transfer the skillet to a preheated 375°F oven and roast for 12-15 minutes, until the internal temperature reaches 165°F.

4. Remove from the oven and let rest for 5 minutes before serving.

> **Hot Tip:** Pat the chicken dry before cooking to ensure crispy skin. Don't move it while searing—let it develop a golden crust naturally.

### Char the Broccolini

1. While the chicken is roasting, heat a large skillet over high heat. Add 1 Tbsp **olive oil**.

2. Add the **broccolini** to the hot pan and cook without stirring for 2-3 minutes, allowing it to char. Toss and cook for another 2 minutes until tender-crisp and charred in spots. Season with **salt** and **pepper**.

### Plate & Serve

1. Spoon a generous portion of **coconut rice** onto each plate. Place 2 **chicken thighs** on top of or alongside the rice.

2. Arrange the **charred broccolini** next to the chicken. Drizzle everything with the **citrus-herb oil**.

3. Garnish with **sliced scallions** and **fresh cilantro**. Serve with **lime wedges** on the side.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** Pair with a crisp lager or a tropical fruit juice.
