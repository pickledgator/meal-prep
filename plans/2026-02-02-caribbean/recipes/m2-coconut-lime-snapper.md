# Coconut-Lime Snapper

_with Black Beans and Garlicky Kale_

_Makes 2 servings_

---

## Ingredients

### For the Snapper

- 2 (6 oz / 170 g) Red Snapper Fillets
- Kosher Salt and Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_
- 1 Tbsp Olive Oil, _for cooking_
- 1 cup Coconut-Lime Sauce (from prep)

### For the Black Beans

- 1 (15 oz / 425 g) can Black Beans, drained and rinsed
- 2 cloves of Garlic, minced (from prep)
- ½ tsp Ground Cumin
- ¼ cup Chicken Stock
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_

### For the Kale

- 1 bunch Lacinato Kale, chopped (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp Olive Oil
- 2 cloves of Garlic, minced (from prep)
- Kosher Salt and Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_
- Squeeze of Fresh Lime Juice

### For Finishing

- 2-3 Tbsp Citrus-Herb Oil (from prep)
- 2 Tbsp sliced Scallions (from prep), _for garnish_
- 2 Tbsp Fresh Cilantro, _for garnish_
- Lime wedges, _for serving_

---

## Instructions

### Prepare the Black Beans

1. In a small saucepan over medium heat, add a drizzle of olive oil. Add the **minced garlic** and sauté for 30 seconds until fragrant.

2. Add the **black beans**, **cumin**, and **chicken stock**. Simmer for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the beans are heated through and the liquid has reduced slightly. Season with **salt** to taste. Keep warm.

### Sauté the Kale

1. In a large skillet over medium-high heat, add 1 Tbsp **olive oil**. Add the **minced garlic** and sauté for 30 seconds.

2. Add the **chopped kale** and sauté for 3-4 minutes, tossing frequently, until wilted and tender. Season with **salt**, **pepper**, and a squeeze of **lime juice**. Set aside and keep warm.

### Cook the Snapper

1. Pat the **snapper fillets** dry with paper towels. Season both sides generously with **salt** and **pepper**.

2. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add 1 Tbsp **olive oil**. Once shimmering, carefully place the **snapper fillets** skin-side down (if skin-on) in the pan.

3. Cook for 3-4 minutes without moving, until the skin is crispy and golden. Carefully flip and cook for another 2-3 minutes until the fish is opaque and flakes easily with a fork.

4. Reduce heat to low and pour the **coconut-lime sauce** over the fish. Let it simmer gently for 1-2 minutes to heat the sauce through.

> **Hot Tip:** Don't overcook the snapper—it should be just opaque in the center. The residual heat will finish cooking it as it rests.

### Plate & Serve

1. Spoon a portion of **black beans** onto each plate, creating a base. Arrange the **sautéed kale** alongside.

2. Place a **snapper fillet** on top of the beans. Spoon the warm **coconut-lime sauce** over the fish and around the plate.

3. Drizzle with **citrus-herb oil** and garnish with **sliced scallions** and **fresh cilantro**. Serve with **lime wedges** on the side.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** Pair with a crisp white wine like Sauvignon Blanc or a light rum cocktail.
