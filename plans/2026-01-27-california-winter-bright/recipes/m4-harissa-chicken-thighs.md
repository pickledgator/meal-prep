# Harissa Chicken Thighs

_with Roasted Fennel & Blood Orange-Olive Relish_

_Makes 2 servings_

---

## Prepped Ingredients

> From your Sunday prep:
>
> - Harissa Marinade — `components/harissa-marinade.md` (chicken already marinating)
> - Blood Orange-Olive Relish — `components/blood-orange-olive-relish.md`
> - Fennel (sliced into wedges)
> - Reserved fennel fronds

---

## Ingredients

### For the Chicken

- 4 Bone-In Skin-On Chicken Thighs (~1.5 lb / 680 g total), marinated (from prep)
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

### For the Roasted Fennel

- 2 medium Fennel Bulbs, sliced into ½" wedges (from prep)
- 2 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

### For Serving

- Blood Orange-Olive Relish (from prep), about ¼ cup per serving
- Reserved Fennel Fronds, roughly chopped
- 1 Blood Orange, sliced into rounds
- Extra-Virgin Olive Oil, _for drizzling_
- Flaky Salt, _for finishing_

---

## Instructions

### Roast the Fennel

1. Preheat oven to **400°F**. Line a large sheet pan with parchment paper.

2. Toss **fennel wedges** with **olive oil**, **salt**, and **pepper**. Arrange in a single layer on the sheet pan.

3. Roast for **15 minutes** to get a head start on caramelization.

### Roast the Chicken

4. Remove **marinated chicken thighs** from refrigerator and let sit at room temperature while fennel starts roasting.

5. After fennel has roasted 15 minutes, push wedges to the edges of the pan. Place chicken thighs **skin-side up** in the center.

6. Return pan to oven and roast for **30-35 minutes** until chicken skin is deeply golden and crispy, and internal temperature reaches **175°F**. The fennel should be caramelized and tender.

7. Let chicken rest for **5 minutes** before serving.

> **Hot Tip:** For extra-crispy skin, pat the chicken dry before marinating, and don't disturb it during roasting. Let the oven do the work.

### Plate & Serve

8. Arrange **roasted fennel wedges** on one side of each plate, fanning them slightly.

9. Place **2 chicken thighs** per plate alongside the fennel, skin-side up to show off that crispy top.

10. Spoon **blood orange-olive relish** generously over and around the chicken—let it pool on the plate.

11. Tuck a few **blood orange slices** alongside for color.

12. Scatter **fennel fronds** over everything. Drizzle with **olive oil** and finish with **flaky salt**.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** A glass of rosé from Provence or a light Grenache showcases the North African spices and bright citrus beautifully.
