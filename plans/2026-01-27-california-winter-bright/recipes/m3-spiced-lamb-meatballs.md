# Spiced Lamb Meatballs

_with Tahini-Yogurt Sauce & Crispy Kale_

_Makes 2 servings_

---

## Prepped Ingredients

> From your Sunday prep:
>
> - Tahini-Yogurt Sauce — `components/tahini-yogurt-sauce.md`
> - Cooked Farro (from prep list)
> - Lacinato Kale (stemmed and torn)
> - Garlic (2 cloves minced for meatballs)
> - Lemon zest and juice
> - Fresh mint and parsley (chopped)

---

## Ingredients

### For the Meatballs

- 1 lb (454 g) Ground Lamb
- ¼ cup (15 g) Panko Breadcrumbs
- 1 Egg, lightly beaten
- 2 cloves Garlic, minced (from prep)
- 1 tsp Ground Cumin
- ½ tsp Ground Coriander
- ¼ tsp Ground Cinnamon
- 1 tsp Lemon Zest (from prep)
- 2 Tbsp Fresh Parsley, finely chopped (from prep)
- ¾ tsp Kosher Salt
- ¼ tsp Freshly Ground Black Pepper

### For the Crispy Kale

- 1 bunch Lacinato Kale (~8 oz / 227 g), stemmed and torn (from prep)
- 2 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- ¼ tsp Kosher Salt

### For Serving

- ¾ cup Cooked Farro, warmed (from prep)
- Tahini-Yogurt Sauce (from prep), 3-4 Tbsp per serving
- 2 Tbsp Fresh Mint, chopped (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp Fresh Parsley, chopped (from prep)
- Flaky Salt, _for finishing_
- Extra-Virgin Olive Oil, _for drizzling_

---

## Instructions

### On Prep Day (Optional):
**Form the Meatballs**

1. In a large bowl, combine **ground lamb**, **panko**, **beaten egg**, **garlic**, **cumin**, **coriander**, **cinnamon**, **lemon zest**, **parsley**, **salt**, and **pepper**.

2. Mix gently with your hands until just combined—do not overmix or meatballs will be tough.

3. Using damp hands, form mixture into **10-12 meatballs** (about 1½" each). Place on a parchment-lined sheet pan, cover, and refrigerate until ready to cook.

---

### Day Of:
**Roast the Meatballs**

1. Preheat oven to **425°F**. Line a rimmed sheet pan with parchment paper.

2. Arrange **meatballs** on the prepared pan, spacing them 1 inch apart.

3. Roast for **15-18 minutes** until browned on the outside and cooked through (internal temp 160°F). Let rest 5 minutes.

### Crisp the Kale

4. While meatballs roast, heat **olive oil** in a large skillet over medium-high heat.

5. Add **kale** in batches, tossing with tongs as it wilts. Cook for **4-5 minutes** until edges are crispy and leaves are tender. Season with **salt**.

> **Hot Tip:** Don't crowd the pan—kale needs space to crisp rather than steam. Work in batches if needed.

### Plate & Serve

6. Spoon **warmed farro** into the center of each shallow bowl as a base.

7. Arrange **5-6 meatballs** on top of and around the farro.

8. Tuck **crispy kale** alongside the meatballs.

9. Drizzle generously with **tahini-yogurt sauce**, creating swooshes and pools.

10. Scatter **fresh mint** and **parsley** over everything. Finish with a drizzle of **olive oil** and **flaky salt**.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** A medium-bodied red like a Côtes du Rhône or Grenache pairs wonderfully with the warm spices.
