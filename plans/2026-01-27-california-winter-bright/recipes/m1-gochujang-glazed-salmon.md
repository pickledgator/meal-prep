# Gochujang-Glazed Salmon

_with Sesame Rice & Roasted Broccolini_

_Makes 2 servings_

---

## Prepped Ingredients

> From your Sunday prep:
>
> - Gochujang Glaze — `components/gochujang-glaze.md`
> - Sesame Rice — `components/sesame-rice.md`
> - Broccolini (trimmed)
> - Scallions (sliced)

---

## Ingredients

### For the Salmon

- 2 Salmon Fillets (6 oz / 170 g each), skin-on
- 2 Tbsp Gochujang Glaze (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp Neutral Oil
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

### For the Broccolini

- 1 bunch Broccolini (6 oz / 170 g), trimmed (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_

### For Serving

- Sesame Rice, reheated (from prep)
- 2 Scallions, thinly sliced (from prep)
- 1 tsp Sesame Seeds
- Flaky Salt, _for finishing_

---

## Instructions

### Roast the Broccolini

1. Preheat oven to **425°F**. Line a sheet pan with parchment paper.

2. Toss **broccolini** with **olive oil** and spread in a single layer on the prepared pan. Season with **salt**.

3. Roast for **12-15 minutes** until stems are tender and florets are lightly charred at the edges. Set aside.

### Sear & Glaze the Salmon

4. While broccolini roasts, pat **salmon fillets** very dry with paper towels. Season generously with **salt** and **pepper** on the flesh side.

5. Heat **neutral oil** in a large oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering.

6. Place salmon **skin-side up** in the pan. Sear without moving for **3-4 minutes** until a golden crust forms.

7. Flip salmon to skin-side down. Brush the top generously with **gochujang glaze**.

8. Transfer skillet to the oven (alongside the broccolini if timing aligns) and roast for **5-7 minutes** until salmon is cooked to your liking (125°F for medium, 135°F for medium-well).

> **Hot Tip:** Double-brush for maximum flavor impact—brush once before the oven, then brush again with remaining glaze right when it comes out.

### Plate & Serve

9. Spoon a mound of **sesame rice** into the center of each plate. Nestle the glazed salmon alongside.

10. Arrange **roasted broccolini** on the plate, leaning against the rice.

11. Scatter **scallions** (green parts) and **sesame seeds** over the salmon and rice. Finish with **flaky salt**.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** A chilled glass of off-dry Riesling or a crisp Grüner Veltliner balances the sweet heat beautifully.
