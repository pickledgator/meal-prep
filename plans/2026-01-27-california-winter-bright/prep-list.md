# Sunday Prep List

_Difficulty: Normal_

---

## Long-Running Tasks First

☐ **Cook Farro** (½ cup dry; according to package, ~25-30 minutes)³
Start this first so it can simmer while you prep produce.

☐ **Cook Sesame Rice** — see `components/sesame-rice.md`¹
Start rice cooker or stovetop method (~20 minutes).

---

## Prep Produce (⏱ 25 minutes)

- ☐ Broccolini (1 bunch; trim ends, halve any thick stalks lengthwise)¹
- ☐ Brussels Sprouts (8 oz; trim ends, halve through root)²
- ☐ Lacinato Kale (1 bunch; wash, remove stems, tear into bite-sized pieces — yield ~6 cups)³
- ☐ Fennel (2 bulbs; trim tops, reserve fronds, quarter and slice into ½" wedges)⁴
- ☐ Blood Oranges (2; supreme into segments, reserve juice)⁴🫙
- ☐ Garlic (10 cloves; peel — 2 mince for glaze¹, 2 slice thin², 2 mince for meatballs³, 2 mince for marinade⁴, 2 mince for sauce³)🫙
- ☐ Fresh Ginger (1" piece; peel and mince)¹🫙
- ☐ Lemon (2; zest 1 lemon³, juice both — yield ~4 Tbsp)¹³🫙
- ☐ Scallions (2; thinly slice, separate white and green parts)¹
- ☐ Fresh Parsley (1 bunch; wash, finely chop — yield ~½ cup)³⁴🫙
- ☐ Fresh Mint (1 small bunch; wash, finely chop — yield ~3 Tbsp)³

---

_— CLEANING BREAK —_

---

## Prepare Components

☐ **Gochujang Glaze** — see `components/gochujang-glaze.md`¹
Whisk together and store in jar (yield: ~¼ cup).

☐ **Harissa Marinade** — see `components/harissa-marinade.md`⁴
Combine harissa with olive oil, garlic, and honey. Coat chicken thighs and refrigerate.

☐ **Tahini-Yogurt Sauce** — see `components/tahini-yogurt-sauce.md`³
Whisk together and refrigerate (yield: ~¾ cup).

☐ **Blood Orange-Olive Relish** — see `components/blood-orange-olive-relish.md`⁴
Combine supremed oranges with olives and parsley. Store in refrigerator.

---

## Form Meatballs (Optional)

☐ **Lamb Meatballs** — see recipe `recipes/m3-spiced-lamb-meatballs.md`³
Mix lamb with spices, egg, and panko. Form into 10-12 meatballs and refrigerate on parchment-lined sheet pan. _(Can also be done day-of.)_
