# Sesame Rice

_Makes about 2 cups cooked_

## Ingredients

- ¾ cup (150 g) Long Grain White Rice
- 1½ cups Water
- ½ tsp Kosher Salt
- 1 tsp Toasted Sesame Oil
- 1 Tbsp Sesame Seeds (white or black)

## Instructions

1. Rinse **rice** in a fine-mesh strainer under cold water until water runs clear.

2. Combine rice, **water**, and **salt** in a small saucepan. Bring to a boil over high heat.

3. Reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15-18 minutes until water is absorbed and rice is tender.

4. Remove from heat and let steam, covered, for 5 minutes.

5. Fluff with a fork, then fold in **sesame oil** and **sesame seeds**.

---

**To Store:** Let cool completely, then transfer to an airtight container. Refrigerate for up to 4 days. Reheat with a splash of water in the microwave or on the stovetop.

**Used in:** Meal 1 (Gochujang-Glazed Salmon)
