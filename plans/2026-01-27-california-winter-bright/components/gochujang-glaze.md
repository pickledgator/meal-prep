# Gochujang Glaze

_Makes about ¼ cup_

## Ingredients

- 2 Tbsp Gochujang
- 1 Tbsp Low-Sodium Soy Sauce
- 1 Tbsp Honey
- 1 tsp Rice Vinegar
- 1 tsp Toasted Sesame Oil
- 2 cloves Garlic, minced
- 1 tsp Fresh Ginger, minced

## Instructions

1. In a small bowl, whisk together **gochujang**, **soy sauce**, **honey**, **rice vinegar**, and **sesame oil** until smooth.

2. Add the **minced garlic** and **ginger**, stirring to combine.

3. Taste and adjust sweetness or heat as needed.

---

**To Store:** Transfer to a small jar or airtight container. Refrigerate for up to 1 week.

**Used in:** Meal 1 (Gochujang-Glazed Salmon)
