# Harissa Marinade

_Makes about ¼ cup (enough for 4 chicken thighs)_

## Ingredients

- 2 Tbsp Harissa Paste
- 2 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- 2 cloves Garlic, minced
- 1 tsp Honey
- ½ tsp Kosher Salt

## Instructions

1. In a small bowl, whisk together **harissa paste**, **olive oil**, **minced garlic**, **honey**, and **salt** until well combined.

2. Place **chicken thighs** in a shallow dish or large zip-top bag. Pour marinade over chicken, turning to coat all sides.

3. Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours, or overnight for best results.

---

**To Store:** Best used immediately as a marinade. If making ahead without chicken, store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 1 week.

**Used in:** Meal 4 (Harissa Chicken Thighs)
