# Tahini-Yogurt Sauce

_Makes about ¾ cup_

## Ingredients

- 3 Tbsp Tahini
- ½ cup (120 g) Plain Greek Yogurt
- 2 Tbsp Fresh Lemon Juice
- 1 clove Garlic, minced
- 2 Tbsp Warm Water, plus more as needed
- ¼ tsp Kosher Salt
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

## Instructions

1. In a medium bowl, whisk together **tahini** and **Greek yogurt** until smooth.

2. Add the **lemon juice** and **minced garlic**, whisking to combine.

3. Drizzle in **warm water** while whisking until the sauce reaches a drizzleable consistency. Add more water 1 Tbsp at a time if needed.

4. Season with **salt** and **pepper**. Taste and adjust acidity or seasoning as desired.

---

**To Store:** Transfer to an airtight container and refrigerate for up to 5 days. Sauce may thicken as it sits; thin with a splash of water before serving.
