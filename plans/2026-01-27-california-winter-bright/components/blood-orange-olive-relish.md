# Blood Orange-Olive Relish

_Makes about ½ cup_

## Ingredients

- 2 Blood Oranges, supremed (segments and juice reserved)
- ½ cup (75 g) Castelvetrano Olives, pitted and roughly chopped
- 2 Tbsp Fresh Parsley, finely chopped
- 1 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- Pinch of Red Pepper Flakes, _optional_
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

## Instructions

1. **Supreme the oranges**: Cut off the top and bottom of each **blood orange**. Stand the orange on one end and cut away the peel and white pith, following the curve of the fruit. Working over a bowl to catch juices, cut between the membranes to release the segments. Squeeze remaining juice from membranes into the bowl.

2. Roughly chop the orange segments into bite-sized pieces and add them back to the bowl with the juice.

3. Add the **chopped olives**, **parsley**, and **olive oil**. Toss gently to combine.

4. Season with **red pepper flakes** (if using), **salt**, and **pepper**. The olives are salty, so taste before adding more salt.

---

**To Store:** Transfer to an airtight container and refrigerate for up to 3 days. The relish is best served at room temperature; remove from refrigerator 15 minutes before serving.

**Used in:** Meal 4 (Harissa Chicken Thighs)
