# Za'atar Chicken Thighs

_with Roasted Cauliflower & Crispy Kale_

_Makes 2 servings_

---

## Prepped Ingredients

> From your Sunday prep:
>
> - Lemon-Tahini Dressing — `components/lemon-tahini-dressing.md`
> - Roasted cauliflower (½ of total, ~3 cups)
> - Lacinato kale (washed, stems removed, torn — ⅓ of total)

---

## Ingredients

### For the Chicken

- 4 Bone-In Skin-On Chicken Thighs (~1.5 lb / 680 g total)
- 2 Tbsp Za'atar
- 2 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

### For the Sheet Pan Vegetables

- 3 cups Roasted Cauliflower (from prep)
- 4 cups Lacinato Kale, torn (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_

### For Serving

- 3-4 Tbsp Lemon-Tahini Dressing (from prep)
- 2 Tbsp Fresh Parsley, chopped (from prep)
- Lemon wedges
- Flaky Salt, _for finishing_

---

## Instructions

### Season & Roast the Chicken

1. Preheat oven to **425°F**. Line a large sheet pan with parchment paper.

2. Pat **chicken thighs** very dry with paper towels. Rub all over with **olive oil**, then season generously with **salt**, **pepper**, and **za'atar**, pressing the za'atar into the skin.

3. Place chicken thighs **skin-side up** on one half of the sheet pan, leaving room for vegetables.

4. Roast for **20 minutes**.

### Add Vegetables to Sheet Pan

5. While chicken roasts, toss **kale** with **1 Tbsp olive oil** and a pinch of **salt**.

6. After 20 minutes, add the **pre-roasted cauliflower** and **kale** to the other half of the sheet pan. The kale will crisp up.

7. Continue roasting for **15-20 minutes** until chicken skin is deeply golden and crispy, internal temp reaches **175°F**, and kale edges are crispy.

8. Let chicken rest for **5 minutes**.

> **Hot Tip:** For maximum crispy kale, spread it in a single layer — overcrowding leads to steaming instead of crisping.

### Plate & Serve

9. Divide **roasted cauliflower** and **crispy kale** between two plates, arranging them as a base.

10. Place **2 chicken thighs** per plate on top of the vegetables.

11. Drizzle **lemon-tahini dressing** over the chicken and vegetables.

12. Scatter **fresh parsley** over everything.

13. Serve with **lemon wedges** and finish with **flaky salt**.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** A glass of Côtes du Rhône or Grenache-based rosé complements the Middle Eastern spices beautifully.
