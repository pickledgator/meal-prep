# Lamb Kofta

_with Farro & Charred Shallot Vinaigrette_

_Makes 2 servings_

---

## Prepped Ingredients

> From your Sunday prep:
>
> - Charred Shallot Vinaigrette — `components/charred-shallot-vinaigrette.md`
> - Cooked farro (1½ cups)
> - Roasted cauliflower (½ of total, ~3 cups)
> - Fresh parsley and mint (chopped)

---

## Ingredients

### For the Lamb Kofta

- ¾ lb (340 g) Ground Lamb
- 2 cloves Garlic, minced
- 2 Tbsp Fresh Parsley, finely chopped (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp Fresh Mint, finely chopped (from prep)
- 1 tsp Ground Cumin
- ½ tsp Ground Coriander
- 2 Tbsp Panko Breadcrumbs
- 1 Egg, lightly beaten
- ½ tsp Kosher Salt
- ¼ tsp Freshly Ground Black Pepper
- 1 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil, _for cooking_

### For Serving

- 1½ cups Cooked Farro, warmed (from prep)
- 3 cups Roasted Cauliflower, warmed (from prep)
- 3-4 Tbsp Charred Shallot Vinaigrette (from prep)
- 2 Tbsp Fresh Parsley, chopped
- 1 Tbsp Fresh Mint, chopped
- Lemon wedges
- Flaky Salt, _for finishing_

---

## Instructions

### On Prep Day (Optional):
**Form the Kofta**

1. In a medium bowl, combine **ground lamb**, **garlic**, **parsley**, **mint**, **cumin**, **coriander**, **panko**, **beaten egg**, **salt**, and **pepper**.

2. Mix gently with your hands until just combined — do not overmix.

3. Divide mixture into **8 portions**. Shape each into an oval patty or small torpedo shape.

4. Place on a parchment-lined plate, cover, and refrigerate until ready to cook.

---

### Day Of:
**Cook the Kofta**

1. Remove kofta from refrigerator 10 minutes before cooking to take the chill off.

2. Heat **1 Tbsp olive oil** in a large skillet over medium-high heat.

3. Add kofta and cook for **3-4 minutes per side** until browned and cooked through (internal temp 160°F).

4. Transfer to a plate and let rest for 2-3 minutes.

> **Hot Tip:** Don't press down on the kofta while cooking — let them develop a nice crust undisturbed.

### Warm the Components

5. In the microwave or a small saucepan, warm the **farro** and **roasted cauliflower** separately until heated through.

### Plate & Serve

6. Spoon **warm farro** into the center of each plate, spreading it slightly.

7. Arrange **roasted cauliflower** alongside and on top of the farro.

8. Place **4 lamb kofta** per plate, nestled among the vegetables and grains.

9. Drizzle **charred shallot vinaigrette** generously over everything — let it pool on the plate.

10. Scatter **fresh parsley** and **mint** over the top.

11. Serve with **lemon wedges** and finish with **flaky salt**.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** A medium-bodied red like a Lebanese red blend or Syrah from the Rhône Valley pairs beautifully with the spiced lamb.
