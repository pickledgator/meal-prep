# Shrimp & Farro Bowl

_with Massaged Kale & Citrus_

_Makes 2 servings_

---

## Prepped Ingredients

> From your Sunday prep:
>
> - Lemon-Tahini Dressing — `components/lemon-tahini-dressing.md`
> - Cooked farro (1½ cups)
> - Lacinato kale (washed, stems removed, torn — ⅓ of total)
> - Cherry tomatoes (halved)
> - Garlic (2 cloves, minced)

---

## Ingredients

### For the Shrimp

- ¾ lb (340 g) Large Shrimp, peeled & deveined
- 2 cloves Garlic, minced (from prep)
- 2 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- Juice of ½ Lemon
- ¼ tsp Red Pepper Flakes
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

### For the Bowl

- 1½ cups Cooked Farro (from prep)
- 4 cups Lacinato Kale, torn (from prep)
- 1 cup Cherry Tomatoes, halved (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil (for massaging kale)
- 1 Tbsp Lemon Juice (for massaging kale)
- Pinch of Kosher Salt

### For Serving

- 3-4 Tbsp Lemon-Tahini Dressing (from prep)
- 2 Tbsp Fresh Parsley, chopped (from prep)
- Lemon wedges
- Flaky Salt, _for finishing_

---

## Instructions

### Massage the Kale (if not done during prep)

1. Place **kale** in a large bowl. Drizzle with **1 Tbsp olive oil**, **1 Tbsp lemon juice**, and a pinch of **salt**.

2. Using your hands, massage the kale for **2-3 minutes** until it softens, darkens in color, and reduces in volume by about half.

### Cook the Shrimp

3. Pat **shrimp** dry with paper towels. Season with **salt** and **pepper**.

4. Heat **2 Tbsp olive oil** in a large skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering.

5. Add shrimp in a single layer. Cook without moving for **1-2 minutes** until pink on the bottom.

6. Flip shrimp, add **minced garlic** and **red pepper flakes**. Cook another **1-2 minutes** until shrimp are pink and cooked through.

7. Remove from heat and squeeze **lemon juice** over the shrimp.

> **Hot Tip:** Don't overcrowd the pan — cook shrimp in batches if needed. Overcrowding leads to steaming, not searing.

### Warm the Farro

8. In a small saucepan or microwave, warm the **cooked farro** with a splash of water until heated through.

### Plate & Serve

9. Divide **massaged kale** between two wide, shallow bowls as the base.

10. Spoon **warm farro** over the kale on one side of the bowl.

11. Arrange **cherry tomatoes** alongside the farro.

12. Top with **garlicky shrimp**, nestling them into the bowl.

13. Drizzle **lemon-tahini dressing** generously over everything.

14. Scatter **fresh parsley** over the top.

15. Serve with **lemon wedges** and finish with **flaky salt**.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** A chilled Verdicchio or Sauvignon Blanc complements the bright, Mediterranean flavors.
