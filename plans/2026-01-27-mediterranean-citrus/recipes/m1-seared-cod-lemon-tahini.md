# Seared Cod with Lemon-Tahini

_with Garlicky Kale & Parsley Gremolata_

_Makes 2 servings_

---

## Prepped Ingredients

> From your Sunday prep:
>
> - Lemon-Tahini Dressing — `components/lemon-tahini-dressing.md`
> - Parsley Gremolata — `components/parsley-gremolata.md`
> - Lacinato kale (washed, stems removed, torn — ⅓ of total)
> - Garlic (2 cloves, minced)
> - Shallots (2, thinly sliced)

---

## Ingredients

### For the Cod

- 2 Cod Fillets (6 oz / 170 g each)
- 2 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

### For the Garlicky Kale

- 4 cups Lacinato Kale, torn (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- 2 cloves Garlic, minced (from prep)
- 2 Tbsp Shallots, thinly sliced (from prep)
- Pinch of Red Pepper Flakes
- Squeeze of Lemon Juice
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_

### For Serving

- 3-4 Tbsp Lemon-Tahini Dressing (from prep)
- 2 Tbsp Parsley Gremolata (from prep)
- Flaky Salt, _for finishing_

---

## Instructions

### Sear the Cod

1. Pat **cod fillets** very dry with paper towels. Season generously on both sides with **salt** and **pepper**.

2. Heat **olive oil** in a large skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering.

3. Place cod in the pan and cook without moving for **4-5 minutes** until a golden crust forms on the bottom.

4. Carefully flip and cook another **3-4 minutes** until fish is opaque and flakes easily. Transfer to a plate and tent loosely with foil.

> **Hot Tip:** Don't move the fish while it sears — let the crust develop. You'll know it's ready to flip when it releases easily from the pan.

### Sauté the Kale

5. In the same skillet, add **1 Tbsp olive oil** over medium heat.

6. Add **shallots** and cook for 1 minute until softened.

7. Add **garlic** and **red pepper flakes**, stirring for 30 seconds until fragrant.

8. Add **kale** in batches, tossing with tongs as it wilts. Cook for **3-4 minutes** until tender but still bright green.

9. Remove from heat, squeeze **lemon juice** over the kale, and season with **salt**.

### Plate & Serve

10. Divide **garlicky kale** between two plates, creating a bed in the center.

11. Place a **seared cod fillet** on top of each kale bed.

12. Drizzle **lemon-tahini dressing** generously over the cod and around the plate.

13. Scatter **parsley gremolata** over the fish for brightness and texture.

14. Finish with **flaky salt**.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** A crisp white wine like Vermentino or Albariño pairs beautifully with the bright citrus and creamy tahini.
