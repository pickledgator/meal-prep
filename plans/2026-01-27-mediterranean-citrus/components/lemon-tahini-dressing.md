# Lemon-Tahini Dressing

_Makes about 1 cup_

---

This is your **workhorse sauce** for the week — used in 3 of 4 meals!

## Ingredients

- ½ cup (120 g) Tahini
- ¼ cup (60 ml) Fresh Lemon Juice (from ~2 lemons)
- 4 cloves Garlic, minced
- 2 Tbsp Honey
- ¼ cup (60 ml) Warm Water, plus more as needed
- ½ tsp Kosher Salt
- Pinch of Red Pepper Flakes, _optional_

## Instructions

1. In a medium bowl, whisk together **tahini** and **lemon juice**. The mixture will seize up and become thick — this is normal.

2. Add the **minced garlic**, **honey**, and **salt**. Whisk to combine.

3. Slowly drizzle in **warm water** while whisking constantly until the dressing reaches a smooth, pourable consistency. Add more water 1 Tbsp at a time if needed.

4. Taste and adjust seasoning. Add **red pepper flakes** if desired.

---

**To Store:** Transfer to an airtight jar or container. Refrigerate for up to 1 week. The dressing will thicken as it sits — thin with a splash of warm water before using.

**Used in:**
- Meal 1: Drizzled over seared cod and garlicky kale
- Meal 2: Drizzled over za'atar chicken and roasted cauliflower
- Meal 3: Dressing for shrimp and farro grain bowl
