# Parsley Gremolata

_Makes about ⅓ cup_

## Ingredients

- ½ cup Fresh Parsley, finely chopped
- Zest of 1 Lemon
- 2 Tbsp Shallot, minced
- 1 clove Garlic, minced
- 1 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- Pinch of Kosher Salt

## Instructions

1. In a small bowl, combine **parsley**, **lemon zest**, **minced shallot**, and **garlic**.

2. Drizzle with **olive oil** and add a pinch of **salt**. Toss to combine.

3. Taste and adjust seasoning as needed.

---

**To Store:** Transfer to a small airtight container. Refrigerate for up to 3 days. Best used fresh — the flavors are brightest within 24 hours.

**Used in:** Meal 1 (Seared Cod) — scattered over the finished dish as a bright, fresh topping
