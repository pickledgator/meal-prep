# Charred Shallot Vinaigrette

_Makes about ½ cup_

## Ingredients

- 2 medium Shallots, charred (from prep)
- 2 Tbsp Red Wine Vinegar
- 1 tsp Dijon Mustard
- ¼ cup (60 ml) Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- 1 Tbsp Fresh Parsley, finely chopped
- ½ tsp Kosher Salt
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

## Instructions

### On Prep Day:
**Char the Shallots**

1. Place **2 whole, unpeeled shallots** in a dry cast iron or stainless steel skillet over high heat.

2. Cook, turning occasionally with tongs, until charred on all sides and softened, **8-10 minutes**. The skins will blacken — that's good.

3. Remove from heat and let cool completely. Peel off the charred skins and mince the soft, smoky flesh.

### Make the Vinaigrette

4. In a small bowl, whisk together **minced charred shallots**, **red wine vinegar**, and **Dijon mustard**.

5. Slowly drizzle in **olive oil** while whisking to emulsify.

6. Stir in **parsley**, **salt**, and **pepper**. Taste and adjust seasoning.

---

**To Store:** Transfer to a small jar. Refrigerate for up to 5 days. Shake well before using — vinaigrette will separate.

**Used in:** Meal 4 (Lamb Kofta) — drizzled over kofta, farro, and roasted cauliflower
