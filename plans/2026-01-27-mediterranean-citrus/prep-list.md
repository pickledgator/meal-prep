# Sunday Prep List

_Difficulty: Normal_

---

## Long-Running Tasks First

☐ **Cook Farro** (1 cup dry + 3 cups water; ~30 minutes)³⁴
Bring to boil, reduce to simmer, cover and cook until tender but chewy. Drain excess water. (Yield: ~3 cups cooked)

☐ **Preheat Oven to 425°F** (for roasting cauliflower)²⁴

☐ **Roast Cauliflower** — see below²⁴
Cut into florets, toss with olive oil, salt, pepper. Roast 25-30 minutes until golden. (Reserve half for M4)

---

## Prep Produce (⏱ 25 minutes)

- ☐ Lacinato Kale (2 bunches; wash, remove stems, tear into pieces — yield ~12 cups)¹²³
  - Reserve ⅓ for sautéing¹
  - Reserve ⅓ for roasting on sheet pan²
  - Reserve ⅓ for massaged salad³
- ☐ Cauliflower (1 large head; cut into bite-sized florets — yield ~6 cups)²⁴🫙
- ☐ Garlic (11 cloves; peel — 4 mince for dressing¹²³, 1 mince for gremolata¹, 2 mince for kale¹, 2 mince for shrimp³, 2 mince for kofta⁴)🫙
- ☐ Shallots (4 medium; 2 thinly slice for gremolata¹, 2 leave whole for charring⁴)🫙
- ☐ Lemon (5; zest 1 for gremolata¹, juice all 5 — yield ~½ cup juice)¹²³⁴🫙
- ☐ Fresh Parsley (2 bunches; wash, finely chop — yield ~1 cup)¹²³⁴🫙
- ☐ Fresh Mint (1 small bunch; wash, finely chop — yield ~3 Tbsp)⁴
- ☐ Cherry Tomatoes (1 pint; halve)³

---

## Cooked Prep Tasks

☐ **Roast Cauliflower Florets**²⁴
Toss 6 cups florets with 2 Tbsp olive oil, salt, pepper. Spread on sheet pan, roast at 425°F for 25-30 minutes until golden and tender. Divide in half — store separately for M2 and M4.

☐ **Char Shallots for Vinaigrette**⁴
Place 2 whole shallots in dry skillet over high heat. Char on all sides, ~8-10 minutes. Cool, then peel and mince for vinaigrette.

---

_— CLEANING BREAK —_

---

## Prepare Components

☐ **Lemon-Tahini Dressing** — see `components/lemon-tahini-dressing.md`¹²³
Whisk together and refrigerate (yield: ~1 cup). This is your workhorse sauce for 3 meals!

☐ **Parsley Gremolata** — see `components/parsley-gremolata.md`¹
Combine parsley, lemon zest, shallot, garlic. Store in refrigerator (yield: ~⅓ cup).

☐ **Charred Shallot Vinaigrette** — see `components/charred-shallot-vinaigrette.md`⁴
Combine charred shallots with red wine vinegar, olive oil, mustard. Store in refrigerator (yield: ~½ cup).

---

## Optional Additional Prep

☐ **Form Lamb Kofta**⁴
Mix lamb with spices, egg, and panko. Form into 8 small patties or oval shapes. Refrigerate on parchment-lined plate. _(Can also be done day-of.)_

☐ **Massage Kale for Grain Bowl**³
Toss reserved kale with 1 Tbsp olive oil, pinch of salt, and 1 Tbsp lemon juice. Massage for 2-3 minutes until tender. Store in container. _(Can also be done day-of.)_
