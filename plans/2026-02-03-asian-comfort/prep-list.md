# Sunday Prep List

_Difficulty: Normal_

---

## Long-Running Tasks First

☐ **Cook Jasmine Rice** (2 cups dry + 3 cups water; ~20 minutes)¹²³
Rinse rice until water runs clear. Combine with water in saucepan, bring to boil, reduce to low, cover and cook 15-18 minutes. Fluff and cool. (Yield: ~6 cups cooked)

☐ **Press Tofu** (30-60 minutes while doing other prep)⁴
Wrap both tofu blocks in clean towels, place heavy items on top. Press while completing other prep tasks.

---

## Prep Produce (⏱ 30 minutes)

- ☐ Fresh Ginger (5-inch piece; peel)
  - 2 Tbsp grate for dressing¹²⁴🫙
  - 1 Tbsp mince for miso glaze¹🫙
  - 1 Tbsp mince for gochujang marinade²🫙
  - 1 Tbsp mince for curry³
  - 1 Tbsp slice into coins for broth⁴🫙
- ☐ Garlic (14 cloves; peel)
  - 2 cloves mince for dressing¹²⁴🫙
  - 2 cloves mince for miso glaze¹🫙
  - 4 cloves mince for gochujang marinade²🫙
  - 2 cloves mince for curry³
  - 2 cloves slice thin for broth⁴🫙
  - 2 cloves reserve for day-of adjustments
- ☐ Scallions (12-14 stalks; trim)
  - Separate white and green parts
  - Whites: thinly slice (yield: ~½ cup) — for curry³ and broth⁴
  - Greens: thinly slice (yield: ~1 cup) — reserve for garnish¹²³⁴
- ☐ Broccolini (2 bunches; trim ends, cut into 3-inch pieces)¹
- ☐ Napa Cabbage (½ head)
  - Core and thinly slice 2 cups for pickles²³🫙
  - Reserve remainder for stir-fry or salads
- ☐ Daikon Radish (6-inch piece; peel and julienne or thinly slice — yield: ~2 cups)²🫙
- ☐ Sugar Snap Peas (8 oz; trim ends and remove strings)³
- ☐ Red Bell Pepper (2; seed and slice into strips)³
- ☐ Shiitake Mushrooms (8 oz; remove stems, slice caps — yield: ~3 cups)⁴
- ☐ Baby Bok Choy (4 heads; halve lengthwise, rinse well between leaves)⁴
- ☐ Lime (3; cut into wedges for serving)³
- ☐ Fresh Cilantro (1 bunch; wash, roughly chop leaves — yield: ~1 cup)²³

## Cooked Prep Tasks

☐ **Marinate Chicken Thighs**²
Combine chicken with gochujang marinade in container or zip-lock bag. Refrigerate at least 2 hours or overnight.

---

## Prepare Components

☐ **Miso-Mirin Glaze** — see `components/miso-mirin-glaze.md`¹
Whisk together miso, mirin, soy sauce, honey, ginger, garlic. (Yield: ~½ cup)

☐ **Sesame-Ginger Dressing** — see `components/sesame-ginger-dressing.md`¹²⁴
Whisk or blend all ingredients until smooth. This is your **workhorse sauce** for 3 meals! (Yield: ~1 cup)

☐ **Gochujang Marinade** — see `components/gochujang-marinade.md`²
Whisk together gochujang, soy sauce, honey, sesame oil, ginger, garlic. (Yield: ~¾ cup)

☐ **Quick-Pickled Vegetables** — see `components/quick-pickles.md`²³
Combine napa cabbage and daikon with rice vinegar brine. Refrigerate at least 1 hour. (Yield: ~2 cups)

☐ **Ginger-Soy Broth** — see `components/ginger-soy-broth.md`⁴
Simmer stock with ginger, garlic, soy sauce, mirin. Strain and cool. (Yield: ~1½ cups)

## Storage Notes

| Component | Container | Shelf Life |
|-----------|-----------|------------|
| Cooked Rice | Airtight container | 4-5 days refrigerated |
| Miso-Mirin Glaze | Glass jar | 1 week refrigerated |
| Sesame-Ginger Dressing | Glass jar | 1 week refrigerated |
| Gochujang Marinade | With chicken | Use within 24-48 hours |
| Quick-Pickled Vegetables | Glass jar, submerged in brine | 1-2 weeks refrigerated |
| Ginger-Soy Broth | Airtight container | 4-5 days refrigerated |
| Pressed Tofu | Wrapped in fresh towel | 2-3 days refrigerated |
| Prepped Vegetables | Separate containers | 3-4 days refrigerated |
