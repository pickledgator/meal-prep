# Crispy Tofu Steaks

_with Mushrooms & Ginger-Soy Broth_

_Makes 4 servings_

## Ingredients

### For the Crispy Tofu

- 2 blocks (28 oz / 794 g total) Extra-Firm Tofu, pressed (from prep)
- 2 Tbsp Cornstarch
- 3 Tbsp Neutral Oil
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_
- White Pepper, _to taste_

### For the Vegetables

- 8 oz (227 g) Shiitake Mushrooms, stemmed and sliced (from prep)
- 4 heads Baby Bok Choy, halved (from prep)
- 2 Tbsp Scallion Whites, sliced (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp Neutral Oil
- Pinch of Kosher Salt

### For Serving

- 1½ cups Ginger-Soy Broth (from prep), warmed
- 3-4 Tbsp Sesame-Ginger Dressing (from prep)
- ¼ cup Scallion Greens, sliced (from prep)
- 1 tsp Toasted Sesame Oil, _for drizzling_
- Flaky Salt, _for finishing_

---

## Instructions

### Prepare and Sear the Tofu

1. Cut each block of **pressed tofu** crosswise into 4 thick steaks (8 steaks total, 2 per serving).

2. Season tofu steaks on all sides with **salt** and **white pepper**. Dust lightly with **cornstarch**, patting off excess.

3. Heat **2 Tbsp neutral oil** in a large non-stick or cast iron skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering.

4. Add tofu steaks in a single layer (work in batches if needed). Cook without moving for **4-5 minutes** until a deep golden crust forms.

5. Carefully flip and cook another **4-5 minutes** on the second side. Transfer to a plate.

> **Hot Tip:** Don't move the tofu while it sears — patience creates that crispy, golden crust. The tofu will release naturally when ready to flip.

### Cook the Vegetables

6. In the same skillet, add **1 Tbsp oil** over medium-high heat.

7. Add **shiitake mushrooms** and **scallion whites**. Sauté for **3-4 minutes** until mushrooms are golden and tender.

8. Add **bok choy** cut-side down. Cook for **2 minutes** until lightly charred on the cut side.

9. Flip bok choy and cook another **1-2 minutes** until leaves are wilted but stems retain some crunch. Season with a pinch of **salt**.

### Plate & Serve

10. Warm the **ginger-soy broth** in a small saucepan or microwave.

11. Divide **sautéed bok choy** between four shallow bowls, placing cut-side up.

12. Arrange **shiitake mushrooms** around the bok choy.

13. Place **2 crispy tofu steaks** in each bowl, overlapping slightly.

14. Pour **warm ginger-soy broth** around (not over) the tofu — about ⅓ cup per bowl.

15. Drizzle **sesame-ginger dressing** over the tofu.

16. Garnish with **scallion greens** and a drizzle of **toasted sesame oil**.

17. Finish with **flaky salt**.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** This light yet satisfying dish pairs beautifully with a chilled glass of junmai sake or a dry rosé.
