# Thai Coconut Curry Shrimp

_with Snap Peas & Jasmine Rice_

_Makes 4 servings_

---

## Prepped Ingredients

> From your Sunday prep:
>
> - Jasmine Rice (cooked, ~2 cups portion)
> - Quick-Pickled Vegetables — `components/quick-pickles.md`
> - Sugar Snap Peas (trimmed)
> - Red Bell Pepper (sliced)
> - Scallion whites (sliced)
> - Scallion greens (sliced, for garnish)
> - Fresh Ginger (1 Tbsp, minced)
> - Garlic (2 cloves, minced)
> - Cilantro (chopped, for garnish)
> - Lime (cut into wedges)

---

## Ingredients

### For the Curry

- 1.5 lb (680 g) Large Shrimp, peeled & deveined
- 1 can (13.5 oz / 400 ml) Full-Fat Coconut Milk
- 3 Tbsp Red Curry Paste
- 2 Tbsp Fish Sauce
- 1 Tbsp Honey or Brown Sugar
- 8 oz (227 g) Sugar Snap Peas, trimmed (from prep)
- 2 Red Bell Peppers, sliced (from prep)
- ¼ cup Scallion Whites, sliced (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp Fresh Ginger, minced (from prep)
- 2 cloves Garlic, minced (from prep)
- 2 Tbsp Neutral Oil
- Pinch of Red Pepper Flakes, _optional_

### For Serving

- 2 cups Cooked Jasmine Rice (from prep), reheated
- ½ cup Quick-Pickled Vegetables (from prep)
- ¼ cup Fresh Cilantro, roughly chopped (from prep)
- ¼ cup Scallion Greens, sliced (from prep)
- Lime wedges (from prep)

---

## Instructions

### Make the Curry

1. Shake the can of **coconut milk** well. Open and measure out ¼ cup of the thick cream from the top — set aside for finishing. Reserve the rest.

2. Heat **neutral oil** in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat.

3. Add **scallion whites**, **ginger**, and **garlic**. Stir-fry for 30 seconds until fragrant.

4. Add **red curry paste** and stir constantly for 1 minute until the paste blooms and becomes very aromatic.

5. Pour in the remaining **coconut milk** (not the reserved cream). Stir in **fish sauce** and **honey**. Bring to a simmer.

6. Add **bell peppers** and cook for 2 minutes until slightly softened.

7. Add **snap peas** and cook for 1 minute — they should stay crisp.

8. Add **shrimp** in a single layer. Cook for **2-3 minutes**, flipping once, until shrimp are pink and just cooked through.

> **Hot Tip:** Don't overcook the shrimp — they cook quickly in the hot curry. Remove from heat as soon as they turn pink and curl slightly.

9. Remove from heat. Stir in the reserved **coconut cream** for richness. Add **red pepper flakes** if you want more heat.

### Plate & Serve

10. Divide **warm jasmine rice** between four shallow bowls.

11. Ladle **curry with shrimp and vegetables** over the rice.

12. Spoon **quick-pickled vegetables** on the side for contrast.

13. Garnish with **cilantro** and **scallion greens**.

14. Serve with **lime wedges** for squeezing over.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** A cold Thai beer (Singha or Chang) or a crisp Sauvignon Blanc complements the rich, spicy curry.
