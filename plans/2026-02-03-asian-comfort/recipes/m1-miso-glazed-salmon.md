# Miso-Glazed Salmon

_with Sesame Rice & Roasted Broccolini_

_Makes 4 servings_

---

## Prepped Ingredients

> From your Sunday prep:
>
> - Miso-Mirin Glaze — `components/miso-mirin-glaze.md`
> - Sesame-Ginger Dressing — `components/sesame-ginger-dressing.md`
> - Jasmine Rice (cooked, ~2 cups portion)
> - Broccolini (trimmed, cut into 3-inch pieces)
> - Scallion greens (sliced, for garnish)

---

## Ingredients

### For the Salmon

- 4 Salmon Fillets, skin-on (6 oz / 170 g each)
- ½ cup Miso-Mirin Glaze (from prep)
- Neutral Oil, _for the pan_

### For the Roasted Broccolini

- 12 oz (340 g) Broccolini, trimmed (from prep)
- 2 Tbsp Neutral Oil
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

### For Serving

- 2 cups Cooked Jasmine Rice (from prep), reheated
- 3-4 Tbsp Sesame-Ginger Dressing (from prep)
- 2 Tbsp Scallion Greens, sliced (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp Sesame Seeds
- Flaky Salt, _for finishing_

---

## Instructions

### Roast the Broccolini

1. Preheat oven to **425°F**.

2. Spread **broccolini** on a sheet pan. Drizzle with **oil** and season with **salt** and **pepper**. Toss to coat evenly.

3. Roast for **15-18 minutes** until tender and charred at the edges. Set aside.

### Broil the Salmon

4. While broccolini roasts, switch oven to **broil** (or use a second oven rack).

5. Pat **salmon fillets** dry with paper towels. Place skin-side down on a foil-lined sheet pan.

6. Brush the top and sides generously with **miso-mirin glaze**, reserving 2 Tbsp for finishing.

7. Broil 4-6 inches from heat for **6-8 minutes** until the glaze is caramelized and bubbling and the salmon is cooked to your liking (125°F for medium, 145°F for well-done).

> **Hot Tip:** Watch the salmon closely under the broiler — miso glazes caramelize quickly and can burn. If the glaze is browning too fast, move the rack lower.

### Plate & Serve

8. Divide **warm jasmine rice** between four shallow bowls or plates, placing it off-center.

9. Arrange **roasted broccolini** alongside the rice.

10. Place a **miso-glazed salmon fillet** on top of or beside the rice.

11. Brush salmon with remaining **miso-mirin glaze**.

12. Drizzle **sesame-ginger dressing** over the salmon and broccolini.

13. Garnish with **scallion greens**, **sesame seeds**, and a pinch of **flaky salt**.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** Pair with a chilled glass of sake or a dry Riesling to complement the sweet-savory glaze.
