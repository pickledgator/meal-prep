# Gochujang Chicken Thighs

_with Quick-Pickled Vegetables & Rice_

_Makes 4 servings_

---

> - Quick-Pickled Vegetables — `components/quick-pickles.md`

> - Jasmine Rice (cooked, ~2 cups portion)

## Ingredients

### For the Chicken

- 8 Bone-In Skin-On Chicken Thighs (~3 lb / 1.4 kg), marinated in gochujang (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp Neutral Oil

### For Serving

- 2 cups Cooked Jasmine Rice (from prep), reheated
- 1 cup Quick-Pickled Vegetables (from prep)
- 3-4 Tbsp Sesame-Ginger Dressing (from prep)
- ¼ cup Scallion Greens, sliced (from prep)
- ¼ cup Fresh Cilantro, roughly chopped (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp Sesame Seeds

---

## Instructions

### Roast the Chicken

1. Preheat oven to **425°F**. Line a sheet pan with foil and place a wire rack on top (or use the pan directly).

2. Remove **marinated chicken thighs** from the refrigerator 20 minutes before cooking to take the chill off.

3. Arrange chicken thighs skin-side up on the rack or pan. Brush any remaining marinade from the container over the tops.

4. Roast for **25-30 minutes** until the skin is deeply caramelized and crispy, and internal temperature reaches **165°F**.

5. For extra-crispy skin, broil for the last **2-3 minutes**, watching carefully to prevent burning.

> **Hot Tip:** The gochujang marinade has sugars that caramelize beautifully but can burn. If the chicken is browning too quickly, tent loosely with foil.

### Plate & Serve

6. Divide **warm jasmine rice** between four plates or shallow bowls.

7. Place **2 chicken thighs** on each plate, leaning against the rice.

8. Spoon **quick-pickled vegetables** alongside the chicken for freshness and crunch.

9. Drizzle **sesame-ginger dressing** over the chicken and rice.

10. Scatter **scallion greens**, **cilantro**, and **sesame seeds** over everything.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** The spicy-sweet chicken pairs well with a cold lager or a slightly sweet off-dry Gewurztraminer.
