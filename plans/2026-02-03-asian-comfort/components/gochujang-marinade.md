# Gochujang Marinade

_Makes about ¾ cup_

---

A bold Korean marinade with the perfect balance of sweet, spicy, and savory. The gochujang caramelizes beautifully when roasted, creating sticky, flavorful chicken.

## Ingredients

- ¼ cup (70 g) Gochujang
- 2 Tbsp Low-Sodium Soy Sauce
- 2 Tbsp Honey
- 1 Tbsp Toasted Sesame Oil
- 1 Tbsp Fresh Ginger, minced
- 4 cloves Garlic, minced
- 1 Tbsp Rice Vinegar

## Instructions

1. In a medium bowl, whisk together **gochujang**, **soy sauce**, and **honey** until smooth.

2. Add **sesame oil**, **ginger**, **garlic**, and **rice vinegar**. Whisk until fully combined.

3. Taste — it should be spicy, sweet, and savory in balance. Adjust honey if needed.

---

**To Use:** Pour marinade over chicken thighs in a container or zip-lock bag. Massage to coat evenly. Refrigerate at least 2 hours, or overnight for best flavor.

**To Store:** Store with chicken in refrigerator. Use within 24-48 hours.

- Meal 2: Marinade and glaze for chicken thighs
