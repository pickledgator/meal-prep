# Sesame-Ginger Dressing

_Makes about 1 cup_

---

This is your **workhorse sauce** for the week — used in 3 of 4 meals! A versatile dressing that works drizzled over proteins, tossed with vegetables, or as a dipping sauce.

## Ingredients

- 3 Tbsp Toasted Sesame Oil
- 3 Tbsp Rice Vinegar
- 2 Tbsp Low-Sodium Soy Sauce
- 2 Tbsp Honey
- 2 Tbsp Fresh Ginger, grated
- 2 cloves Garlic, minced
- 2 Tbsp Neutral Oil (vegetable or avocado)
- 1 Tbsp Sesame Seeds
- Pinch of Red Pepper Flakes, _optional_

## Instructions

1. In a medium bowl or jar, combine **sesame oil**, **rice vinegar**, **soy sauce**, and **honey**. Whisk until honey dissolves.

2. Add **grated ginger** and **minced garlic**. Whisk to combine.

3. While whisking, slowly drizzle in **neutral oil** to emulsify.

4. Stir in **sesame seeds** and **red pepper flakes** if using.

5. Taste and adjust seasoning — more vinegar for brightness, more honey for sweetness.

---

**To Store:** Transfer to an airtight jar. Refrigerate for up to 1 week. Shake well before using as it will separate.

**Used in:**
- Meal 1: Drizzled over miso salmon and broccolini
- Meal 2: Drizzled over gochujang chicken and rice
- Meal 4: Drizzled over crispy tofu and vegetables
