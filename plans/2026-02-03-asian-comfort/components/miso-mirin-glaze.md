# Miso-Mirin Glaze

_Makes about ½ cup_

---

A deeply savory glaze that caramelizes beautifully under the broiler. The miso provides umami depth while the mirin adds subtle sweetness.

## Ingredients

- 3 Tbsp (50 g) White Miso Paste
- 2 Tbsp Mirin
- 1 Tbsp Low-Sodium Soy Sauce
- 1 Tbsp Honey
- 1 Tbsp Fresh Ginger, minced
- 2 cloves Garlic, minced

## Instructions

1. In a small bowl, whisk together **miso paste** and **mirin** until smooth.

2. Add **soy sauce**, **honey**, **ginger**, and **garlic**. Whisk until fully combined.

3. Taste and adjust — add more honey for sweetness or soy sauce for saltiness.

---

**To Store:** Transfer to an airtight jar. Refrigerate for up to 1 week.

**Used in:**
- Meal 1: Brushed on salmon before broiling
