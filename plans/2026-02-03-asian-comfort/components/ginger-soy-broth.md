# Ginger-Soy Broth

_Makes about 1½ cups_

---

A light, aromatic broth that brings the tofu and mushroom dish together. Infused with ginger and garlic, it's savory without being heavy.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups (480 ml) Chicken or Vegetable Stock
- 1 Tbsp Fresh Ginger, sliced into coins
- 2 cloves Garlic, thinly sliced
- 2 Tbsp Low-Sodium Soy Sauce
- 1 Tbsp Mirin
- 1 Tbsp Fish Sauce
- 1 tsp Toasted Sesame Oil

## Instructions

1. In a small saucepan, combine **stock**, **ginger coins**, and **sliced garlic**. Bring to a simmer over medium heat.

2. Reduce heat to low and simmer gently for 10 minutes to infuse flavors.

3. Remove from heat. Stir in **soy sauce**, **mirin**, **fish sauce**, and **sesame oil**.

4. Strain broth through a fine-mesh sieve, discarding ginger and garlic solids.

5. Taste and adjust — add more soy for saltiness or mirin for sweetness.

---

**To Store:** Transfer to an airtight container. Refrigerate for up to 5 days.

**Used in:**
- Meal 4: Poured around crispy tofu, mushrooms, and bok choy
