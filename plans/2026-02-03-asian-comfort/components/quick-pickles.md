# Quick-Pickled Vegetables

_Makes about 2 cups_

---

Bright, crunchy pickles that add contrast to rich dishes. The gentle acidity cuts through the richness of the gochujang chicken and coconut curry.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups Napa Cabbage, thinly sliced
- 1 cup Daikon Radish, julienned or thinly sliced
- ¼ cup (60 ml) Rice Vinegar
- 2 Tbsp Sugar
- 1 tsp Kosher Salt
- ¼ cup Warm Water

## Instructions

1. Place **napa cabbage** and **daikon** in a clean glass jar or container.

2. In a small bowl, whisk together **rice vinegar**, **sugar**, **salt**, and **warm water** until sugar and salt dissolve.

3. Pour the brine over the vegetables. Press down to ensure vegetables are submerged.

4. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour. Flavor improves over time.

---

**To Store:** Keep submerged in brine in an airtight container. Refrigerate for up to 2 weeks.

**Used in:**
- Meal 2: Served alongside gochujang chicken for freshness and crunch
- Meal 3: Served alongside Thai curry for contrast
