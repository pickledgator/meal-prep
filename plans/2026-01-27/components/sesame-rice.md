# Sesame Rice

_Makes about 2 cups cooked_

## Ingredients

- ¾ cup (150 g) Long Grain White Rice
- 1½ cups (360 ml) Water
- 1 Tbsp Toasted Sesame Oil
- 1 Tbsp Sesame Seeds
- ½ tsp Kosher Salt

## Instructions

1. Rinse the **rice** in a fine-mesh strainer under cold water until the water runs mostly clear.

2. Combine the rice, **water**, and **salt** in a medium saucepan. Bring to a boil over high heat.

3. Reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15-18 minutes until water is absorbed and rice is tender.

4. Remove from heat and let stand, covered, for 5 minutes.

5. Fluff rice with a fork. Drizzle with **sesame oil** and sprinkle with **sesame seeds**. Gently fold to combine.

---

**To Store:** Cool completely, then transfer to an airtight container. Refrigerate for up to 4 days. Reheat with a splash of water, covered, in the microwave or in a skillet over low heat.

**Used in:** Meal 1
