# Lemon-Parsley Gremolata

_Makes about ¼ cup_

## Ingredients

- ¼ cup (15 g) Fresh Flat-Leaf Parsley, finely chopped
- 1 Tbsp Lemon Zest (from about 1 lemon)
- 2 cloves Garlic, finely minced or grated
- 1 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- Pinch of Kosher Salt

## Instructions

1. Combine the **parsley**, **lemon zest**, and **garlic** in a small bowl.

2. Drizzle with **olive oil** and add a pinch of **salt**. Stir to combine.

3. Taste and adjust seasoning as needed. The gremolata should be bright and aromatic.

---

**To Store:** Transfer to a small airtight container. Refrigerate for up to 3 days. Best used fresh for maximum flavor.

**Used in:** Meals 2, 3
