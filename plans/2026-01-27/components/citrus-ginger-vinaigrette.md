# Citrus-Ginger Vinaigrette

_Makes about ½ cup_

## Ingredients

- 2 Tbsp Fresh Blood Orange Juice (from 1 orange)
- 1 Tbsp Fresh Lemon Juice
- 1 tsp Lemon Zest
- 1 tsp Fresh Ginger, finely grated
- 1 Tbsp (15 g) Honey
- 1 tsp Dijon Mustard
- ¼ cup (60 ml) Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- 1 small Shallot, finely minced (about 1 Tbsp)
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

## Instructions

1. In a small bowl or jar, combine the **blood orange juice**, **lemon juice**, **lemon zest**, **ginger**, **honey**, and **Dijon mustard**. Whisk until honey dissolves.

2. Slowly drizzle in the **olive oil** while whisking constantly to emulsify.

3. Stir in the **shallot** and season with **salt** and **pepper** to taste. The vinaigrette should be bright and punchy.

---

**To Store:** Transfer to a small jar with a tight-fitting lid. Refrigerate for up to 1 week. Shake well before using, as the vinaigrette will separate.

**Used in:** Meals 1, 3
