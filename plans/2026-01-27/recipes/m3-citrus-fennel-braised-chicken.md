# Citrus-Fennel Braised Chicken

_with Blood Orange & Castelvetrano Olives_

_Makes 2 servings_

---

## Prepped Ingredients

> From your Sunday prep:
>
> - Fennel (sliced into wedges, fronds reserved)
> - Blood Orange (1 sliced into rounds, 1 juiced)
> - Citrus-Ginger Vinaigrette — `components/citrus-ginger-vinaigrette.md`
> - Lemon-Parsley Gremolata — `components/gremolata.md`
> - Minced garlic

---

## Ingredients

### For the Braised Chicken

- 4 Bone-In Skin-On Chicken Thighs (~1.5 lb / 680 g total)
- 1 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- 1 large Fennel Bulb, sliced into ½" wedges (from prep)
- 4 cloves Garlic, thinly sliced (from prep)
- ½ cup (120 ml) Dry White Wine
- ⅓ cup Fresh Blood Orange Juice (from prep)
- ½ cup (120 ml) Chicken Stock
- ½ cup (75 g) Castelvetrano Olives, pitted or left whole
- 1 Blood Orange, sliced into rounds (from prep)
- 2-3 sprigs Fresh Thyme (or ½ tsp dried)
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

### For Serving

- Reserved Fennel Fronds, chopped
- Lemon-Parsley Gremolata (from prep)
- Citrus-Ginger Vinaigrette (from prep), _for drizzling_
- Crusty Bread, _for serving_
- Flaky Salt, _for finishing_

---

## Instructions

### Sear the Chicken

1. Pat **chicken thighs** very dry with paper towels. Season generously on both sides with **salt** and **pepper**.

2. Heat **olive oil** in a large Dutch oven or oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering.

3. Place chicken thighs **skin-side down** in the pan. Press gently to ensure good contact. Cook without moving for **6-8 minutes** until the skin is deeply golden and crispy.

4. Flip and sear the other side for **2 minutes**. Transfer to a plate.

> **Hot Tip:** Resist the urge to move the chicken while searing. Patient, undisturbed cooking is the secret to crispy skin.

### Build the Braise

5. Preheat oven to **350°F**.

6. Pour off all but 1 Tbsp of fat from the pan. Add the **fennel wedges** in a single layer. Cook for **3-4 minutes** per side until lightly caramelized.

7. Add the **garlic** and cook for **30 seconds** until fragrant.

8. Pour in the **white wine**, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom. Let simmer for **1-2 minutes** until slightly reduced.

9. Add the **blood orange juice**, **chicken stock**, and **thyme**. Nestle in the **blood orange slices** and **olives**.

10. Return the **chicken thighs** to the pan, skin-side up, arranging them so the skin is above the liquid.

### Braise

11. Transfer to the oven, uncovered. Braise for **35-40 minutes** until chicken is cooked through (internal temp 175°F) and the fennel is very tender.

12. Remove from oven. Let rest for 5 minutes.

### Serve

13. Divide chicken, fennel, olives, and orange slices between two shallow bowls or plates. Spoon braising liquid over the top.

14. Drizzle with **citrus-ginger vinaigrette** and scatter **gremolata** generously over everything.

15. Garnish with **fennel fronds** and finish with **flaky salt**.

16. Serve with **crusty bread** for sopping up the sauce.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** A chilled rosé or light Côtes du Rhône is perfect alongside this bright, Mediterranean braise.
