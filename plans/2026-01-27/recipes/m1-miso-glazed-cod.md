# Miso-Glazed Cod

_with Roasted Broccolini & Sesame Rice_

_Makes 2 servings_

---

## Prepped Ingredients

> From your Sunday prep:
>
> - Miso Glaze (whisked and ready)
> - Sesame Rice — `components/sesame-rice.md`
> - Blanched Broccolini
> - Citrus-Ginger Vinaigrette — `components/citrus-ginger-vinaigrette.md`
> - Sliced scallions (whites and greens separated)

---

## Ingredients

### For the Cod

- 2 (5-6 oz / 142-170 g) Cod Fillets
- Miso Glaze (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp Neutral Oil (for pan)

### For the Roasted Broccolini

- Blanched Broccolini (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- Pinch of Red Pepper Flakes (optional)
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_

### For Serving

- Sesame Rice (from prep), reheated
- Citrus-Ginger Vinaigrette (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp Sesame Seeds
- Sliced Scallion Greens, _for garnish_
- Flaky Salt, _for finishing_

---

## Instructions

### Roast the Broccolini

1. Preheat oven to **425°F**. Line a sheet pan with parchment paper.

2. Toss the **blanched broccolini** with **olive oil**, a pinch of **salt**, and **red pepper flakes** if using. Spread in a single layer on the prepared pan.

3. Roast for **12-15 minutes**, until charred at the edges and tender.

### Cook the Cod

4. While broccolini roasts, position an oven rack about 6 inches from the broiler and preheat the broiler to high.

5. Pat **cod fillets** dry with paper towels. Line a small sheet pan with foil and lightly oil.

6. Place cod on prepared pan, skin-side down if applicable. Brush generously with the **miso glaze**, coating the top and sides.

7. Broil for **6-8 minutes** until the glaze is caramelized and bubbling, and the fish flakes easily with a fork at its thickest point.

> **Hot Tip:** Keep a close eye on the fish under the broiler—the sugars in the miso glaze can go from perfectly caramelized to burnt quickly.

### Assemble the Bowls

8. Divide the **sesame rice** between two bowls.

9. Arrange the **roasted broccolini** alongside.

10. Place the **miso-glazed cod** on top of or beside the rice.

11. Drizzle with **citrus-ginger vinaigrette**. Sprinkle with **sesame seeds** and **scallion greens**. Finish with a pinch of **flaky salt**.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** Pair with a cold glass of sake or a crisp Grüner Veltliner.
