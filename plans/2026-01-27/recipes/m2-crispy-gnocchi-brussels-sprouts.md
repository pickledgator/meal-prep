# Crispy Gnocchi with Brussels Sprouts

_with Brown Butter, Sage & Fried Egg_

_Makes 2 servings_

---

## Prepped Ingredients

> From your Sunday prep:
>
> - Halved Brussels Sprouts
> - Lemon-Parsley Gremolata — `components/gremolata.md`

---

## Ingredients

### For the Gnocchi & Sprouts

- 1 lb (454 g) Potato Gnocchi (shelf-stable or refrigerated)
- 8 oz (227 g) Brussels Sprouts (halved, from prep)
- 3 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil, divided
- 4 Tbsp (57 g) Unsalted Butter
- 8-10 Fresh Sage Leaves
- 2 cloves Garlic, thinly sliced
- ¼ tsp Red Pepper Flakes
- Juice of ½ Lemon (about 1 Tbsp)
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

### For the Fried Eggs

- 2 Eggs
- 1 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil or Butter

### For Serving

- 1 oz (28 g) Parmesan, freshly grated
- Lemon-Parsley Gremolata (from prep)
- Flaky Salt, _for finishing_

---

## Instructions

### Crisp the Brussels Sprouts

1. Heat **2 Tbsp olive oil** in a large (12-inch) skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering.

2. Add the **Brussels sprouts**, cut-side down, in a single layer. Season with **salt** and **pepper**. Cook without stirring for **4-5 minutes** until deeply golden on the bottom.

3. Toss and cook another **2-3 minutes** until tender and charred in spots. Transfer to a plate and set aside.

### Crisp the Gnocchi

4. Return the skillet to medium-high heat and add **1 Tbsp olive oil**. Add the **gnocchi** in a single layer (work in batches if needed). Cook without stirring for **3-4 minutes** until golden and crispy on the bottom.

5. Toss and continue cooking another **2-3 minutes** until golden all over. Push gnocchi to one side of the pan.

### Make the Brown Butter

6. Add the **butter** to the empty side of the pan. Let it melt, then foam. Continue cooking, swirling occasionally, until the butter turns **golden brown and smells nutty**, about 2 minutes.

7. Add the **sage leaves** and **garlic** to the butter. Let sizzle for **30 seconds** until sage is crisp and garlic is fragrant.

8. Toss everything together with the **red pepper flakes** and **lemon juice**. Return the **Brussels sprouts** to the pan and toss to combine. Season with **salt** and **pepper** to taste.

> **Hot Tip:** Watch the butter carefully—it goes from brown to burnt quickly. The moment it smells nutty and looks amber, add the sage.

### Fry the Eggs

9. In a small nonstick skillet, heat **1 Tbsp olive oil** over medium heat. Crack in the **eggs** and fry until whites are set but yolks are still runny, **3-4 minutes**. Season with salt and pepper.

### Serve

10. Divide the gnocchi and Brussels sprouts between two shallow bowls.

11. Top each with a **fried egg**. Shower with **grated Parmesan** and a generous spoonful of **gremolata**.

12. Finish with **flaky salt** and serve immediately.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** A glass of Lambrusco or Barbera complements the richness beautifully.
