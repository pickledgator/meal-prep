# Sunday Prep List

_Difficulty: Normal_

---

## Prep Produce (⏱ 20-25 minutes)

- ☐ Broccolini (1 bunch; trim ends, halve lengthwise if thick)¹
- ☐ Brussels Sprouts (8 oz / 227 g; trim ends, halve or quarter if large)²
- ☐ Fennel (1 large bulb; trim fronds [reserve 2 Tbsp], halve, core, slice into ½" wedges)³
- ☐ Blood Orange (1; slice into ¼" rounds | 1; juice [yield: ~⅓ cup])³🫙
- ☐ Lemon (3; zest 2 lemons, juice all 3 [yield: ~¼ cup juice + 2 Tbsp zest])¹²³🫙
- ☐ Ginger (1" piece; peel and finely grate)¹³🫙
- ☐ Garlic (8 cloves; peel [4 mince, 4 thinly slice])¹²³🫙
- ☐ Shallot (1 medium; finely mince)¹³🫙
- ☐ Parsley (1 bunch; finely chop [yield: ~½ cup])²³🫙
- ☐ Scallions (2; thinly slice, separate white from green)¹

---

_— CLEANING BREAK —_

---

## Cooked Prep Tasks (⏱ 25-30 minutes)

☐ **Cook Sesame Rice** — see `components/sesame-rice.md`¹
Cook rice, cool, and refrigerate. Reheat with a splash of water before serving.

☐ **Blanch Broccolini**¹
Bring salted water to boil, blanch broccolini 2 minutes until bright green and crisp-tender. Transfer to ice bath, drain, pat dry, and refrigerate.

---

## Prepare Components (⏱ 10-15 minutes)

☐ **Citrus-Ginger Vinaigrette** — see `components/citrus-ginger-vinaigrette.md`¹³
Make full recipe, refrigerate

☐ **Lemon-Parsley Gremolata** — see `components/gremolata.md`²³
Make full recipe, refrigerate

☐ **Miso Glaze**¹
Whisk together 3 Tbsp white miso, 2 Tbsp mirin, 1 Tbsp honey, and 1 tsp sesame oil. Store in airtight container up to 1 week.

---

## Optional Additional Prep

☐ **Pre-Season Chicken Thighs**³
Season chicken with salt and pepper, refrigerate uncovered overnight for crispier skin.

---

**Storage Notes:**

- Prepped vegetables: Store in airtight containers lined with paper towels
- Blanched broccolini: Refrigerate up to 3 days; roast from chilled
- Miso glaze: Refrigerate up to 1 week
- Vinaigrette: Shake well before using; keeps 5-7 days
- Gremolata: Best used within 2-3 days for freshness
