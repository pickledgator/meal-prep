# Sunday Prep List

_Difficulty: Normal — estimated 60–75 minutes_

---

☐ **Cook the Farro** — see `components/farro.md`²³
Start it first: 2 cups dry farro simmers ~25–30 min while you do everything else. Drain, cool, store. (yield: ~6 cups; M2 uses ~3 cups, M3 uses ~3 cups)

---

## Prep Produce (⏱ 25 minutes)

- ☐ Garlic (8 cloves; peel — _1 clove grated → yogurt_, _3 cloves minced → chicken_, _4 cloves minced → chimichurri_)²³🫙
- ☐ Lemon (zest 2 lemons → yogurt + chicken, then juice them; cut 1 lemon into wedges for serving; keep 1 in reserve)¹²³🫙
- ☐ Apricots (5; pit & ¼" dice → salsa)¹🫙
- ☐ Jalapeño (1; seed & finely mince → salsa)¹🫙
- ☐ Red Onion (¼; finely dice → salsa | rest: leave whole, slice into wedges day-of for grilling)¹³🫙
- ☐ Basil (chop ¼ cup → yogurt; leave the rest on the stem for day-of garnish)¹²³🫙
- ☐ Flat-Leaf Parsley (finely chop ~1 cup, packed → chimichurri)³🫙
- ☐ Zucchini (4 medium; slice into ¼" lengthwise planks, store between paper towels)²³
- ☐ Persian Cucumbers (2; ¼" half-moons → salmon salad)¹
- ☐ Cherry Tomatoes (halve ~1½ cups for the salad¹; reserve ~1 cup whole for the bowl²)¹²
- ☐ Cannellini Beans (drain & rinse 1 can → salmon salad)¹
- ☐ Feta (crumble ~1 cup / 6 oz)¹²

## Cooked Prep Tasks

☐ **Char the Corn** — see `components/charred-corn.md`¹²
Char 4 ears in a dry cast-iron skillet (or on the grill) until spotty-black, cut kernels off the cob. (yield: ~3 cups; M1 uses ~1½ cups, M2 uses ~1½ cups)

---

## Prepare Components

☐ **Chimichurri** — see `components/chimichurri.md`³
Uses 4 minced garlic cloves + chopped parsley. (yield: ~1 cup)

☐ **Lemon-Basil Yogurt** — see `components/lemon-basil-yogurt.md`²³
Uses 1 grated garlic clove + ¼ cup chopped basil + zest/juice of 1 lemon. (yield: ~1½ cups)

☐ **Apricot-Basil Salsa** — see `components/apricot-salsa.md`¹
Uses diced apricots, jalapeño, ¼ red onion, lime. Best within 2 days. (yield: ~1½ cups)

☐ **Marinate the Chicken** — see `recipes/m2-lemon-chicken-farro-bowl.md` (On Prep Day section)²
Uses 3 minced garlic cloves + zest/juice of 1 lemon + oregano. Marinate 12 hrs–2 days.

---

**Storage notes:**
- Farro, charred corn, prepped produce, and all sauces keep in airtight containers up to 4–5 days.
- Hold off slicing the **red onion wedges** and tearing **garnish basil** until day-of for freshness.
- Cook **salmon** and **flank steak** fresh on their respective nights — do not pre-cut.
