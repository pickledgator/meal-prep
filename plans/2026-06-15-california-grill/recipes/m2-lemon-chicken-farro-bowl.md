# Lemon-Oregano Chicken Farro Bowl

_with Grilled Zucchini & Lemon-Basil Yogurt_

_Makes 4 servings_

---

## Prepped Ingredients

> From your Sunday prep:
>
> - Marinated chicken thighs (marinated on prep day, below)
> - Pot of Farro (~3 cups) — `components/farro.md`
> - Charred Sweet Corn (~1½ cups) — `components/charred-corn.md`
> - Lemon-Basil Yogurt — `components/lemon-basil-yogurt.md`
> - Zucchini, sliced into planks (2)
> - Cherry tomatoes, whole (~1 cup)
> - Feta, crumbled (~½ cup)

---

## Ingredients

### Lemon-Oregano Chicken (marinade)

- 1¾ lb (794 g) Boneless-Skinless Chicken Thighs
- 3 cloves Garlic, minced
- Zest of 1 Lemon + Juice of 1 Lemon
- 2 Tbsp Olive Oil
- 2 tsp Dried Oregano
- ½ tsp Ground Cumin
- ½ tsp Smoked Paprika
- Kosher Salt & Black Pepper, _to taste_

### The Bowl

- ~3 cups cooked Farro (from prep)
- 2 Zucchini, sliced into planks (from prep)
- ~1½ cups Charred Corn (from prep)
- ~1 cup Cherry Tomatoes (from prep)
- 2 big handfuls Arugula
- ½ cup Feta, crumbled (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp Olive Oil _for grilling_
- Lemon-Basil Yogurt (from prep)
- A handful of torn fresh Basil, _for garnish_

---

## Instructions

### On Prep Day:

**Marinate the Chicken**

1. In a bowl or zip-top bag, combine the **chicken thighs** with the **garlic**, **lemon zest and juice**, **olive oil**, **oregano**, **cumin**, **smoked paprika**, **salt**, and **pepper**. Massage to coat. Refrigerate 12 hours up to 2 days.

### Day Of:

**Grill the Chicken & Zucchini**

1. Heat a grill or grill pan over medium-high. Grill the **chicken** 5–6 minutes per side until charred and cooked through (165°F). Rest 5 minutes, then slice.

2. Toss the **zucchini planks** with **olive oil**, **salt**, and **pepper**. Grill 2–3 minutes per side until tender with char marks. Cut into bite-size pieces.

3. Warm the **farro** in a skillet with a splash of water (or microwave). Fold in the **arugula** so it wilts slightly from the heat.

### Plate & Serve

1. Spoon the warm farro-and-arugula into each bowl as the base. Arrange the **grilled zucchini**, **charred corn**, and **cherry tomatoes** in sections on top. Fan the **sliced chicken** over the center. Drizzle generously with **lemon-basil yogurt**, scatter the **feta** and **torn basil**, and finish with a crack of black pepper.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Let the grilled chicken rest before slicing — cutting too soon lets all the lemony juices run out onto the board instead of staying in the meat.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** Pairs well with a crisp Sauvignon Blanc or a sparkling water with lemon.
