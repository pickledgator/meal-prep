# Grilled Wild Salmon

_with Charred Corn Salad & Apricot-Basil Salsa_

_Makes 4 servings_

---

## Prepped Ingredients

> From your Sunday prep:
>
> - Apricot-Basil Salsa — `components/apricot-salsa.md`
> - Charred Sweet Corn (~1½ cups) — `components/charred-corn.md`
> - Cherry tomatoes, halved (~1½ cups)
> - Persian cucumbers, sliced (2)
> - Cannellini beans, drained & rinsed
> - Feta, crumbled (~½ cup)
> - Lemon wedges

---

## Ingredients

### Salmon

- 4 (6 oz / 170 g) Wild Salmon Fillets
- 1 Tbsp Olive Oil
- ½ tsp Smoked Paprika
- Kosher Salt & Black Pepper, _to taste_

### Charred Corn & White Bean Salad

- ~1½ cups Charred Corn (from prep)
- ~1½ cups Cherry Tomatoes, halved (from prep)
- 1 cup Cannellini Beans (from prep)
- 2 Persian Cucumbers, sliced (from prep)
- 3 cups (or 2 big handfuls) Arugula
- ½ cup Feta, crumbled (from prep)
- 2 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- Juice of ½ Lemon
- Kosher Salt & Black Pepper, _to taste_

### To Finish

- Apricot-Basil Salsa (from prep)
- A handful of torn fresh Basil
- Lemon wedges, _for serving_

---

## Instructions

### Day Of:

**Cook the Salmon**

1. Pat the **salmon** dry. Rub with **olive oil** and season both sides with **smoked paprika**, **salt**, and **pepper**.

2. Heat a grill or cast-iron skillet over medium-high. Cook the salmon skin-side down (or presentation-side down) for 4–5 minutes until a crust forms, then flip and cook another 2–4 minutes until just opaque and it flakes at the thickest part. Rest 2 minutes.

**Toss the Salad**

3. In a large bowl, combine the **charred corn**, **cherry tomatoes**, **cannellini beans**, **cucumbers**, and **arugula**. Dress with **olive oil**, **lemon juice**, **salt**, and **pepper**. Toss gently and fold in most of the **feta**.

### Plate & Serve

1. Spread the corn-and-bean salad across each plate. Lean a **salmon fillet** on top. Spoon a generous **¼ cup of apricot-basil salsa** over and beside the fish, scatter the remaining **feta** and **torn basil** over everything, and tuck a **lemon wedge** alongside.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Don't move the salmon once it hits the pan — let the crust set, and it will release on its own when ready to flip.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** A chilled glass of dry rosé or Vermentino mirrors the bright, sweet-savory salsa beautifully.
