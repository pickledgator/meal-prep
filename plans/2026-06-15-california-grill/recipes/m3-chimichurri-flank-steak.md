# Chimichurri Flank Steak

_with Farro & Grilled Summer Vegetables_

_Makes 4 servings_

---

## Prepped Ingredients

> From your Sunday prep:
>
> - Chimichurri — `components/chimichurri.md`
> - Pot of Farro (~3 cups) — `components/farro.md`
> - Lemon-Basil Yogurt — `components/lemon-basil-yogurt.md`
> - Zucchini, sliced into planks (2)
> - Lemon wedges

---

## Ingredients

### Flank Steak

- 1½ lb (680 g) Flank Steak
- 1 Tbsp Olive Oil
- Kosher Salt & Black Pepper, _to taste_

### Grilled Summer Vegetables

- 2 Zucchini, sliced into planks (from prep)
- 2 Bell Peppers, cut into wide strips
- ½ Red Onion, cut into wedges
- 1 Tbsp Olive Oil
- Kosher Salt & Black Pepper, _to taste_

### To Finish

- ~3 cups cooked Farro (from prep)
- Chimichurri (from prep)
- Lemon-Basil Yogurt (from prep)
- Lemon wedges, _for serving_

---

## Instructions

### Day Of:

**Grill the Steak**

1. Pat the **flank steak** dry, rub with **olive oil**, and season generously with **salt** and **pepper**. Let it sit at room temperature ~15 minutes.

2. Heat a grill or cast-iron skillet over high. Grill the steak 3–4 minutes per side for medium-rare. Transfer to a board and rest 5–10 minutes, then slice thinly **against the grain**.

**Grill the Vegetables**

3. Toss the **zucchini**, **bell peppers**, and **red onion** with **olive oil**, **salt**, and **pepper**. Grill 3–4 minutes per side until charred and tender.

4. Warm the **farro** in a skillet with a splash of water and stir in 2 Tbsp of the **chimichurri** to season the grains.

### Plate & Serve

1. Spread the chimichurri-flecked **farro** across a platter or plates. Pile the **grilled vegetables** on top, then fan the **sliced steak** over them. Spoon **chimichurri** generously over the steak, add a few swooshes of **lemon-basil yogurt** for a cooling contrast, and serve with **lemon wedges**.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Slicing flank steak thinly against the grain is the difference between tender and chewy — look for the direction the muscle fibers run and cut across them.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** A bold Malbec is the classic Argentine match for chimichurri steak.
