# Apricot-Basil Salsa

_Makes about 1½ cups_

## Ingredients

- 5 ripe Apricots, pitted & ¼" diced (~1¼ cups)
- ¼ Red Onion, finely diced
- 1 Jalapeño, seeded & finely minced
- Juice of 1 Lime
- 2 Tbsp thinly sliced Basil
- 1 tsp Honey (optional, if apricots are tart)
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, combine the **apricots**, **red onion**, **jalapeño**, and **lime juice**. Toss gently.

2. Stir in the **basil** and a pinch of **salt**. Taste — if the apricots are sharp, stir in the **honey** to balance.

3. Let sit at least 15 minutes for the flavors to meld before serving.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Add the basil close to serving if you can — it keeps the color fresh and the flavor bright.

**To Store:** Best within 2 days. Transfer to an air-tight container and refrigerate; drain any excess liquid before serving.

**Used in:** Meal 1
