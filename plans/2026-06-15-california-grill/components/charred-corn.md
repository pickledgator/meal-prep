# Charred Sweet Corn

_Makes about 3 cups kernels_

## Ingredients

- 4 ears Sweet Corn, husked
- 1 Tbsp Olive Oil
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_

## Instructions

1. Pat the **corn** dry and rub lightly with **olive oil**.

2. Heat a dry cast-iron skillet (or grill) over high heat until nearly smoking. Add the corn and cook, turning every couple of minutes, until charred and spotty-black in places, about 8–10 minutes total. _(On a grill: char directly over the flame.)_

3. Let cool slightly, then stand each ear in a wide bowl and cut the kernels off the cob. Season with a pinch of **salt**.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Don't move the corn too often — let it sit long enough to take on real color. The browned kernels are where the sweet, smoky flavor lives.

**To Store:** Transfer to an air-tight container and refrigerate up to 5 days.

**Used in:** Meals 1, 2 (~1½ cups each)
