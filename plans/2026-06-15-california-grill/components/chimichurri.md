# Chimichurri

_Makes about 1 cup_

## Ingredients

- 1 cup packed, finely chopped Flat-Leaf Parsley
- 4 cloves Garlic, minced
- ¼ cup Red Wine Vinegar
- ¾ cup Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- 2 tsp Dried Oregano
- ½ tsp Red Pepper Flakes (more to taste)
- ¾ tsp Kosher Salt
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, stir together the **parsley**, **garlic**, **red wine vinegar**, **oregano**, **red pepper flakes**, and **salt**. Let sit 5 minutes to soften the garlic and mellow the vinegar.

2. Whisk in the **olive oil** until combined. Season with **black pepper** and more salt to taste. The sauce should be loose, spoonable, and punchy.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Chimichurri only gets better as it sits — make it ahead and bring to room temperature before serving so the oil loosens up.

**To Store:** Transfer to an air-tight container and refrigerate up to 1 week. Bring to room temperature before serving.
