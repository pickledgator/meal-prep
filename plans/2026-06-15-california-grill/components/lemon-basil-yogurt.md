# Lemon-Basil Yogurt

_Makes about 1½ cups_

## Ingredients

- 1¼ cups (280 g) full-fat Greek Yogurt
- ¼ cup finely chopped Basil
- 1 clove Garlic, grated
- Zest of 1 Lemon
- Juice of ½ Lemon (~1½ Tbsp)
- 1 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

## Instructions

1. In a medium bowl, stir together the **yogurt**, **basil**, **garlic**, **lemon zest**, **lemon juice**, and **olive oil** until smooth.

2. Season with **salt** and **pepper** to taste. It should taste bright and herby with a gentle garlic kick — loosen with a teaspoon of water if you want a more drizzle-able consistency.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Grate the garlic on a microplane so it melts into the yogurt without sharp raw bites.

**To Store:** Transfer to an air-tight container and refrigerate up to 5 days. Stir before using.

**Used in:** Meals 2, 3
