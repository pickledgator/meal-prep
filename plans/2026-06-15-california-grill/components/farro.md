# Pot of Farro

_Makes about 6 cups cooked_

## Ingredients

- 2 cups (370 g) dry Farro
- 6 cups Water
- 1 tsp Kosher Salt

## Instructions

1. Rinse the **farro** under cool water. Add it to a large pot with the **water** and **salt**.

2. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer. Cook uncovered until tender but still chewy, about 25–30 minutes (check your package — pearled farro cooks faster than whole).

3. Drain off any excess water. Spread the farro on a sheet pan to cool quickly and keep the grains separate.

---

**To Store:** Transfer to an air-tight container and refrigerate up to 5 days. Reheat with a splash of water, or warm in a skillet with a little olive oil.

**Used in:** Meals 2, 3 (~3 cups each)
