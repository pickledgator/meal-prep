# Sunday Prep List

_Difficulty: Easy_

---

## Long-Running Tasks First

☐ **Cook Rice** (1 cup dry; according to package instructions, ~18-20 minutes)¹³
Rinse rice, cook with 1.5 cups water. Fluff and cool. (yield: ~3 cups cooked)

---

## Prep Produce (⏱ 15 minutes)

- ☐ Broccolini (1 bunch / 6 oz; trim ends, leave whole or halve thick stalks)¹
- ☐ Brussels Sprouts (8 oz; trim ends, halve through root)²
- ☐ Fennel (1 bulb; trim fronds, quarter, core, slice into 1/2-inch wedges)³
  - Reserve fennel fronds for garnish
- ☐ Garlic (6 cloves; peel)¹²³🫙
  - Allocate: 2 cloves minced (glaze + vinaigrette)¹³ | 2 cloves thinly sliced (chicken)³ | 2 cloves minced (gremolata)¹²³
- ☐ Fresh Ginger (2-inch piece; peel and finely grate, ~2 tsp)¹³🫙
  - Allocate: 1 tsp for glaze¹ | 1 tsp for vinaigrette¹³
- ☐ Lemon (3)¹²³🫙
  - Zest all 3 (need ~1 Tbsp total for gremolata + brown butter)
  - Juice 2 lemons (~4 Tbsp juice for vinaigrette + brown butter)
  - Reserve 1 lemon for wedges (serving M1)
- ☐ Orange (2)¹³🫙
  - Zest 1 orange (~1 tsp for vinaigrette)
  - Juice both oranges (~1/3 cup juice for vinaigrette + chicken)
  - Slice remaining orange into 1/4-inch rounds (for chicken braise)³
- ☐ Flat-Leaf Parsley (1 bunch; wash, dry, finely chop ~4 Tbsp / 1/4 cup)¹²³🫙
  - Allocate: all for gremolata (used across all 3 meals)
- ☐ Scallions (2; thinly slice, white and green parts separate)¹

## Prepare Components

☐ **Miso-Ginger Glaze** — see `components/miso-ginger-glaze.md`¹
Using: 1 clove garlic (minced), 1 tsp ginger (grated)
(yield: ~1/3 cup)

☐ **Citrus-Ginger Vinaigrette** — see `components/citrus-ginger-vinaigrette.md`¹³
Using: 1 clove garlic (minced), 1 tsp ginger (grated), 2 Tbsp lemon juice, 2 Tbsp orange juice, 1 tsp orange zest
(yield: ~1/2 cup)

☐ **Gremolata** — see `components/gremolata.md`¹²³
Using: 2 cloves garlic (minced), 1 Tbsp lemon zest, 4 Tbsp parsley
(yield: ~1/3 cup)

☐ **Sesame Rice** — see `components/sesame-rice.md`¹³
Using: 3 cups cooked rice, 1 Tbsp sesame seeds (toasted), 1 tsp sesame oil
(yield: ~3 cups)
