# Citrus-Ginger Vinaigrette

_Makes about 1/2 cup_

## Ingredients

- 3 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- 2 Tbsp Fresh Lemon Juice
- 2 Tbsp Fresh Orange Juice
- 1 tsp Orange Zest
- 1 tsp Fresh Ginger, finely grated
- 1 small clove Garlic, finely minced
- 1/2 tsp Rice Vinegar
- 1/4 tsp Kosher Salt
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

## Instructions

1. In a small jar or bowl, combine the **lemon juice**, **orange juice**, **orange zest**, **ginger**, **garlic**, **rice vinegar**, and **salt**. Whisk to combine.

2. Slowly drizzle in the **olive oil** while whisking constantly to emulsify.

3. Season with **pepper** to taste.

4. Taste and adjust acidity or salt as needed—the dressing should be bright and zippy with a subtle ginger warmth.

---

**To Store:** Transfer to a jar with a tight-fitting lid. Refrigerate for up to 5 days. Shake well before using, as the dressing will separate.

**Used in:** Meals 1 and 3 (Miso-Glazed Cod, Citrus-Fennel Braised Chicken)

**Note:** This versatile dressing brings brightness to both the umami-rich cod and the Mediterranean-style braised chicken.
