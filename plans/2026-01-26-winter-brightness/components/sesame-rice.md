# Sesame Rice

_Makes about 3 cups_

## Ingredients

- 1 cup (200 g) Jasmine or Long-Grain Rice, dry
- 1 1/2 cups Water
- 1 Tbsp Sesame Seeds
- 1 tsp Toasted Sesame Oil
- 1/4 tsp Kosher Salt

## Instructions

### On Prep Day:

1. Rinse the **rice** in a fine-mesh strainer under cold water until the water runs clear, about 30 seconds.

2. Combine the rinsed rice and **water** in a medium saucepan. Bring to a boil over high heat.

3. Once boiling, reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 18-20 minutes until water is absorbed and rice is tender.

4. While rice cooks, toast the **sesame seeds** in a dry skillet over medium heat, stirring frequently, until golden and fragrant, about 2-3 minutes. Transfer to a plate to cool.

5. When rice is done, remove from heat and let sit covered for 5 minutes.

6. Fluff rice with a fork. Drizzle with **sesame oil**, add the toasted sesame seeds and **salt**, and gently fold to combine.

7. Spread on a sheet pan to cool completely before storing.

---

**To Store:** Transfer to an airtight container. Refrigerate for up to 5 days.

**To Reheat:** Microwave with a splash of water, covered, for 1-2 minutes. Or reheat in a skillet with a little oil over medium heat.

**Used in:** Meals 1 and 3 (Miso-Glazed Cod, Citrus-Fennel Braised Chicken)

**Note:** This subtly nutty rice provides a warm, comforting base for both Asian and Mediterranean-inspired dishes.
