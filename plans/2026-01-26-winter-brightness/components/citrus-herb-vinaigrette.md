# Citrus-Herb Vinaigrette

_Makes about 1/2 cup_

## Ingredients

- 3 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- 2 Tbsp Fresh Lemon Juice
- 1 Tbsp Fresh Orange Juice
- 1 tsp Orange Zest
- 1 tsp Dijon Mustard
- 1 small clove Garlic, finely minced
- 2 Tbsp Fresh Parsley, finely chopped
- 1 Tbsp Fresh Dill, finely chopped
- 1/4 tsp Kosher Salt
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

## Instructions

1. In a small jar or bowl, combine the **lemon juice**, **orange juice**, **orange zest**, **Dijon mustard**, **garlic**, and **salt**. Whisk to combine.

2. Slowly drizzle in the **olive oil** while whisking constantly to emulsify.

3. Add the **parsley** and **dill**. Whisk once more and season with **pepper**.

4. Taste and adjust acidity or salt as needed.

---

**To Store:** Transfer to a jar with a tight-fitting lid. Refrigerate for up to 5 days. Shake well before using, as the dressing will separate.

**Used in:** Meals 2 and 3 (Harissa Chicken with Farro, Mediterranean Cod)

**Note:** This bright, herbaceous dressing ties together both the warm farro and fish dishes with a consistent citrus note.
