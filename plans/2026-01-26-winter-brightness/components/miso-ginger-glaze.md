# Miso-Ginger Glaze

_Makes about 1/3 cup_

## Ingredients

- 3 Tbsp (54 g) White Miso Paste
- 2 Tbsp Mirin
- 1 Tbsp Honey
- 1 tsp Fresh Ginger, finely grated
- 1 clove Garlic, minced
- 1 tsp Rice Vinegar

## Instructions

1. In a small bowl, whisk together the **miso paste**, **mirin**, **honey**, and **rice vinegar** until smooth.

2. Add the **grated ginger** and **garlic**. Whisk until fully combined.

3. Taste and adjust—add more honey for sweetness or more vinegar for brightness.

---

**To Store:** Transfer to a small jar or airtight container. Refrigerate for up to 1 week.

**Used in:** Meal 1 (Miso-Glazed Cod)
