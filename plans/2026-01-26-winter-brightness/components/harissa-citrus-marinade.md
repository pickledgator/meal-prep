# Harissa-Citrus Marinade

_Makes about 1/4 cup_

## Ingredients

- 2 Tbsp Harissa Paste
- 2 Tbsp Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
- Juice of 1/2 Orange (about 2 Tbsp)
- 1 tsp Orange Zest
- 2 cloves Garlic, minced
- 1/2 tsp Ground Cumin
- 1/2 tsp Smoked Paprika
- 1/4 tsp Kosher Salt
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

## Instructions

1. In a medium bowl, whisk together the **harissa paste**, **olive oil**, **orange juice**, and **orange zest** until smooth.

2. Add the **garlic**, **cumin**, **smoked paprika**, **salt**, and **pepper**. Whisk to combine.

3. Taste the marinade—it should be spicy, slightly sweet from the orange, and aromatic. Adjust harissa for heat level.

---

**To Store:** Can be made up to 3 days ahead. Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator.

**Used in:** Meal 2 (Harissa Chicken Thighs)

**Note:** Marinate the chicken for at least 30 minutes, or ideally overnight for deeper flavor.
