# Mediterranean Cod

_with Fennel-Tomato Braise & Herbed Farro_

_Makes 2 servings_

---

## Prepped Ingredients

> From your Sunday prep:
>
> - Citrus-Herb Vinaigrette — `components/citrus-herb-vinaigrette.md`
> - Cooked farro (1.5 cups)
> - Fennel (sliced into wedges)
> - Lacinato kale (stemmed and roughly chopped)

---

## Ingredients

### For the Fennel-Tomato Braise

- 1 Tbsp Olive Oil
- 1 bulb Fennel (about 10 oz / 283 g), sliced into 1/2-inch wedges (from prep)
- 3 cloves Garlic, thinly sliced
- 1 (14.5 oz / 411 g) can Crushed Tomatoes
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) Water or Low-Sodium Chicken Stock
- 1 tsp Orange Zest
- 1/4 tsp Red Pepper Flakes
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

### For the Cod

- 2 (5-6 oz / 142-170 g) Pacific Cod Fillets
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

### For the Kale

- 4 oz (113 g) Lacinato Kale, stemmed and roughly chopped (from prep)

### For the Herbed Farro

- 1.5 cups Cooked Farro (from prep)
- 2-3 Tbsp Citrus-Herb Vinaigrette (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp Fresh Parsley, chopped
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_

### For Garnish

- Fresh dill fronds
- Extra-virgin olive oil, _for drizzling_
- Flaky salt

---

## Instructions

### Build the Braise

1. Heat **olive oil** in a large, deep skillet or braiser over medium heat.

2. Add the **fennel wedges** in a single layer. Cook undisturbed for 3-4 minutes until lightly golden on one side. Flip and cook another 2 minutes.

3. Add the **sliced garlic** and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.

4. Pour in the **crushed tomatoes** and **water** (or stock). Stir in the **orange zest** and **red pepper flakes**. Season with **salt** and **pepper**.

5. Bring to a gentle simmer. Cover and cook for 10 minutes until fennel is nearly tender.

### Poach the Cod

6. Season the **cod fillets** generously with **salt** and **pepper**.

7. Nestle the cod into the simmering braise. Scatter the **chopped kale** around and over the fish.

8. Cover and cook for 8-10 minutes until the cod is opaque and flakes easily with a fork, and the kale is wilted and tender.

### Warm the Farro

9. While the cod cooks, warm the **cooked farro** in a small saucepan or microwave. Toss with **Citrus-Herb Vinaigrette** and **parsley**. Season with **salt**.

### Plate & Serve

1. Spoon a bed of **herbed farro** into two shallow bowls.

2. Use a spatula to carefully lift the **cod fillets** from the braise and place atop the farro.

3. Spoon the **fennel-tomato braise** and **wilted kale** around and over the fish.

4. Garnish with **fresh dill fronds**, a drizzle of **extra-virgin olive oil**, and a pinch of **flaky salt**.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Don't stir the braise once you've added the fish—cod is delicate and breaks apart easily. Let it cook undisturbed, then use a fish spatula for a clean lift.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** Pair with a crisp Vermentino or Albariño.
