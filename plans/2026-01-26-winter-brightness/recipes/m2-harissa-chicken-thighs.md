# Harissa Chicken Thighs

_with Citrus-Herb Farro & Roasted Carrots_

_Makes 2 servings_

---

## Prepped Ingredients

> From your Sunday prep:
>
> - Harissa-Citrus Marinade — `components/harissa-citrus-marinade.md`
> - Citrus-Herb Vinaigrette — `components/citrus-herb-vinaigrette.md`
> - Cooked farro (1.5 cups)
> - Rainbow carrots (halved lengthwise)
> - Fennel (thinly shaved)
> - Chicken thighs (marinated overnight)

---

## Ingredients

### For the Chicken

- 1 lb (454 g) Boneless Skinless Chicken Thighs (about 4 thighs)
- All of Harissa-Citrus Marinade (from prep)
- Olive Oil, _for cooking_
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_

### For the Roasted Carrots

- 8 oz (227 g) Rainbow Carrots, halved lengthwise (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp Olive Oil
- 1/2 tsp Ground Cumin
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_

### For the Citrus-Herb Farro

- 1.5 cups Cooked Farro (from prep)
- 1/2 cup (40 g) Fennel, thinly shaved (from prep)
- 2-3 Tbsp Citrus-Herb Vinaigrette (from prep)
- 2 Tbsp Fresh Parsley, chopped
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

### For Garnish

- Orange segments (from 1/2 orange)
- Fresh parsley leaves
- Flaky salt

---

## Instructions

### On Prep Day

**Marinate the Chicken**

1. Pat the **chicken thighs** dry with paper towels. Place in a shallow dish or zip-lock bag.

2. Pour the **Harissa-Citrus Marinade** over the chicken, turning to coat evenly. Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours, or ideally overnight.

### Day Of

**Roast the Carrots**

1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Line a sheet pan with parchment paper.

2. Toss the **halved carrots** with **olive oil**, **cumin**, and **salt**. Spread in a single layer on the sheet pan.

3. Roast for 20-25 minutes, flipping halfway, until caramelized and tender.

**Cook the Chicken**

4. Remove the **marinated chicken** from the refrigerator 15 minutes before cooking to take off the chill.

5. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat with a drizzle of **olive oil**. Shake excess marinade off the chicken and place in the pan.

6. Cook for 5-6 minutes per side until deeply golden and cooked through (internal temperature 165°F). Let rest for 3-4 minutes before slicing.

**Assemble the Farro**

7. In a medium bowl, combine the **cooked farro**, **shaved fennel**, **Citrus-Herb Vinaigrette**, and **parsley**. Toss gently to coat. Season with **salt** and **pepper**.

### Plate & Serve

1. Spoon the **citrus-herb farro** onto two plates, spreading into a shallow bed.

2. Arrange the **roasted carrots** alongside the farro.

3. Slice the **chicken thighs** against the grain and fan over the farro.

4. Tuck **orange segments** around the plate.

5. Finish with **fresh parsley** and a pinch of **flaky salt**.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Let the chicken rest after cooking—this allows the juices to redistribute, keeping every slice moist and flavorful.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** Pair with a glass of Grenache rosé or an amber ale.
