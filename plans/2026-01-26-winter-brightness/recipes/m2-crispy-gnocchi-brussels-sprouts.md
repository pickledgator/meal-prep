# Crispy Gnocchi with Brussels Sprouts

_with Brown Butter and Lemon_

_Makes 2 servings_

---

## Prepped Ingredients

> From your Sunday prep:
>
> - Gremolata — `components/gremolata.md`
> - Brussels sprouts (trimmed and halved)

---

## Ingredients

### For the Gnocchi

- 1 lb (454 g) Potato Gnocchi (from package, do not boil)
- 2 Tbsp Olive Oil

### For the Brussels Sprouts

- 8 oz (227 g) Brussels Sprouts, trimmed and halved (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp Olive Oil
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

### For the Brown Butter

- 3 Tbsp (43 g) Unsalted Butter
- 1 Tbsp Fresh Lemon Juice
- 1 tsp Lemon Zest

### For Serving

- 1/2 cup (30 g) Parmesan, freshly grated
- 1-2 Tbsp Gremolata (from prep)
- Freshly cracked Black Pepper
- Pinch of Red Pepper Flakes (optional)

---

## Instructions

### Crisp the Brussels Sprouts

1. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat with **1 Tbsp olive oil**.

2. Add the **halved Brussels sprouts**, cut-side down. Season with **salt**. Cook undisturbed for 4-5 minutes until deeply golden on the cut side.

3. Stir and continue cooking for 2-3 more minutes until tender-crisp. Transfer to a plate and set aside.

### Crisp the Gnocchi

4. In the same skillet, add **2 Tbsp olive oil** over medium-high heat.

5. Add the **gnocchi** in a single layer. Cook undisturbed for 3-4 minutes until golden and crispy on the bottom.

6. Flip and cook another 2-3 minutes until crispy on multiple sides. Transfer to a plate.

### Make the Brown Butter

7. Wipe the skillet clean. Add the **butter** and cook over medium heat, swirling occasionally.

8. The butter will foam, then the milk solids will begin to brown. Watch carefully—once it smells nutty and turns golden brown (about 2-3 minutes), remove from heat immediately.

9. Stir in the **lemon juice** and **lemon zest**. The butter will sizzle—this is normal.

### Bring It Together

10. Return the **gnocchi** and **Brussels sprouts** to the skillet with the brown butter. Toss gently to coat.

11. Season with **salt** and **pepper** to taste.

### Plate & Serve

1. Divide the **brown butter gnocchi and Brussels sprouts** between two plates or shallow bowls.

2. Shower generously with **freshly grated Parmesan**.

3. Scatter **gremolata** over the top.

4. Finish with a few cracks of **black pepper** and a pinch of **red pepper flakes** if you like heat.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Don't move the gnocchi or Brussels sprouts too soon—letting them sit undisturbed is the secret to achieving that crispy, golden crust.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** Pair with a medium-bodied white like Verdicchio, or a light red like Barbera.
