_with Sesame Rice and Roasted Broccolini_

_Makes 2 servings_

## Ingredients

### For the Cod

- 2 (5-6 oz / 140-170 g) Cod Fillets
- 3 Tbsp Miso-Ginger Glaze (from prep)
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_
- Olive Oil, _for cooking_

### For the Roasted Broccolini

- 6 oz (170 g) Broccolini, trimmed (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp Olive Oil
- 1/4 tsp Kosher Salt
- Pinch of Red Pepper Flakes (optional)

### For Serving

- 1.5 cups Sesame Rice, reheated (from prep)
- 2-3 Tbsp Citrus-Ginger Vinaigrette (from prep)
- 1-2 Tbsp Gremolata (from prep)
- Sliced scallions (from prep), _for garnish_
- Lemon wedges, _for serving_

---

## Instructions

### Roast the Broccolini

1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Line a sheet pan with parchment paper.

2. Toss the **broccolini** with **olive oil**, **salt**, and **red pepper flakes** if using. Spread in a single layer on the sheet pan.

3. Roast for 12-15 minutes, until tender and slightly charred at the tips.

### Cook the Cod

4. While broccolini roasts, pat the **cod fillets** dry with paper towels. Season both sides with **salt** and **pepper**.

5. Heat a skillet over medium-high heat with a drizzle of **olive oil**. When shimmering, add the cod fillets.

6. Cook for 3-4 minutes on the first side until golden. Flip the fish.

7. Brush the tops generously with **Miso-Ginger Glaze**. Continue cooking for 3-4 minutes until the fish is opaque and flakes easily with a fork.

### Plate & Serve

1. Divide the **warm sesame rice** between two plates or shallow bowls, creating a bed in the center.

2. Place a **glazed cod fillet** on top of the rice.

3. Arrange the **roasted broccolini** alongside.

4. Drizzle the fish and broccolini with **Citrus-Ginger Vinaigrette**.

5. Scatter **gremolata** over the cod and finish with **sliced scallions**.

6. Serve with **lemon wedges** on the side.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Pat the cod very dry before cooking—moisture prevents a good sear and keeps the glaze from sticking properly.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** Pair with a chilled glass of dry Riesling or a crisp Grüner Veltliner.
