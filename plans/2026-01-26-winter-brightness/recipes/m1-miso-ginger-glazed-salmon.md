# Miso-Ginger Glazed Salmon

_with Crispy Kale & Sesame Carrots_

_Makes 2 servings_

---

## Prepped Ingredients

> From your Sunday prep:
>
> - Miso-Ginger Glaze — `components/miso-ginger-glaze.md`
> - Rainbow carrots (sliced on diagonal)
> - Lacinato kale (stemmed and torn)

---

## Ingredients

### For the Salmon

- 2 (6 oz / 170 g) Salmon Fillets, skin-on
- 3 Tbsp Miso-Ginger Glaze (from prep)
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

### For the Sesame Carrots

- 8 oz (227 g) Rainbow Carrots, sliced on diagonal (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp Olive Oil
- 1/2 Tbsp Toasted Sesame Oil
- 1 Tbsp Sesame Seeds
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_

### For the Crispy Kale

- 4 oz (113 g) Lacinato Kale, stemmed and torn (from prep)
- 1 Tbsp Olive Oil
- 1/4 tsp Kosher Salt
- Pinch of Red Pepper Flakes (optional)

### For Garnish

- 1 Tbsp Fresh Dill, roughly chopped
- 1 tsp Sesame Seeds
- Lemon wedges, _for serving_

---

## Instructions

### Roast the Carrots & Kale

1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Line a large sheet pan with parchment paper.

2. Toss the **sliced carrots** with **olive oil**, **sesame oil**, and a pinch of **salt**. Spread on one half of the sheet pan.

3. Toss the **torn kale** with **olive oil**, **salt**, and **red pepper flakes** if using. Spread on the other half of the sheet pan.

4. Roast for 12-15 minutes, until the carrots are tender and the kale is crispy at the edges. Remove kale if it crisps before carrots are done.

### Glaze & Cook the Salmon

5. While vegetables roast, pat the **salmon fillets** dry and season with **salt** and **pepper**.

6. Heat a cast-iron or oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat with a drizzle of oil. Place salmon skin-side up and sear for 3 minutes until golden.

7. Flip the salmon skin-side down. Brush the tops generously with the **Miso-Ginger Glaze**.

8. Transfer the skillet to the oven and roast for 5-7 minutes, until the salmon reaches 125°F for medium (or 135°F for well-done). The glaze should be caramelized and sticky.

### Plate & Serve

1. Divide the **roasted carrots** between two plates, creating a bed. Sprinkle with **sesame seeds**.

2. Place a **glazed salmon fillet** on top of the carrots.

3. Pile the **crispy kale** alongside.

4. Garnish with **fresh dill**, an extra sprinkle of **sesame seeds**, and serve with **lemon wedges** on the side.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Don't skip the step of patting the salmon dry—moisture is the enemy of a good sear and proper glaze adhesion.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** Pair with a chilled glass of dry Riesling or a light lager.
