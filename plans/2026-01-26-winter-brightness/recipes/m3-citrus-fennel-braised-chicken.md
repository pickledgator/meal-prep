# Citrus-Fennel Braised Chicken

_with Olives and Fresh Herbs_

_Makes 2 servings_

## Ingredients

### For the Chicken

- 4 Bone-In, Skin-On Chicken Thighs (~1.5 lbs / 680 g total)
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_
- 1 Tbsp Olive Oil

### For the Braise

- 1 Fennel Bulb, sliced into 1/2-inch wedges (from prep)
- 2 cloves Garlic, thinly sliced (from prep)
- 1/2 cup (80 g) Castelvetrano Olives, lightly crushed or halved
- 3-4 Orange Slices (from prep)
- 1/4 cup Fresh Orange Juice (from prep)
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) Low-Sodium Chicken Stock
- Kosher Salt, _to taste_
- Freshly Ground Black Pepper, _to taste_

### For Serving

- 1.5 cups Sesame Rice, reheated (from prep)
- 2-3 Tbsp Citrus-Ginger Vinaigrette (from prep)
- 1-2 Tbsp Gremolata (from prep)
- Reserved fennel fronds, _for garnish_
- Flaky salt, _for finishing_

---

## Instructions

### Sear the Chicken

1. Pat the **chicken thighs** very dry with paper towels. Season generously on both sides with **salt** and **pepper**.

2. Heat **olive oil** in a large, deep skillet or braiser over medium-high heat.

3. Place the chicken thighs **skin-side down** in the hot pan. Press gently to ensure contact. Cook undisturbed for 6-7 minutes until the skin is deeply golden and crispy.

4. Flip the chicken and cook for 2 minutes on the other side. Transfer to a plate.

### Build the Braise

5. Reduce heat to medium. Add the **fennel wedges** to the skillet in a single layer. Cook for 2-3 minutes per side until lightly golden.

6. Add the **sliced garlic** and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.

7. Scatter the **olives** and **orange slices** over the fennel.

8. Pour in the **orange juice** and **chicken stock**. Scrape up any browned bits from the bottom of the pan. Season lightly with **salt** and **pepper**.

### Braise the Chicken

9. Nestle the **chicken thighs** back into the pan, **skin-side up**, on top of the fennel and olives. The liquid should come about halfway up the chicken—not covering the skin.

10. Bring to a gentle simmer. Reduce heat to medium-low, cover, and braise for 18-20 minutes until the chicken reaches 165°F and the fennel is tender.

11. Uncover and increase heat to medium. Simmer for 3-4 more minutes to reduce the liquid slightly and re-crisp the chicken skin.

### Plate & Serve

1. Divide the **warm sesame rice** between two plates or shallow bowls.

2. Using tongs, place 2 **chicken thighs** on each plate, alongside the rice.

3. Spoon the **braised fennel, olives, and orange slices** around and over the chicken.

4. Drizzle everything with **Citrus-Ginger Vinaigrette**.

5. Scatter **gremolata** over the chicken and finish with **fennel fronds** and a pinch of **flaky salt**.

---

> **Hot Tip:** Keep the skin above the liquid while braising—submerged skin turns soggy. The steam will cook the meat while the dry heat keeps that skin crispy.

---

**Serving Suggestion:** Pair with a citrus-forward white like Albariño, or a light Provençal rosé.
