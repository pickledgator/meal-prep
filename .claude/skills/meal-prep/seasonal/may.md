# May — Northern California Seasonal Guide

## Flavor Direction
**Late spring into early summer** — Asparagus season ending, but stone fruits are beginning. Strawberries are at their peak. Cherries arrive. The produce is getting sweeter and more colorful. Perfect grilling weather.

---

## Peak Produce

### Spring Vegetables (Ending)
- Asparagus (last of season)
- Artichokes (winding down)
- Fava beans (peak)
- English peas (peak)
- Snap peas
- Green beans

### Summer Arrivals
- Zucchini (beginning)
- Summer squash
- Early tomatoes (greenhouse)
- Corn (very early)
- Cucumbers

### Fruits (Exciting!)
- Strawberries (peak!)
- Cherries (Bing, Rainier beginning)
- Apricots (late May)
- Avocados

### Leafy Greens
- Arugula
- Butter lettuce
- Little Gem
- Spinach
- Swiss chard

### Alliums
- Spring onions
- Green garlic (ending)
- Garlic scapes

### Herbs
- Basil (beginning)
- Mint
- Cilantro
- Dill
- Chives
- Tarragon

---

## Suggested Proteins

**Seafood:** Wild salmon (excellent), halibut, swordfish, ahi tuna, spot prawns
**Poultry:** Chicken (great grilled)
**Meat:** Lamb, beef (grilling season)
**Plant-based:** Fava beans, eggs, early summer beans

---

## Sauce Ideas

1. **Basil Vinaigrette** — Fresh basil, lemon, garlic, olive oil, shallot
2. **Strawberry-Balsamic** — Macerated strawberries, aged balsamic, black pepper
3. **Fava Bean Pesto** — Fava beans, pecorino, mint, garlic, olive oil
4. **Herb Butter** — Softened butter with mixed fresh herbs
5. **Yogurt-Dill** — Greek yogurt, dill, lemon, cucumber

---

## Cooking Methods to Favor

- Grilling (peak season!)
- Quick sautés
- Raw preparations
- Light dressings
- Minimal cooking to showcase freshness

---

## Theme Suggestions

- **Grilled Everything:** Salmon, chicken, vegetables — all on the grill
- **Mediterranean Light:** Fava beans, fresh herbs, olive oil, feta
- **Farm-to-Table California:** Showcase strawberries, early stone fruit, spring vegetables
