# June — Northern California Seasonal Guide

## Flavor Direction
**Early summer glory** — Stone fruit season begins in earnest. Tomatoes are improving. Corn is here. Berries everywhere. Cooking should be simple and bright; let the produce shine. Perfect weather for outdoor cooking and minimal heat in the kitchen.

---

## Suggested Proteins

**Seafood:** Wild salmon, swordfish, ahi tuna, halibut, sardines, anchovies
**Poultry:** Chicken
**Meat:** Skirt steak, flank steak (grilling cuts)
**Plant-based:** Fresh shell beans, eggs, tofu

---

## Sauce Ideas

1. **Fresh Tomato Sauce** — Raw crushed tomatoes, garlic, basil, olive oil
2. **Stone Fruit Salsa** — Diced apricots or peaches, jalapeño, red onion, lime, cilantro
3. **Basil Pesto** — Classic: basil, pine nuts, Parmesan, garlic, olive oil
4. **Chimichurri** — Parsley, oregano, garlic, red wine vinegar, olive oil
5. **Corn Butter** — Sweet corn kernels blended with butter and lime

---

## Cooking Methods to Favor

- Grilling (proteins and vegetables)
- Raw preparations (gazpacho, salads)
- Quick high-heat searing
- Minimal cooking
- No-cook sauces

---

## Theme Suggestions

- **Summer Mediterranean:** Tomatoes, basil, olive oil, grilled fish
- **California Grill:** Everything on the grill, fresh salsas, corn
- **Light and Fresh:** Salads with grilled proteins, stone fruit accents
