# January — Northern California Seasonal Guide

## Flavor Direction
**Wintry yet bright** — Embrace hearty cooking methods (braises, roasts) but balance with vibrant citrus and bitter greens. This is peak citrus season; use it generously to add brightness to heavier dishes.

---

## Peak Produce

### Citrus (Peak Season)
- Blood oranges
- Cara Cara oranges
- Meyer lemons
- Satsuma mandarins
- Pomelos
- Kumquats
- Grapefruit (Ruby Red, Oro Blanco)

### Brassicas
- Broccoli & broccolini
- Brussels sprouts
- Cabbage (green, red, savoy, napa)
- Cauliflower (white, purple, Romanesco)
- Kale (lacinato, curly, red Russian)
- Collard greens

### Roots & Alliums
- Beets (red, golden, Chioggia)
- Carrots (including rainbow varieties)
- Celery root (celeriac)
- Parsnips
- Turnips
- Rutabaga
- Leeks
- Shallots
- Storage onions

### Squash (Late Season)
- Butternut squash
- Delicata squash
- Kabocha
- Spaghetti squash
- Acorn squash

### Leafy Greens
- Spinach
- Chard (rainbow, Swiss)
- Arugula
- Escarole
- Radicchio
- Endive

### Other
- Fennel
- Mushrooms (cremini, shiitake, oyster)
- Persimmons (Fuyu, Hachiya — late)
- Pomegranates (late season)

---

## Suggested Proteins

**Seafood:** Dungeness crab (peak!), Pacific cod, halibut, mussels, clams, sardines
**Poultry:** Chicken (thighs for braising, breasts for quick-cook), duck
**Meat:** Lamb shoulder, beef chuck (for braises)
**Plant-based:** White beans, lentils, chickpeas

---

## Sauce Ideas

1. **Citrus Vinaigrette** — Blood orange juice, shallot, Dijon, olive oil, fresh thyme
2. **Tahini-Meyer Lemon** — Tahini, Meyer lemon juice, garlic, warm water to thin
3. **Salsa Verde** — Parsley, capers, anchovy, garlic, lemon zest, olive oil
4. **Romesco** — Roasted red peppers, almonds, smoked paprika, sherry vinegar
5. **Gremolata** — Lemon zest, parsley, garlic (fresh topping, not a sauce)

---

## Cooking Methods to Favor

- Braising (root vegetables, tough cuts)
- Roasting (Brussels sprouts, cauliflower, squash)
- Quick-sear with pan sauce (fish, chicken)
- Raw preparations with citrus (salads, slaws)
- Warm grain bowls

---

## Theme Suggestions

- **Mediterranean Bright:** Lean into citrus, olive oil, herbs, feta, tahini
- **Italian Comfort:** Braised meats, beans, bitter greens, crusty bread
- **California Fresh:** Grain bowls with roasted vegetables, citrus dressings
