# August — Northern California Seasonal Guide

## Flavor Direction
**Late summer richness** — Still peak tomato and stone fruit season, but there's a sense of abundance becoming almost overwhelming. Peppers are at their best. Early fall crops (apples, grapes) begin appearing. Preserve-worthy produce everywhere.

---

## Peak Produce

### Tomatoes (Still Peak)
- Heirloom varieties
- Cherry tomatoes
- Roma tomatoes
- Dry-farmed Early Girls

### Stone Fruit
- Peaches (peak)
- Nectarines
- Plums (peak)
- Pluots
- Figs (beginning — Black Mission, Brown Turkey)

### Peppers (Peak!)
- Bell peppers (all colors)
- Shishito peppers
- Padrón peppers
- Jimmy Nardello
- Poblanos
- Jalapeños
- Serranos

### Summer Vegetables
- Corn
- Eggplant (peak!)
- Zucchini
- Summer squash
- Cucumbers
- Green beans

### Grapes & Early Fall
- Table grapes
- Wine grapes
- Early apples
- Pears (beginning)

### Herbs
- Basil
- Oregano
- Thyme
- Rosemary
- Sage (beginning)

---

## Suggested Proteins

**Seafood:** Wild salmon (ending), swordfish, ahi tuna, local halibut, grilled shrimp
**Poultry:** Chicken, duck
**Meat:** Lamb, beef (grilling cuts)
**Plant-based:** Shell beans, black beans, eggs

---

## Sauce Ideas

1. **Roasted Pepper Sauce** — Charred peppers, garlic, sherry vinegar
2. **Fig-Balsamic Glaze** — Fresh figs, balsamic, honey, black pepper
3. **Shishito-Ginger** — Blistered shishitos blended with ginger, rice vinegar
4. **Late Summer Pesto** — Basil with a little arugula for pepper, walnuts
5. **Eggplant "Caviar"** — Roasted eggplant, tahini, lemon, garlic

---

## Cooking Methods to Favor

- Grilling
- Charring/blistering (peppers)
- Roasting
- Quick sautés
- Raw preparations

---

## Theme Suggestions

- **Pepper Festival:** Celebrate the pepper harvest with stuffed, blistered, and sauced preparations
- **Mediterranean Late Summer:** Eggplant, tomatoes, peppers, olive oil, feta
- **California End-of-Summer:** Last hurrah of grilling season, stone fruit desserts
