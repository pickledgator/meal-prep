# November — Northern California Seasonal Guide

## Flavor Direction
**Deep autumn into early winter** — Peak brassica and root vegetable season. Citrus begins returning (the first mandarins!). Hearty, warming, celebratory. Cooking for comfort and gathering. Think make-ahead braises, roasted vegetables, warming grains.

---

## Peak Produce

### Brassicas (Peak!)
- Brussels sprouts (peak!)
- Broccoli
- Cauliflower (white, purple, Romanesco)
- Cabbage (red, green, savoy)
- Kale (all varieties)
- Collard greens

### Winter Squash
- Butternut squash
- Delicata
- Kabocha
- Acorn
- Spaghetti squash

### Roots
- Sweet potatoes
- Carrots
- Parsnips
- Beets
- Turnips
- Rutabaga
- Celery root

### Early Citrus (Arriving!)
- Satsuma mandarins (first of season!)
- Early tangerines
- Lemons

### Other
- Pomegranates (peak)
- Persimmons (peak)
- Cranberries
- Apples (storage varieties)
- Pears
- Fennel
- Mushrooms

### Herbs
- Sage
- Rosemary
- Thyme
- Parsley

---

## Suggested Proteins

**Seafood:** Pacific cod, halibut, mussels, clams, oysters
**Poultry:** Chicken (roasting, braising), duck, turkey
**Meat:** Lamb, beef (braising cuts)
**Plant-based:** Lentils, white beans, chickpeas, black beans

---

## Sauce Ideas

1. **Cranberry-Shallot Relish** — Fresh cranberries, shallot, orange zest, touch of honey
2. **Tahini-Lemon** — Tahini, lemon juice, garlic, warm water
3. **Brown Butter-Sage** — Classic autumn sauce
4. **Pomegranate Vinaigrette** — Pomegranate juice, shallot, Dijon, olive oil
5. **Mustard-Herb Cream** — Dijon, Greek yogurt, thyme, parsley

---

## Cooking Methods to Favor

- Roasting (vegetables and proteins)
- Braising
- Make-ahead preparations
- Warm grain bowls
- One-pot meals

---

## Theme Suggestions

- **Thanksgiving-Adjacent:** Familiar flavors (sage, cranberry, squash) without the turkey
- **Mediterranean Winter:** Brassicas, olive oil, lemon, tahini, chickpeas
- **Comfort Classics:** Roasted chicken, braised vegetables, warming preparations
