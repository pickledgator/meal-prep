# April — Northern California Seasonal Guide

## Flavor Direction
**Full spring abundance** — Peak season for asparagus, artichokes, and the first serious strawberries. Light, bright, verdant. Cooking should showcase ingredients simply. Herbs are lush; use them generously.

---

## Peak Produce

### Spring Vegetables (Peak)
- Asparagus (peak!)
- Artichokes (peak!)
- Fava beans
- English peas
- Snap peas
- Snow peas
- Green garlic
- Spring onions
- Ramps (if available)
- Morel mushrooms

### Leafy Greens
- Spinach
- Arugula
- Watercress
- Butter lettuce
- Little Gem lettuce
- Pea shoots
- Mâche

### Roots & Alliums
- Radishes (all varieties)
- Baby carrots
- Spring turnips
- New potatoes
- Leeks (last of season)

### Herbs (Lush)
- Mint
- Chives
- Parsley
- Cilantro
- Dill
- Tarragon

### Fruits
- Strawberries (California season!)
- Avocados
- Valencia oranges

### Other
- Fennel
- Green beans (early)

---

## Suggested Proteins

**Seafood:** Wild salmon (spring Chinook), halibut, petrale sole, spot prawns, Pacific cod
**Poultry:** Chicken
**Meat:** Spring lamb
**Plant-based:** Fava beans, fresh peas, eggs

---

## Sauce Ideas

1. **Mint Chimichurri** — Mint, parsley, garlic, red wine vinegar, olive oil
2. **Green Goddess** — Tarragon, chives, parsley, anchovy, Greek yogurt
3. **Preserved Lemon Gremolata** — Preserved lemon, parsley, garlic
4. **Pea and Ricotta** — Pureed peas, fresh ricotta, lemon, mint
5. **Asparagus Pistou** — Blanched asparagus, basil, garlic, Parmesan, olive oil

---

## Cooking Methods to Favor

- Quick blanching (green vegetables)
- Grilling
- Light sautés with butter and herbs
- Raw preparations (shaved vegetables)
- Steaming

---

## Theme Suggestions

- **French Spring:** Tarragon, butter, white wine, delicate preparations
- **Mediterranean Garden:** Fresh vegetables, olive oil, lemon, herbs
- **California Seasonal:** Salmon with asparagus, strawberry salads, farmers market focus
