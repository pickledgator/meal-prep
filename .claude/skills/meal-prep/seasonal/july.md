# July — Northern California Seasonal Guide

## Flavor Direction
**Peak summer abundance** — This is the height of summer produce. Tomatoes are finally perfect. Stone fruits are at their best. Corn is sweet. Keep cooking minimal — raw preparations, grilling, quick sautés. Let the ingredients speak.

---

## Peak Produce

### Tomatoes (Peak!)
- Heirloom varieties (Brandywine, Cherokee Purple, Green Zebra)
- Cherry tomatoes (Sungold, Sweet 100)
- Roma/plum tomatoes
- Beefsteak

### Stone Fruit (Peak!)
- Peaches (peak!)
- Nectarines (peak!)
- Plums (all varieties)
- Pluots
- Apricots (ending)
- Cherries (ending)

### Summer Vegetables
- Corn (peak!)
- Zucchini
- Summer squash
- Cucumbers
- Bell peppers (all colors)
- Eggplant
- Green beans
- Okra

### Berries
- Blueberries (peak)
- Blackberries
- Raspberries
- Boysenberries

### Melons
- Watermelon
- Cantaloupe
- Honeydew

### Herbs
- Basil (abundant!)
- Mint
- Cilantro
- Oregano
- Thyme

---

## Suggested Proteins

**Seafood:** Wild salmon, swordfish, ahi tuna, grilled shrimp, sardines
**Poultry:** Chicken (quick-cooking cuts)
**Meat:** Bavette, skirt steak, lamb chops
**Plant-based:** Shell beans, black-eyed peas, eggs

---

## Sauce Ideas

1. **Tomato-Basil Raw Sauce** — Peak tomatoes, garlic, basil, olive oil (no cooking)
2. **Peach-Jalapeño Salsa** — Diced peaches, jalapeño, red onion, lime, cilantro
3. **Corn Crema** — Grilled corn, Greek yogurt, lime, chili
4. **Zhoug** — Cilantro, jalapeño, garlic, cumin, cardamom, olive oil
5. **Romesco** — Roasted red peppers, almonds, tomatoes, smoked paprika

---

## Cooking Methods to Favor

- Grilling (everything)
- Raw preparations
- Quick sautés
- Chilled soups (gazpacho)
- Minimal heat

---

## Theme Suggestions

- **Peak Summer Italian:** Tomatoes, basil, olive oil, fresh mozzarella (if tolerated), grilled bread
- **Grilled California:** Everything over fire, fresh salsas, corn on the cob
- **Middle Eastern Summer:** Grilled meats, zhoug, fresh salads, yogurt sauces
