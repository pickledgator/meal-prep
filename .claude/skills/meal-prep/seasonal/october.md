# October — Northern California Seasonal Guide

## Flavor Direction
**Full autumn embrace** — Winter squash is king. Apples and pears are excellent. Root vegetables return in force. Warming spices (cumin, cinnamon, smoked paprika), deeper flavors, heartier preparations. Braising weather.

---

## Peak Produce

### Winter Squash (Peak!)
- Butternut squash
- Delicata squash
- Kabocha
- Acorn squash
- Spaghetti squash
- Sugar pumpkins (for cooking)

### Apples & Pears
- Apples (Honeycrisp, Fuji, Pink Lady, Granny Smith)
- Pears (Bartlett, Bosc, Comice)

### Cruciferous (Excellent)
- Brussels sprouts (peak)
- Broccoli
- Cauliflower
- Cabbage
- Kale
- Collard greens

### Roots
- Sweet potatoes
- Carrots
- Beets
- Parsnips
- Turnips
- Celery root

### Other Fall Produce
- Pomegranates (peak)
- Persimmons (Fuyu, Hachiya)
- Figs (ending)
- Fennel
- Mushrooms (chanterelles, porcini)

### Herbs
- Sage
- Rosemary
- Thyme
- Parsley

---

## Suggested Proteins

**Seafood:** Pacific cod, black cod, mussels, clams, salmon (fall run)
**Poultry:** Chicken (thighs for braising), duck, turkey
**Meat:** Lamb shoulder, beef (chuck, short ribs)
**Plant-based:** Lentils, white beans, chickpeas

---

## Sauce Ideas

1. **Pomegranate-Walnut (Fesenjan-inspired)** — Pomegranate molasses, walnuts, warm spices
2. **Sage-Apple Butter** — Browned butter, sage, diced apple
3. **Maple-Mustard** — Maple syrup, Dijon, apple cider vinegar
4. **Tahini-Squash** — Roasted squash blended with tahini, lemon, garlic
5. **Balsamic Reduction** — Thick balsamic glaze with rosemary

---

## Cooking Methods to Favor

- Roasting (squash, roots, Brussels sprouts)
- Braising (tough cuts, beans)
- Pan-searing with pan sauces
- Warm composed salads
- Soups and stews

---

## Theme Suggestions

- **Persian-Inspired Fall:** Pomegranate, walnuts, warm spices, rice
- **Italian Autumn:** Squash risotto-style preparations, sage, beans
- **Harvest Comfort:** Roasted vegetables, warming proteins, apple accents
