# March — Northern California Seasonal Guide

## Flavor Direction
**Spring awakening** — The true start of spring produce. Citrus is fading but still available; asparagus, artichokes, and tender greens take center stage. Lighter cooking methods, brighter flavors, celebrate the freshness of new growth.

---

## Peak Produce

### Spring Stars
- Asparagus (peak begins!)
- Artichokes (peak season starts)
- Fava beans
- English peas (late March)
- Green garlic
- Spring onions
- Ramps (limited)

### Citrus (Winding Down)
- Meyer lemons (last of season)
- Blood oranges (ending)
- Cara Cara oranges
- Grapefruit
- Valencia oranges (beginning)

### Leafy Greens (Excellent)
- Spinach (peak)
- Arugula
- Watercress
- Butter lettuce
- Spring mix
- Nettles

### Brassicas
- Broccoli
- Broccolini
- Cauliflower
- Kale
- Cabbage

### Roots (Transitioning)
- Carrots
- Beets
- Radishes (French breakfast, Easter egg)
- Turnips (small, spring)

### Other
- Fennel
- Mushrooms (morels begin late March!)
- Avocados
- Strawberries (very early, from Watsonville)

---

## Suggested Proteins

**Seafood:** Halibut (excellent), petrale sole, Pacific cod, Dungeness crab (ending), salmon (spring run beginning)
**Poultry:** Chicken
**Meat:** Lamb (spring lamb is traditional)
**Plant-based:** Fava beans, eggs, fresh legumes

---

## Sauce Ideas

1. **Lemon-Caper Butter** — Brown butter, capers, lemon juice, parsley
2. **Mint-Pea Pesto** — Fresh peas, mint, Parmesan, olive oil, lemon
3. **Artichoke-Lemon Aioli** — Roasted artichoke hearts blended with aioli base
4. **Salsa Verde (Spring)** — Heavy on parsley, mint, chives, lighter on anchovy
5. **Sherry-Shallot Vinaigrette** — Sherry vinegar, shallot, Dijon, olive oil

---

## Cooking Methods to Favor

- Blanching and shocking (asparagus, peas, favas)
- Quick sautés
- Grilling (vegetables and proteins)
- Raw preparations (shaved asparagus, pea shoots)
- Light pan sauces

---

## Theme Suggestions

- **Spring Mediterranean:** Artichokes, fava beans, lamb, lemon, fresh herbs
- **California Fresh:** Light grain bowls, lots of green vegetables, citrus dressings
- **Italian Primavera:** Celebrate spring vegetables with pasta, risotto, simple preparations
