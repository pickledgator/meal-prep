# September — Northern California Seasonal Guide

## Flavor Direction
**Harvest transition** — Summer and fall overlap beautifully. Tomatoes are still good, but apples, pears, and winter squash are arriving. Peppers remain excellent. The mood shifts toward warmer spices and heartier preparations while still celebrating summer's end.

---

## Peak Produce

### Late Summer (Still Good)
- Tomatoes (winding down)
- Peppers (all varieties)
- Eggplant
- Corn (late)
- Figs (peak!)

### Fall Arrivals
- Winter squash (butternut, delicata, acorn)
- Apples (Gravenstein, Gala, Fuji)
- Pears (Bartlett, Bosc)
- Grapes
- Pomegranates (beginning)
- Persimmons (beginning)
- Quince

### Cruciferous (Returning)
- Broccoli
- Cauliflower
- Brussels sprouts (beginning)
- Cabbage
- Kale (returns strong)

### Roots
- Carrots
- Beets
- Sweet potatoes
- Turnips
- Parsnips

### Herbs
- Sage
- Rosemary
- Thyme
- Oregano
- Parsley

---

## Suggested Proteins

**Seafood:** Pacific cod, halibut, mussels, clams (cooler weather = shellfish)
**Poultry:** Chicken (roasting season begins), duck
**Meat:** Lamb, beef (braising cuts)
**Plant-based:** Cannellini beans, lentils, chickpeas

---

## Sauce Ideas

1. **Apple Cider Gastrique** — Apple cider reduced with cider vinegar, touch of maple
2. **Sage Brown Butter** — Browned butter with crispy sage leaves
3. **Harissa-Yogurt** — Harissa paste, Greek yogurt, lemon
4. **Fig-Walnut Relish** — Chopped figs, toasted walnuts, honey, rosemary
5. **Romesco** — Roasted peppers (use them up!), almonds, smoked paprika

---

## Cooking Methods to Favor

- Roasting (squash, roots, proteins)
- Pan-searing with pan sauces
- Light braises
- Grilling (last of the season)
- Warm grain bowls

---

## Theme Suggestions

- **Harvest Mediterranean:** Squash, peppers, olive oil, warm spices
- **California Autumn:** Apples, figs, pork-free preparations with harvest vegetables
- **Transitional Comfort:** Bridge summer and fall with warm salads and roasted vegetables
