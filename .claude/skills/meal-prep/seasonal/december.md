# December — Northern California Seasonal Guide

## Flavor Direction
**Peak winter, peak citrus** — Citrus season is in full swing. Root vegetables and brassicas are excellent. The holidays call for both celebratory dishes and simple weeknight comfort. Balance festive flavors with everyday practicality.

---

## Suggested Proteins

**Seafood:** Dungeness crab (season opens!), Pacific cod, halibut, mussels, clams, oysters
**Poultry:** Chicken, duck
**Meat:** Lamb, beef (celebratory and braising cuts)
**Plant-based:** White beans, lentils, chickpeas

---

## Sauce Ideas

1. **Citrus Vinaigrette** — Orange or grapefruit juice, shallot, Dijon, olive oil
2. **Tahini-Meyer Lemon** — Tahini, Meyer lemon juice, garlic, warm water
3. **Pomegranate-Herb** — Pomegranate molasses, fresh herbs, olive oil
4. **Brown Butter-Citrus** — Browned butter, orange zest, thyme
5. **Salsa Verde** — Parsley, capers, anchovy, lemon, olive oil

---

## Cooking Methods to Favor

- Roasting (vegetables, proteins)
- Braising
- Pan-searing with citrus pan sauces
- Warm salads
- One-pot meals

---

## Theme Suggestions

- **Citrus Celebration:** Make citrus the star — vinaigrettes, supremes, zest everywhere
- **Mediterranean Winter:** Bright flavors with olive oil, lemon, herbs
- **Holiday Comfort:** Elegant but not fussy, make-ahead friendly
- **Dungeness Season:** Celebrate local crab simply prepared
