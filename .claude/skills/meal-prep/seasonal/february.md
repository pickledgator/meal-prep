# February — Northern California Seasonal Guide

## Flavor Direction
**Late winter warmth with hints of spring** — Still in the heart of citrus season, but early spring vegetables begin to appear. Balance comforting dishes with bright, acidic notes. Look for the first asparagus and spring onions toward month's end.

---

## Peak Produce

### Citrus (Still Peak)
- Blood oranges (peak!)
- Meyer lemons
- Cara Cara oranges
- Grapefruit
- Kumquats
- Mandarins

### Brassicas
- Broccoli & broccolini
- Brussels sprouts (late)
- Cabbage (all varieties)
- Cauliflower
- Kale (all varieties)
- Collard greens

### Roots & Alliums
- Beets
- Carrots
- Celery root
- Parsnips
- Turnips
- Leeks
- Spring onions (late February)

### Squash (End of Season)
- Butternut (use up remaining)
- Delicata
- Kabocha

### Leafy Greens
- Spinach
- Chard
- Arugula
- Escarole
- Radicchio
- Frisée

### Early Spring Arrivals (late month)
- Asparagus (first of season)
- Nettles
- Green garlic
- Fava beans (very early)

### Other
- Fennel
- Mushrooms
- Avocados (Hass beginning)

---

## Suggested Proteins

**Seafood:** Pacific halibut, black cod, mussels, clams, sardines
**Poultry:** Chicken, duck
**Meat:** Lamb (great with early spring vegetables), beef
**Plant-based:** White beans, lentils, chickpeas, eggs

---

## Sauce Ideas

1. **Blood Orange Gastrique** — Blood orange juice reduced with vinegar, touch of honey
2. **Green Goddess** — Herbs, anchovy, Greek yogurt, lemon, garlic
3. **Shallot-Herb Vinaigrette** — Minced shallot, Dijon, herbs, red wine vinegar
4. **Chermoula** — Cilantro, parsley, cumin, paprika, preserved lemon, olive oil
5. **Bagna Cauda** — Warm anchovy-garlic olive oil (for dipping vegetables)

---

## Cooking Methods to Favor

- Roasting (still great for roots and brassicas)
- Pan-searing with citrus pan sauces
- Light braises
- Raw citrus salads
- Warm composed salads

---

## Theme Suggestions

- **Transitional Mediterranean:** Bridge winter and spring with citrus and early greens
- **North African Inspired:** Chermoula, preserved lemons, chickpeas, warming spices
- **French Bistro:** Leeks vinaigrette, roasted chicken, simple preparations
